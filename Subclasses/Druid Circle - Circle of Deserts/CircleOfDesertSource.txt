## Druid Circle

### Circle of the Desert
The druids of this circle hail from some of the driest and most
unforgiving environments imaginable. Yet, even in these deadly
realms, life can thrive, and these druids seek to emulate the
tenacity of the desert's inhabitants while also channeling its
brutality against nature's enemies. To this end, these druids
can shapeshift into some of the desert's most deadly creatures
while also calling upon the scorching sand and heat of their
homeland to rapidly wither away their foes.

#### Desert Spells
*2nd-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

Your mystical connection to the desert infuses you with the
ability to cast certain spells. Once you gain access to one of
these spells, you always have it prepared, and it doesn't count
against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you
gain access to a spell that doesn't appear on the Druid spell
list, the spell is nonetheless a Druid spell for you. (New spells and revised spells can be found at end of document.)

##### Circle of the Desert Spells

| Druid Level | Spells |
|:-----------:|:------:|
| 2nd | *Warding Light, Sand Blast* |
| 3rd | *Dust Devil, Ray of Enfeeblement* |
| 5th | *Wall of Sand, Dazzling Light* |
| 7th | *Decrepify, Sickening Radiance* |
| 9th | *Ash Storm, Transmute Rock** |

**This spell can target sand as well as rock*

#### Desert Forms
*2nd-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You can use your Wild Shape feature to transform into unique
desert animal forms granted by this subclass. When using your
Wild Shape this way, you can use it as a Bonus Action. Detailed
descriptions of these forms can be found in the Desert Wild
Shapes section.

When shapeshifting into one of these forms, you can choose to
be Small or Medium. Starting at 10th level, you can also choose
to be Large.

While in these forms, you can perform the Somatic and Verbal
components of Druid spells from your Desert Spells list and
ignore their Material component requirements.

You begin knowing three of these forms: Camel, Canid, and
Venomous Snake. Additional forms become available at levels 4
and 8.

#### Arid Adaptations
*2nd-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You gain Resistance to Fire damage. If you already have
Resistance to Fire damage from another source, you instead have
Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects that
deal Fire damage to you. You also require only half as much
water each day to avoid the effects of dehydration, and your
movement is unaffected by difficult terrain composed of sand.


Additionally, you have Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) and
Intelligence (Nature) checks relating to desert environments
and the creatures that inhabit them. You can also speak with
animals that dwell in desert environments as though you were
under the effects of the *Speak with Animals* spell, and you
have Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made while
interacting with such creatures.

#### Primal Weapons
*6th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

When you merge into one of your Wild Shape forms while wielding
a magical weapon, your attacks in that form count as magical
for the purpose of overcoming Resistance and Immunity to
nonmagical attacks and damage.

#### Heat of the Desert
*6th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You can call forth the punishing heat of your homeland. As an Action, you conjure a 20-foot-radius Sphere of withering heat centered on a point you can see within 30 feet of yourself.

Whenever a creature of your choice within the area makes a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution-based D20 Test, it takes 1d6 Fire damage. This damage is dealt after the result of the test is determined. If the creature fails the test, it takes 2d6 Fire damage instead.

As a Bonus Action, you can move the Sphere up to 20 feet. The Sphere lasts for 1 minute, or until you dismiss it (no action required).

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a spell slot of 3rd level or higher to use it again.

\columnbreak

#### Avatar of the Desert
*10th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

When you use your Wild Shape feature to transform into one of
the forms granted by this subclass, you can choose one of the
following enhancements:

- **Sand Form.** Your form is composed of shifting sand. You along with any equipment you are wearing or carrying can move through any space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing ignore, and you can move through enemies without having your movement speed slowed, you ignore difficult terrain case by any ground, you can't be grappled or restrained. Your attacks also gain additional reach of 10 feet.

- **Mirage Form.** Your form takes on a shimmering, illusory
quality. While in this form, you are under the effects of
the *Mirror Image* spell.

- **Mummy Form.** Your form becomes horrifically desiccated,
infused with necrotic energy. When you deal none bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage with a Circle of the Desert Spells, your Heat of the Desert feature, or your wilde Shape, you deal necrotic damage instead. If that creature
then has a number hit points remaining lower then your druid level, it is reduced to 0
hit points and turns to dust.

#### Withering Heat
*14th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You can cast *Withering Heat** without expending a spell slot,
and it doesn't require Concentration for you. It counts as a
Druid spell for you, and you can cast it while in one of your
Desert Forms as though it were one of your Desert Spells.

Once you cast the spell this way, you can't do so again until
you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a spell slot of 7th
level or higher.

### New Spells

#### Ash Storm
*5th-level Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of soot)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a wave of scalding ash in a 60-foot-long, 15-foot-wide line originating from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d10 Fire damage, is pushed 15 feet away from you, and is Blinded until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no other effects.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 5th.

#### Dazzling Light
*3rd-level Evocation (Cleric, Bard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:**   Instantaneous

You unleash a burst of radiant light at a point you choose within range. Each creature in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 2d10 Radiant damage and become Blinded until the end of your next turn. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw takes half as much damage and isn’t Blinded.
 
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 3rd.

#### Decrepify
*4th-level Necromancy (Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of shriveled weeds)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You attempt to drain the vitality of a creature within range, leaving it feeble and vulnerable. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have its Speed halved and its Strength and Dexterity scores reduced to 1.

If you cast it using an 8th-level spell slot, the spell lasts until dispelled and no longer requires Concentration; the affected creature can only attempt a new saving throw after completing a Long Rest.

#### Dust Devil (Revised)
*2nd-level Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dust)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A 5-foot-diameter sphere of whirling dust appears in an unoccupied space of your choice within range and lasts for the duration. The sphere's space is Heavily Obscured and Difficult Terrain, and creatures can move through it.

When the sphere first appears and as a Magic action on your following turns, you can move the sphere up to 30 feet in a straight line. If the sphere moves into a creature's space, that creature must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 1d10 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed 10 feet away. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

If the sphere moves over sand, dust, loose dirt, or small gravel, it sucks up the material and forms a 10-foot-radius cloud of debris around itself that lasts until the start of your next turn. The cloud Heavily Obscures its area.

A creature that ends its turn within 5 feet of the sphere must also make the saving throw described above.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.

> ##### New Condition: Burning
> The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid.
> * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
> * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
> * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

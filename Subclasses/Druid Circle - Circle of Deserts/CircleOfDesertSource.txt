## Druid Circle

### Circle of the Desert
The druids of this circle hail from some of the driest and most
unforgiving environments imaginable. Yet, even in these deadly
realms, life can thrive, and these druids seek to emulate the
tenacity of the desert's inhabitants while also channeling its
brutality against nature's enemies. To this end, these druids
can shapeshift into some of the desert's most deadly creatures
while also calling upon the scorching sand and heat of their
homeland to rapidly wither away their foes.

#### Desert Spells
*2nd-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

Your mystical connection to the desert infuses you with the
ability to cast certain spells. Once you gain access to one of
these spells, you always have it prepared, and it doesn't count
against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you
gain access to a spell that doesn't appear on the Druid spell
list, the spell is nonetheless a Druid spell for you.

##### Circle of the Desert Spells

| Druid Level | Spells |
|:-----------:|:------:|
| 2nd | *Warding Light, Sand Attack* |
| 3rd | *Dust Devil, Ray of Enfeeblement* |
| 5th | *Wall of Sand, Dazzling Light* |
| 7th | *Decrepify, Sickening Radiance* |
| 9th | *Ash Storm, Transmute Rock** |

**This spell can target sand as well as rock*

#### Desert Forms
*2nd-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You can use your Wild Shape feature to transform into unique
desert animal forms granted by this subclass. When using your
Wild Shape this way, you can use it as a Bonus Action. Detailed
descriptions of these forms can be found in the Desert Wild
Shapes section.

When shapeshifting into one of these forms, you can choose to
be Small or Medium. Starting at 10th level, you can also choose
to be Large.

While in these forms, you can perform the Somatic and Verbal
components of Druid spells from your Desert Spells list and
ignore their Material component requirements.

You begin knowing three of these forms: Camel, Canid, and
Venomous Snake. Additional forms become available at levels 4
and 8.

##### Desert Wild Shape Options

| Druid Level | Wild Shape Option |
|:-----------:|:-----------------:|
| 2nd | Camel, Canid (Jackal or Coyote), Venomous Snake |
| 4th | Roadrunner, Scorpion, Vulture |
| 8th | Tortoise, Wurm |

#### Arid Adaptations
*2nd-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You gain Resistance to Fire damage. If you already have
Resistance to Fire damage from another source, you instead have
Advantage on Constitution saving throws against effects that
deal Fire damage to you. You also require only half as much
water each day to avoid the effects of dehydration, and your
movement is unaffected by difficult terrain composed of sand.

Additionally, you have Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) and
Intelligence (Nature) checks relating to desert environments
and the creatures that inhabit them. You can also speak with
animals that dwell in desert environments as though you were
under the effects of the *Speak with Animals* spell, and you
have Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made while
interacting with such creatures.

#### Primal Weapons
*6th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

When you merge into one of your Wild Shape forms while wielding
a magical weapon, your attacks in that form count as magical
for the purpose of overcoming Resistance and Immunity to
nonmagical attacks and damage.

#### Heat of the Desert
*6th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You can call forth the punishing heat of your homeland. As an
Action, you conjure a 20-foot-radius Sphere of withering heat
centered on a point you can see within 30 feet of yourself.

Whenever a creature of your choice within the area makes a
Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution-based D20 Test, it takes
X Fire damage. This damage is dealt after the result of the
test is determined. If the creature fails the test, it takes Y
Fire damage instead.

As a Bonus Action, you can move the Sphere up to 20 feet. The
Sphere lasts for 1 minute, or until you dismiss it (no action
required).

\columnbreak

#### Avatar of the Desert
*10th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

When you use your Wild Shape feature to transform into one of
the forms granted by this subclass, you can choose one of the
following enhancements:

- **Sand Form.** Your form is composed of shifting sand. As a
Bonus Action, you can transform into a cloud of sand and fly
to a point you can see within 30 feet. While flying this way,
you are under the effects of the *Gaseous Form* spell, which
ends when you reach your destination or are prevented from
doing so for any reason. Each creature of your choice that
you pass through while in this state takes X Slashing damage.

- **Mirage Form.** Your form takes on a shimmering, illusory
quality. While in this form, you are under the effects of
the *Mirror Image* spell.

- **Mummy Form.** Your form becomes horrifically desiccated,
infused with necrotic energy. Once per turn, when you hit a
creature with an attack, you can force it to make a
Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a
failed save, it takes X Necrotic damage. If that creature
then has Y hit points or fewer remaining, it is reduced to 0
hit points and turns to dust.

#### Withering Heat
*14th-Level Circle of the Desert Feature*

You can cast *Withering Heat** without expending a spell slot,
and it doesn't require Concentration for you. It counts as a
Druid spell for you, and you can cast it while in one of your
Desert Forms as though it were one of your Desert Spells.

Once you cast the spell this way, you can't do so again until
you finish a Long Rest, unless you expend a spell slot of 7th
level or higher.

#### Frost Nova
*2nd-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot radius)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of water <br> or piece of ice)
- **Duration:**   Instantaneous
- **Classes:**  Sorcerer, Wizard
___

You cause a ring of ice and frost to burst out <br> from you to a radius of up to a 15-feet. Each <br> creature other than you in that area must make <br> a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a <br> creature takes 2d10 cold damage, and until the <br> end of your next turn, its speed is reduced by <br> 20 feet, and it has disadvantage on weapon <br> attack rolls. On a successful save, a creature <br> takes half as much damage and suffers no <br> other effects.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell <br> using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, both the <br> damage increases by 1d10, and the maximum possible <br> radius increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 3rd.

___

A wave of cold 30 feet long and 15 feet wide emanates from you in a direction you choose. The ground in the area is covered with ice, turning it into difficult terrain for the duration. Any surface of a body of water or a liquid that is principally water-based within the area freezes to a depth of 6 inches for the duration. Creatures may move across the frozen surfaces as if they are difficult terrain. 

**Destroying The Ice.** A 5-foot square area of ice can be destroyed by attacking it (AC equal to your spell save; HP 20; vulnerability to fire, thunder bludgeoning damage; immunity to cold, poison, and psychic damage). 

**Slipping On The Ice.** When the ice appears, each creature standing in its area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. A creature that enters the area or ends its turn there must also succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. 

**Being Caught In The Water.** If a creature is swimming in an area where ice appears, it must make a Strength saving throw instead or become restrained by ice for the duration. A creature restrained by the ice or another creature that can reach it can also use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC. On a success, the target is freed. The target is also freed if any ice where at its location is destroyed. On a successful save, the creature can move below or above the now frozen surface.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the length of the line increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 2nd.

#### Ice Storm
*4th-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 300 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dust and a few <br> drops of water)
- **Duration:**  1 Round
- **Classes:**  Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
___

A barrage of rock-hard ice plummets from the sky, forming a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on a point within range. The area becomes difficult terrain until the end of your next turn, and any  creature within it has disadvantage on ranged attack rolls.

When the ice storm initially appears or if a creature is inside its area at the end of your next turn, the creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 3d4 bludgeoning damage and 3d4 cold damage. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, both the bludgeoning and cold damage increase by 1d4 for each slot level above 4th.

#### Icy Tomb
*5th-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when you or a creature you can see within range would take damage or make a death saving throw.
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:**   Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Classes:**  Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

Ice encases one willing Medium or smaller creature you can see within range. It is trapped inside a Medium-sized ice block object that occupies its space for the duration of the spell or until the ice is reduced to 0 hit points. The ice has an AC equal to your spell save; HP 50; vulnerability to fire, thunder bludgeoning damage; immunity to cold, poison, and psychic damage). A creature trapped inside the ice is blinded, restrained, incapacitated, has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the ice, and becomes stable if it is at 0 hit points.

**Casting It As An Action.** You can choose to cast this spell as an action on your turn, choosing any creature within range. If the creature is not willing, it may make a Strength saving throw; on success, the spell ends and has no effect on the creature.

**Damaging The Ice From Within.** A creature inside the ice can choose to deal bludgeoning damage to the ice equal to 1d6 + its Strength modifier at the end of each of its turns.

**Casting It On Yourself.** If you cast the spell on yourself, it does not require Concentration, and you can choose to end the spell at the start of your turn. 

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the ice block gains 10 additional Hit Points for each slot level above 5th.

#### Winter's Kiss
*3rd-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 bonus action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:**  Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Classes:**  Sorcerer, Wizard

A burst of cold energy emanates from you in a 40-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a Strength saving throw. 

On a failed save, a creature takes 4d6 cold damage and is restrained by ice until the spell ends. At the end of each of its turns, while restrained this way, the target takes 2d6 cold damage. It or another creature within reach of it can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save, shattering the ice on a success. The ice can also be attacked and destroyed (AC equal to your spell save; HP 20; vulnerability to fire, thunder bludgeoning damage; immunity to cold, poison, and psychic damage). On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't restrained.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the initial damage and the damage at the end of the restrained target's turn are increased by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

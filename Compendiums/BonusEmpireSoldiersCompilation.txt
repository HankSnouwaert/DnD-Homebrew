# Bonus Empire Soldiers Compilation

## Hans Von Löwenhacke
*Grizzled General & Master Tactician of the Empire*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

No other commander is able to orchestrate the armies of man with militaristic efficiency as General Von Löwenhacke. Even in his youth, Hans’s aptitude for leadership was clear when he joined the ranks of the imperial forces of his homeland. His mind could fabricate the most complex strategies for the conflicts his soldiers would face, yet also adapt intricate tactics when unexpected factors arose amid the turbulence of war. His voice is infused with such thunderous bravado that any underling receiving an order from that reverberating roar will carry out the instruction without a hint of hesitation. 

Von Löwenhacke has become a legend after the countless victories he has accumulated against seemingly superior forces. Beyond his ability to coordinate his comrades, Hans is an exquisite fighter; with his massive warhammer he is able to perform all varieties of precision maneuvers, sending foes flying into his allies’ gleaming weapons, delivering blows that leave enemies bewildered, or throwing such an assailant off balance, leading to a misstep that exposes them to further assaults from the guileful leader and the rest of his devoted warriors.

### Hans as a Boss Battle
Hans works best when paired with his minions, who are made to work in tandem with him. Each one he has increases his CR by 2.

### Von Löwenhacke Tactics
Von Löwenhacke is flexible in his tactics and how he approaches an opponent. Each of his minions is used in the following ways:

* **Archers** are used as damage dealers. They help keep opponents from removing stacks of Assessment, so they should prioritize those targets.

* **Swordsmen** are used to help keep Von Löwenhacke alive by remaining within 5 feet of him.

* **Pikemen** are used to lock down targets, especially in combination with Von Löwenhacke’s Push.

#### Versatile Hammer 
This attack can be used in a variety of ways, mostly attacking targets that have low saving throws against the chosen effect. Here are a few combos to consider when focusing on a single target:

* **Bash and Push** — Best when a Pikeman is lined up to lock down a highly mobile target (such as a rogue or monk).

\columnbreak

* **Expose and Bash** — Good if the target has low Dexterity and you want to prevent them from escaping, especially if an Archer is Quickened.

* **Expose and Push** — Useful if the Hans is low on health but has a large number of minions ready to attack.

#### Tactical  Actions
##### Bolster 
Best used on targets that are close to death to keep them alive. Otherwise, use it on Archers so they can multiply pressure on targets that have high levels of Assessment.

##### Coordinated Strike
Can be used both to reposition allies and to grant them extra attacks. Common uses include:
* Moving a Swordsman back within 5 feet of Von Löwenhacke.
* Moving a Pikeman into position to impale a target, or to drag an already impaled target toward or away from allies.
* Having an Archer retreat from melee, or keeping as many targets as possible from removing stacks of Assessment.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Dazed 
> A Dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Quickened
> A Quickened creature gains an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack or unarmed strike only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action. The *Haste* spell applies the Quickened condition among its other effects, and a creature cannot benefit from the Quickened condition from multiple sources at once.

## Irregular Militia
*Ragtag Soldiers Operating Outside Usual Military Structures*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

In empires ravaged by all manner of marauding armies that perpetually attempt to tear down and consume all that they have built, the forces that defend such provinces must be laden with as many able bodies as possible to drive off these rapacious hordes. In dire times, many of these troops are not part of any formal military but drafted or willing conscripts gathered from the region, local residents finding what weapons they can to protect their homes and loved ones against the oncoming evil. Others are bandits, mercenaries, or other outlaws pressed into service through threat or the promise of coin, whether from government coffers or by picking clean the corpses of their enemies for payment. Most informal footsoldiers are commonly known as Irregulars. 

Many nobles and commanders are eager to take such lawless men into their ranks, as it allows them to keep these unruly lots under some semblance of control. Callous commanders also see it as a convenient opportunity to remove troublemakers from their lands, for it is not uncommon to assign such willful fighters to the most dangerous tasks so that military funds are not wasted on these uncouth soldiers.

### Vengeful Militia

Some warriors do not join a cause out of potent avarice for silver or gold, nor from a higher calling to protect the weak or maintain law in the land. Instead, they are driven by a flame kindled by vengeance. Many cities and hamlets have been scoured from the land by the clawed limbs of furious beasts or the axes and swords of merciless scum. Yet from the rubble of such ruins, those few who have persisted through tragedy rise transformed; all that they once were left broken as wreckage beneath their feet, with only their hate left to sustain what remains of them. 

These vengeful militiamen travel far and wide in small bands, seeking to eradicate any person or creature even remotely connected to those responsible for destroying their homes. Once they have caught wind of its target, fury begins to boil in their blood as they rush forward, unleashing a hail of bullets before hacking their foes apart with decisively brutal strikes.

### Veteran Militia
Many freelancers set out across the land to gather a fortune that no commoner could hope to acquire, aiming to amass such a stockpile through the application of steel and black powder. Such undertakings rarely result in the mountains of wealth one would hope for; these endeavors are usually halted by a well-placed swipe of a behemoth’s clawed appendage, being surrounded by a gaggle of spiteful goblins with jagged implements, or some other deadly encounter.

> ##### New Condition: Fury  
> The creature gains levels of Fury that cause its attacks to deal increasing amounts of damage with each level. 
> 
> **Gaining Fury.** Unless an ability specifies otherwise, a creature can gain up to 6 levels of Fury and only during combat. <Br>
> &emsp;**Extra Damage.** The first time each turn that the creature hits a target with an attack, it deals an additional 1d4 damage for each level of Fury it has.<br>
> &emsp;**Losing Fury.** All levels of Fury are lost if a creature goes 1 minute without gaining any levels of Fury. Additionally, a creature loses all levels of Fury if it becomes Charmed or Frightened, or is affected by the *Calm Emotions* spell. It also can't gain levels Fury while under these conditions.

However, there are some rapscallions who possess true grit, and when overwhelming danger bears down, they become filled with a vigor that pushes the condottiere beyond their limits, thrusting blades with such force as to rend opponents in twain, sidestepping all manner of hazards sent to dispatch them, or enduring thrashings that would cripple or outright slay men of lesser bravado. 

\columnbreak

### Irregular Militia Tactics

Militiamen continue advancing to engage a target in melee after firing their pistol, unless hazards or obstacles block their path. In which case, they seek cover and continue firing from afar. 

#### Veteran Tactics
When veterans become bloodied, they switch tactics: they use Adrenaline Rush to disengage to a safe distance, then resume using their pistol.

## Outriders
*Veteran Marksmen Armed With The Finest Blackpowder Weaponry*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Outriders are imposing men of high military standing who have climbed their way from novitiate rank-and-file soldiers to commanders and drill sergeants. They are accomplished in both horsemanship and firearms, with a knack for refining young recruits into an efficient fighting force. As such, many noble families pay a hefty sum to these masters so that their vivacious sons may acquire exemplary military training and so that their youthful fire is tempered before it drives them to an early end.

Outriders therefore acquire the finest armor and weapons with which to swathe themselves. They also favor well-coiffed hair and carefully groomed mustaches to distinguish themselves from lower-ranking officers; such finery only heightens the aura of command that a veteran outrider exudes.

Many times outriders lead groups of pistoleers to keep them from acting foolishly, getting themselves killed, or jeopardizing the mission. At other times an entire company of these weathered virtuosos rides together to show the younger lads what a true cavalry unit can accomplish. Outriders spare no expense on the firearms they carry, spending a small fortune on each weapon. Many favor repeater guns capable of loosing a storm of metal within seconds; others carry small hand-mortars with which they lob grenades that, upon landing, leave charred and bloody holes in enemy formations.

### Outrider Bordermen
At the edges of the greatest empires lie lands that have never been refined by the will of civilization or cleansed of the unknown. All manner of danger skulks in the undergrowth and rocky crevasses. The gentility of aristocracy and the proper edicts of the bustling metropolises lose their significance in the breath of such untamed domains. In these realms, those who know how to move through strenuous terrain and who are acquainted with the dangers it conceals are held in the highest esteem.

Outriders sent to these barbaric corners must quickly adapt or lose both their high standing and their lives. Those who not only survive but thrive become hardy souls, bred apart from their polished kin and able to tame the land with steel and powder. Such troopers gallop across the most challenging topography atop robust mounts without slowing, weaving past every danger while their adversaries slash and stab vainly at empty air. All the while the outriders load their weapons with ease, giving the cretins who pursue them a proper parting gift before riding on into the wilderness.

### Onyx Outriders
When the magi who have mastered the macabre winds of death require champions capable of eradicating the most vile and resilient warriors and beasts, they hire outriders, paying them such vast sums of gems and metal as they could never hope to earn by any other means, and offering weapons infused with power beyond anything science could produce.

Even so, the majority of the most talented marksmen turn away from these bounties. Rumors and stories of service to such grim wizards are told throughout the realms: soldiers who pledge themselves to this duty become mirthless and cold, for joy seems to refuse any part in such a venture. The few who are foolish, or already cold-blooded enough to accept, soon find themselves swathed in macabre shells of ebon steel crowned with great skull helms and given all manner of death-laced contraptions to aid them in their duty, along with dark steeds to enact their masters’ bidding.

As the riders close on their victims, they draw ghastly firearms. Some carry repeaters that loose rounds gleaming with violet flame; the bullets cackle horribly as they rush through the air. When these ghoulish projectiles find their mark, the wounds they leave blacken, and a terror seeps into the host, growing until it drowns every other thought. Others carry grenades of amethyst that burst with howls capable of shattering the courage of all who hear them.

Yet what has made these men infamous is the fate of those who withstand the psychic punishment and do not die of pure fright. For such outriders then charge forward with their blades. With the momentum of the assault, the hexed weapons plunge deep into the bewildered opponent’s flesh, slicing into the very soul. 

\columnbreak

The life-force is hacked away and siphoned into the outrider, imparting a vigor that allows him to endure every manner of punishment that would fell a normal mortal.


#### Onyx Outrider Tactics
Onyx outriders charge into melee against frightened targets and remain engaged to impose disadvantage on the target’s saving throws, helping maintain the frightened condition. When multiple onyx outriders are present, one can enter melee to impose disadvantage on saving throws triggered by the rest of the group.

## Pistoleers
*Gunslinging Sharpshooters Armed With a Brace of Pistols*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Armed with a bandolier of pistols across their chest or waist, pistoleers must be exceptionally swift to make the most of such armaments. These warriors fire, discard, and draw another firearm in rapid succession, repeating the process without error. Because pistols lack the range of many other firearms, they are forced to fight closer to the heat of combat rather than safer distances that other marksmen enjoy. Only the boldest and most reckless embrace this strategy, and most are hot-headed fighters eager to rush into danger at any opportunity to display their deadly skill.

### Noble Riders
Many young nobles are too inexperienced or lack the pedigree required for acceptance into the knightly orders scattered throughout the kingdoms. When these highborn warriors cannot join such prestigious ranks, they use their family fortunes to purchase pistols, armor, and the finest stallions, then band together with like-minded rabble-rousers to form regiments known as Noble Riders. These fighters are well trained in both marksmanship and horsemanship. As fast cavalry, they race past enemy flanks, pouring fire into the foe as they pass and striking from every angle.

### Quick Draws
The most grizzled and battle-worn pistoleers are known as Quick Draws. These grim figures travel alone or in small groups, usually as mercenaries or guns-for-hire. They earn their title from the blistering speed with which they can empty their sidearms, as these veterans can unleash a volley of pistol fire as fast as a bolt of lightning. In the blink of an opponent’s eye, multiple rounds have already been fired with such accuracy that targets are disarmed, brought to their knees, or left dead. Many have become local folk heroes, with bards singing of wandering gunmen who ride into towns plagued by bandits or corrupt officials and deliver justice with iron and powder.

## Riflemen
*Marksmen Able To Snipe Foes From Afar With Long Rifles*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

Among the most advanced and deadly handheld weapons ever developed, long rifles can deliver skull-shattering shots capable of inflicting lethal wounds at astonishing distances. Each pull of the trigger ends a life, and many have heard the crack of thunder from a distant hillside only to find a limb shattered into blood and gore. The firearms themselves feature long, thin barrels that make reloading difficult; as a result, the marksmen chosen to carry these lethal tools train diligently to load their weapons with maximum efficiency. 

### The Jaegerkorps

Jaegerkorps are elite strike forces designed to eliminate back-line commanders in the heat of combat. They can strike a songbird from two hundred yards away and slip through entire regiments of hostile forces without anyone realizing their doom is approaching. Finding the best vantage point, these agents hunker down and carefully scan the battlefield to determine where their fire will most destabilize the opposing force. At a frantic rate, men are cut down, and commanders and sergeants drop with gaping chunks of their heads removed in the blink of an eye. Chaos floods through the ranks of the fallen leaders’ subordinates, who cannot discern where the death originated, hearing only the booming roar of gunfire ringing out like mocking laughter.

#### Jaegerkorps Rifleman Tactics

The rifleman first tries to hide from their opponent (if they are not already hidden). They then find a secure vantage point, using black powder discipline to fire each turn.

### Mage Striker Rifleman

Few forces can tip the scales of battle so completely in an army’s favor as a spellcaster raining far devastation down on the fields of war. Entire regiments are turned into clouds of billowing ash by a fireball, or a charge of thundering knights is mired by a sea of entangling roots called forth from the ground. Many believe such potent figures can only be countered by other wielders of the arcane. Yet there are warriors, masters of mundane weaponry, who have found a way to push back against this oppressive power through the fruits of technology.

Using long rifles, these snipers have brought doom even to the most skilled of spellcasters. They understand the gestures and phrases required to weave enchantments and invocations, and they know precisely at which point during the process a disturbance will cause the most turbulence. An incantation that would make the land buckle and break, or cause flames to rain from the sky, can be halted by a well-placed shot, disrupting the magic, damping its effects, or negating the devastation entirely. Even the most dreadful beings, liches and Sload Mage-Priests, have been interrupted while preparing to utter words of primal power that would dissolve reality itself.

> *Failure:* The spell is dampened. The caster can still choose to cast the spell, but it becomes weakened, and until the end of the rifleman's next turn: attack rolls made by the spell have disadvantage, targets have advantage on saving throws made to resist the spell, and if the spell allows a creature to make a saving throw to take only half damage, a successful save causes no damage and a failed save causes half damage instead.

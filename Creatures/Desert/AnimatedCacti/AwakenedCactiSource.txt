## Awakened Cacti
*Barbed Guardians of Precious Desert Water*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert; **Treasure:** None <br>
Even amid the dry sands and blistering heat of an inhospitable desert, life-giving magic can coalesce to suffuse the local vegetation with sapience, allowing them to move and speak with voices of their own. Most commonly granted such sentience are the cacti that litter these arid lands, uprooting themselves from the dry soil to stride across the parched wastes.

Such cacti are often awakened by druids to guard pockets of life scattered throughout the dunes, keeping watch for loathsome horrors that would despoil these precious sanctuaries. At other times, they are brought into being to serve more sordid schemes. These cacti are able to store gallons of water within their fibrous, spined forms for months, even years. Such vast reserves can be as precious as silver or gold to desert tribes that struggle for survival, often warring bitterly over what little water can be salvaged.

### Cacti Warders
A Cacti warder may at first seem like an oddly shaped cactus, rising from the ground and unfurling its body into full view. It is a massive, barrel-chested creature supported by four thick legs, each ending in paw-like digits tipped with thorns. Its head bears jaws filled with rows of needle-like teeth, and the whole of its bulk is covered in waxy green skin layered with fine barbs.

Despite their bestial form, these creatures possess remarkable intelligence, acting as stewards and sages of oases hidden deep within the desert. Many adventurers seek out a cacti warder on spiritual pilgrimages, hoping for guidance. If the creature deems a traveler worthy, it releases psychedelic compounds that can liberate the mind, allowing one to delve deep into their own psyche.

Those who incur the ire of such a hazardous scholar, or attempt to take its vision-granting substances without permission, are swiftly punished. The warder can unleash a rain of piercing spines or capture victims with its needle-filled maw. The same hallucinogenic compounds it uses for guidance can also debilitate its foes; a breath of bizarre mist may send targets staggering in random directions or collapsing in bewilderment.

#### Cacti Warder Tactics
Cacti warders use Psychedelic Breath when they can catch three or more targets in the area. After using Psychedelic Breath, they follow up with Needle Burst under similar conditions, again aiming to strike at least three enemies.

Once their foes have been forced to spread out, Cacti Warders shift tactics and focus on isolating a single target—typically the one with the lowest Constitution. They concentrate their attacks on this creature to embed as many barbs as possible. If they successfully grapple the target, they drag it away from its allies, preventing others from coming to its aid.

## Armadillofolk
*Hardened Nomads United Through Sacred Familial Bonds*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Forest, Grasslands, Hills, Swamps; **Treasure:** Relics <br>


Within tunnels carved into the hillsides of arid landscapes dwell a people who have endured the test of time again and again. The armadillofolk hold no value more sacred than the bond and defense of one’s siblings.

For these armored folk, this bond is inherent, as armadillofolk mothers always give birth to identical quadruplets of the same sex. Stranger still, these siblings share a connection known simply as “the Roll.” This bond psychically links the four together, allowing them to share thoughts and emotions. The psionic link strengthens as they age, enabling flawless coordination without a word spoken. With discipline, this power can even be used to subtly manipulate their own bodies.


***Union of Bonds.*** Armadillofolk society is entirely structured around the value of one’s Roll. A set of quadruplets never willingly separates and makes all major decisions collectively.

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br>

Upon reaching adulthood, a Roll typically sets out into a nomadic life, relying solely on one another for survival. In time, female Rolls may settle if they discover a suitable place to carve a lasting settlement into the earth. Male Rolls continue wandering until they encounter a group of unattached female Rolls; at that point, both Rolls may consider union.

When determining such unions, individual attraction carries little weight. What matters is the compatibility of the two Rolls as unified entities. If the pairing is agreed upon, one male and one female from each Roll will mate, producing a new set of quadruplets.

***Shells and Loyalty of Stone.*** The armadillofolk worship no gods. It is said among them that they were created to mock and defy the gods themselves. The details of this ancient humiliation vary from Roll to Roll, each preserving its own version of which deities were mocked and how.

Having forsaken divine worship, their spiritual practices instead center upon the veneration of the Roll. Rituals are performed to strengthen familial bonds. Some take the form of prolonged meditations within special chambers, lasting for days, during which the minds of the four interlock so completely that the sense of individual self nearly dissolves. Others involve the creation of sacred artifacts: each member contributes to the crafting and psychically imprints upon the object as it is formed. Weaving agave fibers and shaping jade are among the primary crafts of these rites.

So sacred is the Roll that an armadillofolk individual will sacrifice anything to protect their brothers or sisters. They will endure, or even commit, grievous acts if it ensures the safety of their siblings.


***Battle Brothers and Sisters.*** Armadillofolk are generally peaceful and avoid confrontation, as well as prolonged relationships with other peoples, save for trading the gems they mine and the garments they weave.

However, should one member of a Roll be threatened, the aggressor must contend with the entire quartet until driven off or slain.

In battle, armadillofolk fight with deadly harmony. They execute precise maneuvers without speech, wielding jade weapons infused with psionic force and crafted by their own Roll. They can curl into armored spheres, deflecting lethal blows from their hardened shells. Using telekinetic force, they launch themselves forward, barreling into foes with devastating impact.

Even aerial opponents are not safe from these tactics. A Roll of armadillofolk can transform into a barrage of living missiles in an instant.

***Found Family.*** It is possible, through powerful ritual and the expenditure of great quantities of jade, for an armadillofolk individual to form a Roll with non-sibling creatures. Such an act is deeply controversial. Most armadillofolk consider bonding outside one’s birth Roll unnatural, even blasphemous. Those who attempt it usually do so out of desperation.

### Armadillofolk Tactics
Armadillofolk prioritize disrupting vulnerable backline targets, using javelins to pressure spellcasters and ranged enemies. If all four members of a Roll can coordinate, they will use Roll Out on a single target to quickly overwhelm it. This tactic is risky, as emerging from their shells costs a full action.

If a member becomes bloodied or overwhelmed, it will use Roll Out defensively to disengage and escape. Even while rolled up, armadillofolk remain useful by positioning adjacent to enemies to grant advantage to their allies’ attacks.

### Armadillofolk Burrow Defender
Among the armadillofolk, it is crucial that each member of a Roll undertakes a distinct role to ensure the group’s continued survival. One may dedicate their life to gathering food, another to crafting tools and clothing. Often, one member devotes themselves to mastering martial weapons and military tactics, serving as the primary defender of the Roll.

When conflict arises, Rolls are often more organized and effective than formally trained military units.

\columnbreak

#### Armadillofolk Burrow Defender Tactics
Burrow defenders act as frontline anchors. They may roll into position alongside another defender to reinforce a defensive line.

If a burrow defender or nearby ally becomes bloodied, they will roll up and remain close, maintaining the benefits of Guardian Formation while reducing incoming damage.

### Armadillofolk Manipulator
Some armadillofolk are attuned to unseen forces that shape the world, able to sense the subtle gravitational pull of the earth itself. These individuals can harness this connection through the power of their Roll.

They are able to infuse stones with tremendous force. When thrown or launched, these stones can repel or draw in creatures near their point of impact. Most remarkable of all, they can teleport those with whom they share a mental link, provided all remain in contact with the earth.

\columnbreak

#### Armadillofolk Manipulator Tactics
Manipulators operate from the backline, controlling enemy positioning and battlefield flow. They use Gravity Slings to:

&#8226; Push enemies away to disrupt formations.

&#8226; Pull clustered enemies together for area attacks.

&#8226; Reposition allies, either pulling them into combat to overwhelm a target or pushing them out to safety.

They use Leyline Shift to escape melee or to swap positions with a frontline ally, reinforcing pressure where needed.

### Armadillofolk Thought Weaver
Among the most dangerous of their kind are the thought weavers, armadillofolk who have pushed the innate mental power of the Roll to its zenith.

They manipulate waves of force capable of tearing matter apart, crushing minds, and sending victims into comas, while simultaneously invigorating their siblings in battle. Their most insidious ability is the imposition of a twisted imitation of the Roll onto their enemies, binding the minds of their foes together so that any psychic harm inflicted upon one reverberates through them all.

\columnbreak

####  Armadillofolk Thought Weaver Tactics
Thought weavers begin combat by targeting multiple enemies, attempting to bind as many minds as possible to amplify group damage.
They prioritize:

&#8226; Mind Spike against targets with low Intelligence

&#8226; Psychic Headbutt against enemies concentrating on powerful spells (3rd level or higher)

They reserve *Here We Go* for moments when allies are fully positioned, maximizing the impact of a coordinated assault.

### Jade-Sworn Armadillofolk 
Some Rolls, whose bonds have grown exceptionally strong, partake in sacred rites of crafting jade weapons together. These weapons become bound to the soul of one of the members.

Such armadillofolk are living symbols of endurance. They can draw life-giving energy from their weapons to heal their entire Roll, while also delivering devastating blows capable of felling even powerful foes with a single strike.

\columnbreak

#### Armadillo Jade-Sworn Tactics
Jade-sworn focus on high-threat enemies, particularly those that deal heavy area damage or make multiple attacks each turn.

When bloodied, a jade-sworn will withdraw by rolling away and recovering hit points. Nearby allies will follow suit, creating a temporary defensive reset before re-engaging.

> ### Traits
>
> ***United Roll.*** The armadillofolk is linked with three other creatures that have this ability. Each linked creature can telepathically speak to one another while they are within 60 feet of each other.
>
> ***Invigorating Aura.*** The armadillofolk and each creature it is linked with regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of their turns if it has at least 1 Hit Point and the armadillofolk has 1 or more Hit Points.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Jade Warhammer.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit (with advantage if one creature it is linked with is within 5 feet of the target and is not Incapacitated), reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Force damage. If the target is a creature, until the end of the armadillofolk's next turn, all of its damage rolls are reduced by 5.
>
> ***Roll Out.*** If the armadillofolk is rolled into a ball, it moves up to 30 feet in a straight line. Any other creatures linked with the armadillofolk within 60 feet of it may choose to use their reactions to roll up and also move up to 30 feet in a straight line. If any creature moving this way would collide with another creature after having moved 10 feet, that creature must make a *Dexterity or Strength Saving Throw (its choice):* DC 13. <br>
> *Failure:* The creature takes 8 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. For each additional linked creature that collides with the same creature, the DC increases by 2 and the creature takes an extra 8 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If four creatures collide with it and it failed its saving throw, it is Dazed until the end of its next turn.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Roll Up.*** The armadillofolk rolls into a ball. Until it emerges, it gains a +3 bonus to AC. While in its shell, the armadillofolk's speed is 0 and can't increase, it has Resistance to falling damage, it has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws, it can't take reactions, and the only action it can take is an action to emerge.

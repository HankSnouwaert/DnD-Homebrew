## Monstrous Actions

If you wish to make your vampire a true solo threat, you may grant it an extra Monstrous Action it can use each round of combat, increasing its CR by 1.

This grants the vampire an additional action in combat, taken on Initiative Count 21 (losing ties), separate from the vampire’s normal initiative. On that count, the vampire can take one of the following actions, provided it is not Incapacitated; it may still use Tenacious Will even while Incapacitated.

**Tenacious Will.**  If the vampire is subject to an ongoing effect that allows a saving throw at the start or end of its turn (or as an action), it can immediately make that saving throw with advantage. On a success, the effect ends.

**Rush.**  The vampire moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Swipe.**  *Melee Weapon Attack:* Vampiric Power Bonus to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (3d12) Slashing damage.

**Vampiric Power.** The vampire uses one of its Vampiric Powers.

**Spellcasting.** The vampire casts a 1st- or 2nd-level spell it knows from any spellcasting feature it has.

You may choose to grant the vampire additional Monstrous Actions on Initiative Count 15 and Initiative Count 0 (losing ties). For each additional action granted this way, the vampire’s challenge rating increases by 1.

##  Vampiric Traits & Powers

Any vampire can be given any of the following Vampiric Traits or Vampiric Powers. Vampiric Traits are passive abilities, and each one granted increases its CR by 1 unless otherwise stated. Vampric Powers can be used as a Bonus Action, and granting one or more of these powers increases the vampire's CR by 1. If a Vampiric Power involves an attack roll or forces a saving throw, the attack bonus or save DC used can be found in the Vampire's stat block in the Vampiric Power bonus action. 

### General Vampiric Traits & Powers
The following abilities are non-specific in their theme and could be applied to a broad swath of vampires from different backgrounds.

#### Flying Horror (Vampiric Trait)

*Some vampires can rise into the night sky, floating effortlessly and soaring through the heavens. From above, they swoop down to gorge themselves on prey below.*

The Vampire has a Fly Speed of 60 feet and can hover. Vampire Masters and Vampire Lords gain a fly speed of 70.

#### Misty Step (Vampiric Power)

*When threatened, the vampire can dissolve into vapor and vanish, only to reform elsewhere moments later.*

The vampire teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space that it can see.

####  Night Stalker (Vampiric Trait)

*As silent as the flutter of a moth’s wing, the vampire moves across walls and ceilings and is able to pass through the smallest of openings to reach its unsuspecting victim.*

The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check, and can move through and stop in a space large enough to fit a creature one size smaller than it without squeezing. The vampire also gains a +5 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) Checks.

#### Regeneration (Vampiric Trait)

*Even when inflicted with wounds that would kill any mortal, the vampire’s body continually renews itself. Torn flesh knits back together in moments, and shattered bones fuse and become whole once more.*

The vampire regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.

#### Spectral Form (Vampiric Trait)

*The most formidable vampires have shed their physical bodies, becoming phantasmal beings able to pass effortlessly through walls, barriers, and fortifications. Conventional weapons are largely ineffective against these specters, rendering them nearly indestructible.*

The Vampire has a Fly Speed of 10 feet and can hover. The Vampire can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object. It also gains resistance to all damage types except Force, Psychic, and Radiant. 

**Increases CR by 2.** Granting the vampire this trait increases its CR by 2 rather than 1.

#### Supernatural Terror (Vampiric Trait)

*The vampire’s wicked spirit has twisted its appearance into a manifestation of pure corruption. Any creature that gazes upon this horrific visage bears witness to true evil and is gripped by overwhelming dread.*

The Vampire emits an aura of terror in 30-foot emanation from itself. Each non-allied creature that starts its turn in the aura must make a Wisdom saving throw.

 *Failure:* The creature becomes Frightened of the <br> Vampire until the end of the Vampire’s next turn. <br> While Frightened, the creature can’t cast spells <br> other than cantrips.  <br>
 &emsp;*Failure by 5+:* While Frightened, the creature <br> can’t take reactions or Bonus Actions and <br> must use the Dash action on its turn to <br> move away from the vampire by <br> the safest route.  <br>
 &emsp;*Success:* The creature is <br> immune to this aura for <br> 1 hour.

### Vampiric Knight Traits

Many mortals spend the entirety of their lives training to become accomplished warriors, honing blade and body to lethal precision. Such masters of combat, however, are but feeble when compared to the vampires who have devoted an eternity to perfecting their art of war. These immortals roam the world in search of worthy foes, endlessly refining their techniques and pushing their prowess to ever-greater heights.

The oldest among them have studied the martial traditions of countless cultures, enduring innumerable duels and battles against every manner of opponent. When such vast experience is joined with inhuman speed and strength, even the greatest mortal champions of legend stand only the faintest chance of prevailing against these ferocious soldiers of darkness.

Vampires of this disposition adhere to a grim and macabre code. They disdain the slaughter of helpless villagers and common peasants, feeding instead only upon those capable of offering true resistance. Most who face them, however, lack the skill to land even a single blow before being impaled, hewn apart, and drained of blood.

Those rare individuals who offer a meaningful challenge—yet still fall—may suffer a far darker fate. A victorious martial vampire may deem its broken foe worthy of the “gift” of immortality, binding them forever to an existence of blood and battle.
These paragons of slaughter wander the lands in search of worthy blood against which to test their mettle. Tales are told of knights astride black chargers, clad in crimson mail and plate, bearing unholy heraldry as they ride through the wilderness in the dead of night, seeking honorable warriors to duel. Other legends speak of ruined keeps that house crusaders of death—unholy paladins who march forth from vile sanctums to sack the countryside, annihilating any armies sent to quell their nefarious rampage.


#### Red Frenzy (Vampiric Power)

*The vampire charges heedlessly into the fray, cleaving apart any foes caught in the path of its rancor.*

As a Bonus Action, the vampire moves up to half its Speed and then makes one Rend attack with advantage. If it does so, attack rolls against the vampire have advantage until the start of its next turn.


#### Honour of Death (Vampiric Power)

*The vampire is bound by an unholy code of honor, a grim creed it can impose upon others. Through this dread oath, it compels even the most fearsome warriors to face it in single combat, for only such pristine blood can sate the undead champion’s appetite.*

The vampire casts *Compelled Duel*, using its Vampiric Power save DC for the spell’s saving throw.


#### Dread Knight (Vampiric Trait)

*A virtuoso of mounted warfare, the vampire and its loathsome gravebeast move as one, becoming a beacon of death across the battlefield.*

The vampire gains the following benefits:

- While mounted, the vampire has advantage on attack rolls against any unmounted creature within 5 feet of its mount that is at least one size smaller than the mount.
- If the vampire’s mount is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage on a successful save and only half damage on a failed save. For the mount to gain this benefit, the vampire must be riding it, and neither the vampire nor the mount can be Incapacitated.
- While mounted, the vampire can force an attack that hits its mount to instead hit the vampire, provided the vampire isn’t Incapacitated.


#### Armored Death (Vampiric Trait)

*Clad in dense steel plating, the vampire advances upon its prey unhindered, blows glancing harmlessly from its metal shell.*

The vampire gains a +4 bonus to its Armor Class and is considered to be wearing metal armor.


#### Heart Piercing (Vampiric Trait)

*The vampire favors polearms and spears, tools exquisitely suited to impaling foes and holding them in place while it feeds.*

While the vampire is holding a polearm, its Rend attack is altered in the following ways:

- The attack deals Piercing damage instead of Slashing damage.
- The attack has a reach of 10 feet.
- While the target is grappled by this attack, it is also Restrained. The vampire can’t make attacks against other targets while it has a creature grappled this way.
- The vampire can take a Reaction to make a Rend attack against a creature that enters the attack’s reach.


#### Strength of Steel (Vampiric Trait)

*Driven by unnatural brawn, the vampire wields heavy weapons with devastating force, pulverizing bone and staggering all struck by these murderous implements.*

While the vampire is holding a melee weapon that deals Bludgeoning damage, its Rend attack is altered in the following ways:

- The attack deals Bludgeoning damage instead of Slashing damage.
- The attack no longer grapples the target. Instead, the target must make a *Constitution Saving Throw*.

  *Failure:* The creature is Dazed until the end of the vampire’s next turn. While Dazed, the creature can’t concentrate or cast spells other than cantrips.  
  *Failure by 5+, or if the creature is already Dazed:* The creature is Stunned until the end of the vampire’s next turn.

#### Blademaster (Vampiric Trait)

*Not even the eldest mortal swordmasters can rival the skill accumulated over the vampire’s tenebrous existence. Each precise maneuver shifts the battle to its favor, until its foe’s doom is written in exquisite strokes of the blade.*

While the vampire is holding a melee weapon that deals Slashing damage, its Rend attack gains one of the following effects (the vampire’s choice):

- **Defensive Slash.** The target has disadvantage on attack rolls

#### Domination (Vampiric Trait)

*The bite of this alluring predator is suffused with unnatural bliss. Mortals subjected to its intoxicating influence find their loyalties dissolved, fighting with frenzied devotion for their new master.*

*Wisdom Saving Throw:* Triggered when the vampire hits a creature with its Bite attack.

*Failure:* The creature becomes Charmed by the vampire. While Charmed in this way, the creature can’t cast spells other than cantrips, and the vampire forms a telepathic link with the creature while both are on the same plane of existence. On the vampire’s turn, it can issue commands to the creature through this link (no action required), such as “Attack that creature,” “Move over there,” or “Fetch that object.” The creature does its best to obey on its turn. If it completes an order and receives no further commands, it acts as it chooses, prioritizing its own safety.  
At the end of each of the creature’s turns, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

*Failure by 5+ (including on repeat saves):* While Charmed, the creature is **Quickened**.

The vampire can command the creature to take a Reaction, but doing so requires the vampire to use its own Reaction.

**Increases CR by 2.** Granting the vampire this trait increases its CR by 2 rather than 1.

#### Hypnotic Allure (Vampiric Power)

*The vampire moves with a swaying, entrancing rhythm, draining vigor from those nearby as helpless victims are lulled into a waking trance.*

*Wisdom Saving Throw:* Each creature in a 20-foot cone.

*Failure:* The creature becomes *Drowsy**. The effect ends early if the creature takes damage from a source other than the vampire, or if another creature within 5 feet of it uses an action to shake it free of the effect.

*Failure by 5+, or if the creature is already Drowsy:* While Drowsy, the creature is Unconscious.

*The vampire’s unearthly beauty ensnares the senses of those who gaze upon its immaculate visage, leaving them helpless against the oncoming claws and dark fangs of this terrible beast.*

The vampire emits an aura of seduction in a 30-foot emanation. Each non-allied creature that starts its turn in the aura must make a *Wisdom Saving Throw*.

*Failure:* The creature becomes Charmed by the vampire until the end of the vampire’s next turn. While Charmed in this way, the creature can’t cast spells other than cantrips.  
&emsp;*Failure by 5+:* The creature falls Prone, and while Charmed, its speed is 0.  
*Success:* The creature is immune to this aura for 1 hour.

#### Surge of Speed (Vampiric Action)

*The vampire weaves effortlessly through its <br> foes,  crimson splattering across the battlefield <br> as  throats are torn open in a blur of motion.*

The vampire takes the Dash or Disengage <bR> action.



#### Summon Bound Soul <br> (Vampiric Power)

*Not even death frees one from the vampire’s <br> allurement, for the spirits of its victims remain <br> shackled to the seductress, forever trapped by <br> their delusion.*

The vampire summons a **Tormented Specter** <br> in an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet <br> of it. The specter remains until it is reduced to <br> 0 hit points or until the vampire dies.

A specter summoned this way acts on <br> Initiative Count 0 (losing ties) of each round. If <br> the vampire attempts to summon a specter <br> while three or more specters summoned by this <br> power are already active, the vampire must <br> choose one existing specter to be immediately <br> destroyed.

#### Transfixing Performance <br> (Vampiric Action)

*The vampire performs a tantalizing display that ensnares the senses. Any warrior who beholds it finds their mind clouded and their will to fight utterly drowned.*

*Wisdom Saving Throw:* Each non-Undead creature in a 15-foot emanation originating from the vampire.

*Failure:* Until the end of the vampire’s next turn, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls, can make only one attack if it takes the Attack action, and can’t cast spells other than cantrips.  
&emsp;*Failure by 5+:* The creature also can’t take the Attack action and can’t cast spells.  
*Success:* The creature is immune to this effect for 1 hour.



#### Uncanny Reflexes (Vampiric Trait)

*With blinding speed and impossible grace, the vampire evades volleys of arrows and torrents of flame, emerging without so much as a singed hair or a single scratch.*

If the vampire is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage on a successful save and only half damage on a failed save.

In addition, the vampire gains the following reaction:

***Evasive Twist.***  
*Trigger:* A creature the vampire can see hits it with an attack.  
*Response:* The attacker rerolls the attack roll. If the attack still hits, the damage dealt by the attack is halved (round down).

### Feral Vampire Traits

Within shadowed forests and forgotten sewers dwell vampires who have cast off all trappings of civilization, devolving into profane savages. These feral undead arise when a vampire surrenders wholly to its hunger for gore, losing all restraint and feeding upon anything, living or dead, that it can catch.

Others are driven into this existence by circumstance rather than choice. Some are hunted relentlessly by vampire slayers, while others have committed unforgivable transgressions against their own kind and now flee the vengeance of their brethren. Forced into seclusion, these vampires lurk in the most forsaken places, feeding upon recently buried corpses or carrion to avoid drawing attention and inviting destruction.

Whatever the cause, the outcome is always the same. The vampire’s body and mind become irrevocably corrupted. Its predatory instincts intensify beyond reason, and its form twists into something barely recognizable as the man or woman it once was. These transformations vary wildly: some develop grotesquely distorted faces, their jaws packed with rows of serrated teeth crusted with putrefaction; others swell into hulking brutes, their frames wrapped in gray, leathery hide stretched tight over distorted muscle.

Such opportunistic hunters are a constant menace to merchants traveling remote roads, to wanderers in dark wilderness, and to those who labor in the sewers beneath great cities. From shadowed crevices and undergrowth, these vampires wait for moments of vulnerability, springing forth in unrestrained fury to slash and tear apart their unsuspecting prey, dragging shredded remains back to be devoured.

Most feral vampires rule over domains as forsaken as themselves, surrounding their lairs with ghouls, ghasts, and other degenerate undead. These courts are crude mockeries of the refined hierarchies maintained by more civilized vampires. Their revolting subjects follow their master without hesitation, raiding graveyards, attacking isolated outposts, and even attempting to overwhelm bands of adventurers who stray into their territory.

Yet not all such creatures have forgotten their former <br> lives. Among them are the Strigen, once such loathsome canaille were rulers of a grand empire long since lost to history. Their arrogance and contempt for other bloodlines was so profound that when their dynasty finally collapsed, their own kind turned upon them. Scattered and broken, the Strigen fled their ruined homeland, dispersing across the world to escape the wrath of their former brethren.

In the depths of darkness, the Strigen brood upon their lost glory. It is whispered that some have begun to rally the wretched beings that lurk in shadowed places, gathering the damned into crude armies in a bid to resurrect the empire they once ruled.

#### Grave Filth (Vampiric Trait)

*Having feasted upon countless decaying corpses, the vampire’s fangs are permanently coated in vile toxins. Wounds inflicted by its bite stiffen muscle and wither flesh.*

*Constitution Saving Throw:* Triggered when the vampire hits a creature with its Bite attack. 

*Failure:* The target is Poisoned. While Poisoned in this way, the creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. On a failed repeat save, the creature takes 13 (3d8) Poison damage.  
*Failure by 5+ (including on repeat saves):* While Poisoned, the target is Paralyzed until the poison ends.

#### Hunter in the Shadows (Vampiric Trait)
*The vampire melts into surrounding gloom, moving unseen as the blood-hungry beast prowls the night in search of its next feast.*

The vampire is Invisible while in Dim Light or Darkness. In addition, while in Dim Light or Darkness, the vampire can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.

#### Infinite Fury (Vampiric Trait)

*The miserable unlife of the vampire has stripped it of all limits to the hatred it bears for those who wrong it. Any who dare harm the creature soon feel the full consequences of their actions.*

When the vampire takes damage for the first time on a turn, it gains 1 Fury (condition described below). The vampire can have up to 12 Fury instead of 6.

#### Massive Monstrosity (Vampiric Trait)

*Some vampires who fully embrace their predatory instincts grow beyond all restraint. These hulking abominations swell into massive forms, capable of enduring horrific punishment.*

The vampire gains 50 additional hit points, and its size becomes Large.

#### Shredding Jaws (Vampiric Trait)

*The vampire’s jagged fangs sink deep, not only draining blood but tearing flesh from bone. Victims are left crippled as muscle and sinew are shredded beneath its brutal bite.*<br>
*Strength Saving Throw:* Triggered when the vampire hits a creature with its Bite attack.

*Failure:* The target takes 14 (4d6) Piercing damage and suffers a rending wound. While wounded this way, the target has disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws, ability checks, and attack rolls, and its Speed is halved.

The wounded creature, or another creature within 5 feet of it, can use an action to make a *Wisdom (Medicine) Check* against the vampire’s Vampiric Power save DC, ending the effect on a success. The effect also ends if the creature regains 15 or more hit points. 

*Failure by 5+:* The target instead takes 21 (6d6) Piercing damage.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Fury  
> The creature gains levels of Fury that cause its attacks to deal increasing amounts of damage with each level.
> * The creature can only gain levels of Fury during combat, and all levels are lost when combat ends.
> * A creature can gain up to a max of 6 levels of Fury.
> * First time each turn the creature hits a target with an attack, it deals an additional 1d4 damage for each level of Fury it has.
> * A creature loses all levels of Fury if it becomes Charmed or Frightened, or is affected by the *Calm Emotions* spell.  It also can't gain levels Fury while under these conditions.

#### Summon Ghoul (Vampiric Power)

*Degenerate scavengers are drawn to the feral vampire’s savage power, gathering around it in reverent awe and deifying the undead lord as a god of death.*

The vampire summons a **Crypt Ghoul** in an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet of it. The ghoul remains until it is reduced to 0 hit points or until the vampire dies.

A ghoul summoned this way acts on Initiative Count 0 (losing ties) of each round. If the vampire attempts to summon a ghoul while three or more ghouls summoned by this power are already active, the vampire must choose one existing ghoul to be immediately destroyed.

**Increases CR by 3.** Granting the vampire this power increases its CR by 3 rather than 1.

#### Unholy Fortitude (Vampiric Trait)
*Axes bite into its chest, flails slam against its skull, and bolts of flame scorch its body, yet the vampire does not falter, advancing relentlessly through devastation that would fell any mortal.*

If the vampire takes 10 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 hit points, it is reduced to 1 hit point instead.  
If the damage is greater than 10, the vampire must succeed on a Constitution Saving Throw with a DC equal to 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is Radiant or from a critical hit. On a successful save, the vampire is reduced to 1 hit point instead.

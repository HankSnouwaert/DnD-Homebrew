## Vargoths
*Bestial Vampires, Fully Succumbed to Their Savage Nature*<br>
**Habitat:** Underdark; **Treasure:** Any <br>
Vampires are capable of cunning and stratagem beyond that of most other unholy beings. They devise insidious plots to ensnare their adversaries, master many forms of warfare and arcane arts, and puppet vast networks of servants to enact their will. Yet at their core, every vampire is driven by a ravenous urge to feed upon the blood of the living.

Most vampires are able to keep such instincts at bay; some even counterbalance them by fashioning veneers of nobility around themselves, masking their base appetites beneath ritual, refinement, and false restraint. Others, however, through loss of control or utter rejection of their former selves, shed what little humanity remains and allow savage gluttony to consume them wholly.

As the last remnants of civility are eroded away, body and mind twist into something altogether bestial. Vampires who undergo such a metamorphosis are known among mortals as Vargoths.

These ghastly beasts are death-dealing brutes. Their frames swell until they tower over even the tallest of men, massive bodies coated in matted fur stretched across rippling muscle. Elongated limbs end in sickle-like claws capable of tearing through oak as easily as flesh, while their maws bristle with sword-like fangs, terrible weapons that can shred a warrior into bloody ruin with a single bite. Such gnashing jaws reduce their victims to gore, which the beast devours in frenzied gulps.

Though no longer capable of higher thought, Vargoths are far from mindless. They possess keen predatory instincts, allowing them to bring down powerful foes or even entire groups of combatants. They may stalk prey for many nights, waiting patiently for a moment of weakness, or retreat when outmatched, only to return once their supernatural regeneration has mended every wound.

Vargoths often come to dominate other loathsome pack-beasts, which instinctively recognize the vampire-spawn as their alpha. Ghouls in particular are commonly seen under a Vargoth’s command, sharing a grim kinship in their warped, feral state. Such alliances are devastating, for these roaming wretches slay indiscriminately and gorge themselves upon fresh meat, leaving trails of crimson in their wake.

Those who face a Vargoth have but one hope: to end the terror swiftly, for its rage only grows the longer the beast is allowed to fight.

### Vargoth Tactics
Varghoths move into the largest group of enemies, attempting to hit as many targets with their rend attack as they can, Grappling the target with the lowest Strength and slowing each other target. They then bite the Grappled target. The vargoth will then move out of combat if it has less than half its hit points, as it keeps rending and biting the target it is Grappling. Otherwise, it will keep moving into the largest group of foes it can find hitting as many targets as it can to build up levels of Fury.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Regeneration.*** The vargoth regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point and isn’t in sunlight or running water. If the vargoth takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn’t function at the start of the vargoth’s next turn.
>
> ***Vampire Weakness.*** The vargoth has the following weaknesses:
>
> **Forbiddance.** The vargoth can’t enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.
>
> **Running Water.** The vargoth takes 20 Acid damage if it ends its turn in running water.
>
> **Sunlight.** The vargoth takes 20 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The vargoth makes a Rend attack against any number of targets within 10 feet of it, then makes one Bite attack.
>
> ***Rend.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
> *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Slashing damage. If the target is a creature, the vargoth chooses one of the following effects:
>
> - **Grab.** If the target is Medium or smaller, it is Grappled (escape DC 17). The vargoth can Grapple only one creature at a time.
> - **Slow.** The target’s speed is reduced by 15 feet until the start of the vargoth’s next turn.
>
> Hit or miss, the vargoth gains one level of Fury*.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target that is willing or rrappled.  
> *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage. The target’s hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and the vargoth regains hit points equal to that amount. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

> ##### New Condition: Fury  
> The creature gains levels of Fury that cause its attacks to deal increasing amounts of damage with each level. 
>
> **Gaining Fury.** Unless an ability specifies otherwise, a creature can gain up to 6 levels of Fury and only during combat. <Br>
> &emsp;**Extra Damage.** The first time each turn that the creature hits a target with an attack, it deals an additional 1d4 damage for each level of Fury it has.<br>
> &emsp;**Losing Fury.** All levels of Fury are lost if a creature goes 1 minute without gaining any levels of Fury. Additionally, a creature loses all levels of Fury if it becomes Charmed or Frightened, or is affected by the *Calm Emotions* spell. It also can't gain levels Fury while under these conditions.

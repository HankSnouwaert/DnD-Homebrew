## Bonus Hydra Variants

### Fire Hydras
**Habitat:** Mountain, Swamp, Volcanic; **Treasure:** Any <br>

Fire hydras are evil creatures whose many maws burn with ferocious spite, taking primal glee in destruction. Each maw the creature possesses glows with cinders, its teeth akin to heated pokers, inflicting terrible burns and deep gashes with every bite. Such creatures are also capable of pelting enemies with a persistent barrage of flame, consuming them in an inferno long before their heads are within striking distance.

These infernal beasts are the product of dark elven magic. The shadowed elves found even the already magnificent hydra wanting, so they captured wild specimens and subjected them to generations of cruel enchantments. 

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br>

Over successive generations, they infused their captives with dragon's blood, eventually creating these wicked brutes.

Dark elf armies that employ fire hydras possess a living firestorm at their fingertips. Such a creature serves as both artillery and a front-line assault beast, reducing opposing ranks of archers to piles of writhing, charred flesh. It crashes through the formations of even the most elite soldiers, who desperately hack at the endless deluge of snapping heads, only for the beast to emerge from the smoke and carnage bearing even more hungry jaws, eager to continue its hellish rampage.

### Frost Hydras
**Habitat:** Arctic, Mountain; **Treasure:** Any <br>
If you were to brave the frozen waste and go deep where no cattle or crop can flourish and man’s cities cannot take root, you may happen upon great glaciers towering over the tundra, monuments that have remained undisturbed since man lit his first fire and had only shaped stones to hunt with.

If you approach the wall of ice, you may see an odd shape within the structure: a mass of beastly heads trapped deep within, fixed in place, outstretched and weaving between one another like some sort of tangle of vines, all originating from a massive figure at the core of the knot, larger than a bison. The central figure bears four muscular limbs and a sinuous tail.

If gifted with good sight, you may even make out the features of many animals within the blur. The heads have the eyes of a wildcat, blank and yellow with black slits, unblinking. Fangs of wolves gleam in open jaws. 

\columnbreak

<br>

Claws bearing a resemblance to those of a wolverine float motionless in the sea of crystal. Its whole figure is covered in a thick, shaggy, rust-colored coat as dense as any musk ox’s.

Yet deeper observation of its anatomy only amplifies the already bewildering mixture of features. For the heads and necks bear more resemblance to those of a snake than any mammal. Even weightless in its tomb and obscured by layers of fur, the body from which all its heads flower has the bearing and tail of a large lizard rather than any canine, feline, or other mammal.

Such findings may fascinate you and spark all manner of questions. Know this, though: if you would seek to understand this anomaly and your curiosity would have you wrest this corpse from the frost which holds it so that you may examine the creature with clarity, let your eagerness pass you by. Leave, and let the fractured sight of the thing be all that you take with you from that place.

For you look upon not a grave, but a prison. A godless cold voracity held back only by such gleaming walls. So leave such bitter bringers of death forgotten.

### Flame Skull Hydras
**Habitat:** Swamp, Underdark; **Treasure:** Any <br>
When greater beings of necromancy—liches, vampires, or other undead tyrants—succeed in capturing living hydras and binding them to their will, they do not merely raise the beasts as ordinary undead. Through a guarded ritual of desecration, these creatures are elevated into something far worse: nightmares that hover in the space between life and death. Known as Flame Skull Hydras, they become living engines of dark magic, capable of reaping entire armies with torrents of necrotic flame and regenerating heads that burn with baleful green fire.

The process of creating a Flame Skull Hydra is similar to the creation of a classic flameskull. First, the captive hydra is force-fed the carefully prepared remains of dozens of slain magic-users until its multiple gullets are thoroughly gorged with arcane flesh. Once the creature is bloated and lethargic, it is subjected to a sustained barrage of the deadliest hexes and invocations its masters can muster: spells of rotting, burning, desiccation, and soul-searing fire that eat away its flesh faster than even its legendary regeneration can repair. 

\columnbreak

<br><br>

The bombardment continues without mercy until the beast hangs mere inches from true death, its scales blackened and cracking, its remaining heads lolling. Only then does the magical onslaught cease.

The hydra is allowed to recover. As its body desperately draws upon the mystical remains fermenting in its stomach, the absorbed arcane essence is redirected and twisted by a continuous web of mutating auras.Instead of simply regenerating healthy flesh, the creature rebuilds itself according to a new, blasphemous design. Its scales take on a charred, bone-like texture. Each head is emaciated, with ghostly green or violet flame in its eyes.

The creatures that survive this brutal rite emerge not only more powerful, but far more cunning. Having absorbed the arcane knowledge of every magic-user they consumed, they possess a predatory intelligence and an instinctive mastery of destructive spellcraft that no ordinary hydra could ever claim. Their blackened, scarred bodies still regenerate with terrifying speed, yet the process has been fundamentally altered.

**Creatures Caught Partially Within the Cage.** When the cage is created, creatures only partially within the cube's area, or those too large to fit inside the area, must choose to be pushed away from the center of the area until they are completely outside the area or move into the cage if they can fit. 

**Creatures In The Cage When It Forms.** Any creature fully within the cage when it forms (including those that chose to move inside it) must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 7d8 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. 

**Moving Through The Cage.** A creature takes the 7d8 fire damage when it passes through a side of the cube for the first time on a turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 6th.

#### Wind of Death 1.1.1
*5th-level Necromancy (Cleric)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (A owl feather)
- **Duration:**  Instantaneous
___

You release unholy wind in a 60-foot-long, 15-foot-wide line in a direction of your choice. Each creature within the line must make a Constitution saving throw. A creature takes 5d6 Cold damage and 5d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save, and half as much on a success.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Cold damage and the Necrotic damage increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.

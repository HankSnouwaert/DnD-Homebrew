## Crossbowmen
*Soldiers Armed With Heavy Ranged Weaponry*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

Crossbows are among the most potent ranged weapons, many requiring specialized draw devices to pull back their draw strings. Such meticulous preparation yields high-velocity projectiles that collide with devastating effect.

### Crossbowmen  River Patrol
Within the murky depths of vast river bends, slime-covered lids slide back to reveal hungry eyes fixed on the silhouettes moving atop the glimmering surface above. Such shadows are cast by merchant ships laden with opulence, their curved metal and crystalline faces catching the light. Adding to the temptation are the merchants and sailors scrambling across the decks: soft creatures bearing no talons, horns, or thick scales to protect the wealth upon their persons.

Such vessels are beacons to the waterway's inhabitants, who gather in great schools to hollow out the bounty aboard, webbed claws and slithering tentacles hauling them up the hulls to tear through the stores within, before returning to their aquatic lairs with gem, metal, and bone clutched in viscous appendages to bury in the mud below.

This would be the outcome if not for the River Patrol, which keeps watch over such vessels and unleashes its wrath upon any wretches that rise with intent to plunder. Bolts greet the snarling maws of muculent forms breaching the waves, shafts piercing rubbery skin and splitting cartilage, sending lifeless corpses drifting back into the abyss trailing dark ichor. When the amphibious marauders grow numerous enough to weather the barrage, any beast fortunate enough to haul itself free of the water finds its entrance greeted by a steel-toed boot crashing against its unctuous form, sending it hurtling back into the depths, usually with a fresh iron-tipped bolt not far behind.

### Crossbowmen Bulwark
Among the most dangerous warriors to bear bolt-launching implements are those who use the weapon in tandem with massive, towering shields known as pavises: sheets of wood adhered together, covered in leather and canvas, reaching nearly the height of the wielder and covering the whole of the user's body. The warrior is able to move forward as enemy fire falls in vain, arrows and enemy bolts sticking into the handheld wall. Once close enough to the desired quarry, the marksman drives the shield into the ground, a great spike at the bottom being driven deep into the earth, turning the pavise into a full-fledged barricade. From behind the cover of this makeshift rampart, these warriors draw forth and load massive crossbows.

\columnbreak

They only peer out from above the crest, using the shield to steady the massive contraption as they take aim and smite oncoming combatants. The men who implement such tactics are known as Bulwarks and have changed the course of wars; many mercenary groups and armies possess them, forever carving their names into history.

#### Bulwark Tactics
Bulwarks work well alongside other melee fighters to keep foes occupied. They try to hold their position, forcing enemies to close the distance. If an enemy reaches melee range, the Bulwark uses the Dodge action to retreat. When multiple Bulwarks fight together, they should scatter so opponents must chase one while the others fire from range.

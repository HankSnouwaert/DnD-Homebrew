## Swordsmen
*Frontline Soldiers & Warrior Duelists*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Swordsmen go into battle with blade and shield in hand. Such warriors hold the front lines effectively, keeping enemy opponents at bay while polearms and arrows finish off those locked in place.

### Militants
Militants stride forth to bring about divine justice. These men have pledged themselves to a god or holy mission and serve with fervent devotion. Though they possess no divine magic like paladins or clerics, their faith gives them the courage to stand against even the most disturbing and powerful foes without succumbing to the fear that would seize the hearts of those who lack such resolve. These fanatical followers also fight with forthright ferocity, rushing headlong into combat and striking without regard for their own well-being. Their only purpose is the greater cause they serve, even if that cause demands their death.

#### Militant Tactics
Militants use their Frenzied Longsword under the following circumstances:
* If the opponent would already have advantage on attacks against the Militant.
* To draw an opponent’s focus onto the Militant. This is especially effective when another Militant is near death. The opponent must then choose whether to finish off the wounded Militant (and take a large amount of damage) or leave the wounded Militant alive, allowing it to keep fighting while the opponent is forced to deal with the vulnerable Militant.

### Wolf Warriors
Rare are the Wolf Warriors, devoted to the White Wolf, god of winter and war. These regiments drape themselves in pelts of alabaster fur over battered and worn armor. They grip blades engraved with savage beasts upon brass hilts, and emblems of snarling wolves mark their shields. When loosed upon the fields of war, these men emulate the hunting techniques of primal canids, working in savage unity to bring down their foes.
Most infamous is the moment these regiments join together and unleash a chorus of hunting war-cries akin to the howls of wild lupines. 

\columnbreak

<br>

Delivered with such devotion and doggedness, the cries often cause foes to turn tail and retreat before ever locking weapons with the daunting pack of soldiers.


#### Wolf Warrior Tactics
Wolf Warriors use the same tactics as Militants, except they use their Frenzied Longsword in combination with Battle Howl. The disadvantage from being Frightened cancels out the advantage granted by their attack. It is also important for them to keep other allies alive so that their Pack Tactics feature remains active.

> ***Battle Howl (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The swordsman lets out a terrifying howl. Each other Wolf Warrior Swordsman in a 90-foot emanation that is not Incapacitated may expend a use of this ability to bolster the howl. Each hostile creature in the emanation must then make a *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 10 + 2 (Max of 20) for each other swordsman that contributed to the howl. <br>
> *Failure:* The target is Frightened of the swordsman and each other swordsman that contributed to the howl for the next minute. It can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. <br>
> *Failure by 5+:* While Frightened, the creature can't cast spells other than cantrips, can't concentrate, and can make only one attack if it takes the Attack action.

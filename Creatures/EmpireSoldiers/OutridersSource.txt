## Outriders
*Veteran Marksmen Armed With The Finest Blackpowder Weaponry*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Outriders are imposing men of high military standing who have climbed their way from novitiate rank-and-file soldiers to commanders and drill sergeants. They are accomplished in both horsemanship and firearms, with a knack for refining young recruits into an efficient fighting force. As such, many noble families pay a hefty sum to these masters so that their vivacious sons may acquire exemplary military training and so that their youthful fire is tempered before it drives them to an early end.

Outriders therefore acquire the finest armor and weapons with which to swathe themselves. They also favor well-coiffed hair and carefully groomed mustaches to distinguish themselves from lower-ranking officers; such finery only heightens the aura of command that a veteran outrider exudes.

Many times outriders lead groups of pistoleers to keep them from acting foolishly, getting themselves killed, or jeopardizing the mission. At other times an entire company of these weathered virtuosos rides together to show the younger lads what a true cavalry unit can accomplish. Outriders spare no expense on the firearms they carry, spending a small fortune on each weapon. Many favor repeater guns capable of loosing a storm of metal within seconds; others carry small hand-mortars with which they lob grenades that, upon landing, leave charred and bloody holes in enemy formations.

### Outrider Bordermen
At the edges of the greatest empires lie lands that have never been refined by the will of civilization or cleansed of the unknown. All manner of danger skulks in the undergrowth and rocky crevasses. The gentility of aristocracy and the proper edicts of the bustling metropolises lose their significance in the breath of such untamed domains. In these realms, those who know how to move through strenuous terrain and who are acquainted with the dangers it conceals are held in the highest esteem.

Outriders sent to these barbaric corners must quickly adapt or lose both their high standing and their lives. Those who not only survive but thrive become hardy souls, bred apart from their polished kin and able to tame the land with steel and powder. Such troopers gallop across the most challenging topography atop robust mounts without slowing, weaving past every danger while their adversaries slash and stab vainly at empty air. All the while the outriders load their weapons with ease, giving the cretins who pursue them a proper parting gift before riding on into the wilderness.

It is said that in the most intense conflicts, when battle flows, and a wild tempo fills the minds of the outriders, driving them to fervor, as the violence escalates, their aim becomes infallible and every bullet brings devastation. Hooves thunder, guns scream, and the burning hearts of these brazen men beat ever faster until a brutal crescendo reaches its peak: a song powerful enough to bring even the most savage lands to heel.

#### Outriders & Outrider Bordermen Tactics
Outriders weave in and out of combat, delivering devastating attacks before riding away to reload. Those armed with repeater guns prioritize low-AC enemies. Those with grenade launchers focus on clusters of low-Dexterity targets. Whenever possible, outriders end their turn adjacent to an ally so they gain advantage on Wisdom saving throws.

### Onyx Outriders
When the magi who have mastered the macabre winds of death require champions capable of eradicating the most vile and resilient warriors and beasts, they hire outriders, paying them such vast sums of gems and metal as they could never hope to earn by any other means, and offering weapons infused with power beyond anything science could produce.

Even so, the majority of the most talented marksmen turn away from these bounties. Rumors and stories of service to such grim wizards are told throughout the realms: soldiers who pledge themselves to this duty become mirthless and cold, for joy seems to refuse any part in such a venture. The few who are foolish, or already cold-blooded enough to accept, soon find themselves swathed in macabre shells of ebon steel crowned with great skull helms and given all manner of death-laced contraptions to aid them in their duty, along with dark steeds to enact their masters’ bidding.

As the riders close on their victims, they draw ghastly firearms. Some carry repeaters that loose rounds gleaming with violet flame; the bullets cackle horribly as they rush through the air. When these ghoulish projectiles find their mark, the wounds they leave blacken, and a terror seeps into the host, growing until it drowns every other thought. Others carry grenades of amethyst that burst with howls capable of shattering the courage of all who hear them.

Yet what has made these men infamous is the fate of those who withstand the psychic punishment and do not die of pure fright. For such outriders then charge forward with their blades. With the momentum of the assault, the hexed weapons plunge deep into the bewildered opponent’s flesh, slicing into the very soul. 

\columnbreak

The life-force is hacked away and siphoned into the outrider, imparting a vigor that allows him to endure every manner of punishment that would fell a normal mortal.


#### Onyx Outrider Tactics
Onyx outriders charge into melee against frightened targets and remain engaged to impose disadvantage on the target’s saving throws, helping maintain the frightened condition. When multiple onyx outriders are present, one can enter melee to impose disadvantage on saving throws triggered by the rest of the group.

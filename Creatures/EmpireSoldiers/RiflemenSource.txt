## Riflemen
*Marksmen Able To Snipe Foes From Afar With Long Rifles*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

Among the most advanced and deadly handheld weapons ever developed, long rifles can deliver skull-shattering shots capable of inflicting lethal wounds at astonishing distances. Each pull of the trigger ends a life, and many have heard the crack of thunder from a distant hillside only to find a limb shattered into blood and gore. The firearms themselves feature long, thin barrels that make reloading difficult; as a result, the marksmen chosen to carry these lethal tools train diligently to load their weapons with maximum efficiency. 

### The Jaegerkorps

Jaegerkorps are elite strike forces designed to eliminate back-line commanders in the heat of combat. They can strike a songbird from two hundred yards away and slip through entire regiments of hostile forces without anyone realizing their doom is approaching. Finding the best vantage point, these agents hunker down and carefully scan the battlefield to determine where their fire will most destabilize the opposing force. At a frantic rate, men are cut down, and commanders and sergeants drop with gaping chunks of their heads removed in the blink of an eye. Chaos floods through the ranks of the fallen leaders’ subordinates, who cannot discern where the death originated, hearing only the booming roar of gunfire ringing out like mocking laughter.

#### Jaegerkorps Rifleman Tactics

The rifleman first tries to hide from their opponent (if they are not already hidden). They then find a secure vantage point, using black powder discipline to fire each turn.

### Mage Striker Rifleman

Few forces can tip the scales of battle so completely in an army’s favor as a spellcaster raining far devastation down on the fields of war. Entire regiments are turned into clouds of billowing ash by a fireball, or a charge of thundering knights is mired by a sea of entangling roots called forth from the ground. Many believe such potent figures can only be countered by other wielders of the arcane. Yet there are warriors, masters of mundane weaponry, who have found a way to push back against this oppressive power through the fruits of technology.

Using long rifles, these snipers have brought doom even to the most skilled of spellcasters. They understand the gestures and phrases required to weave enchantments and invocations, and they know precisely at which point during the process a disturbance will cause the most turbulence. An incantation that would make the land buckle and break, or cause flames to rain from the sky, can be halted by a well-placed shot, disrupting the magic, damping its effects, or negating the devastation entirely. Even the most dreadful beings, liches and Sload Mage-Priests, have been interrupted while preparing to utter words of primal power that would dissolve reality itself.

> ### Reactions
>
> ***Disrupt Spell.*** *Trigger:* A creature the rifleman can see within 600 feet casts a spell with Verbal, Somatic, or Material components while its weapon is loaded. <br>
> *Response:* The rifleman makes an attack against the target. On a hit, the creature must make a *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. <br>
> *Failure:* The spell is dampened. The caster can still choose to cast the spell, but it becomes weakened, and until the end of the rifleman's next turn: attack rolls made by the spell have disadvantage, targets have advantage on saving throws made to resist the spell, and if the spell allows a creature to make a saving throw to take only half damage, a successful save causes no damage and a failed save causes half damage instead. If the caster chooses not to cast the spell, the action, Bonus Action, or reaction used to cast it is wasted, but the spell slot is not expended. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* The spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot or daily use, the slot or use is expended.

## Captains and Generals
*TBD*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
It is not enough to merely have a sea of armed warriors united under one cause to fashion an army. Such rabble must be guided to amplify the effectiveness of each soldier so that the whole becomes more than the sum of its parts, with each Man-at-arms sorted to where their unique skills will deliver the most potent effect. Overseeing such an undertaking and keeping the masses in unison with one another are the captains. The greatest of these men send out orders to subordinates with as much control as a man might swing his arm to swat a fly. 


Such figures of authority rise up from all walks of life to take up this eminent and demanding role. Many are nobility, steeped in martial pursuits from a young age, mastering the art of the blade and tutored in a variety of tactics. Others started out with the rest of the rank and file as common troopers, only to crawl their way up the ladder of command through serving in countless battles, performing acts of valor, and showing an aptitude for promoting loyalty in their companions.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Hold the Line! (2/Day).*** While the Captain is not Incapacitated, when the Captain or an allied creature within 30 feet of him that can hear him makes a saving throw, that creature and each other allied creature also making the saving throw against the same effect have advantage. >
> ***Maneuver Allies (Recharges when Initiative is Rolled).*** The Captain and up to six other allies within 60 feet of him that can hear him can each use their reaction to move up to half their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Battle Command.*** The Captain targets one ally he can see within 30 feet of him. If the target can see or hear the Captain, the target can use its reaction to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. It can then make a single attack.

When on the battlefield, such men are as birds taking to the air—deftly maneuvering through the tides of chaos and death, directing regiments through the turbulence to crash against foes where they are most vulnerable. Most leaders fight from the front line, shoulder to shoulder where the conflict is most vigorous, dispatching foes with precision strikes all the while counting his duties of directing those around him. Such presence strengthens the allegiances of his allies so they are able to endure even as all hope of victory seems to have slipped away. So powerful is this bond that the howl form of a leader standing one’s ground when bombarded by all manner of deadly projectiles can be the difference between a battle line breaking or such walls withstanding the punishment.

### Captains and Generals Tactics
Captains and Generals should always be paired with at least 6 other creatures for full effect. The breadth of their tactics will be determined by allies bolstering them with extreme attacks and allowing them to reposition allies where they need to go.

## Hans Von Löwenhacke
*Grizzled General & Master Tactician of the Empire*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

No other commander is able to orchestrate the armies of man with militaristic efficiency as General Von Löwenhacke. Even in his youth, Hans’s aptitude for leadership was clear when he joined the ranks of the imperial forces of his homeland. His mind could fabricate the most complex strategies for the conflicts his soldiers would face, yet also adapt intricate tactics when unexpected factors arose amid the turbulence of war. His voice is infused with such thunderous bravado that any underling receiving an order from that reverberating roar will carry out the instruction without a hint of hesitation. 

Von Löwenhacke has become a legend after the countless victories he has accumulated against seemingly superior forces. Beyond his ability to coordinate his comrades, Hans is an exquisite fighter; with his massive warhammer he is able to perform all varieties of precision maneuvers, sending foes flying into his allies’ gleaming weapons, delivering blows that leave enemies bewildered, or throwing such an assailant off balance, leading to a misstep that exposes them to further assaults from the guileful leader and the rest of his devoted warriors.

### Hans as a Boss Battle
Hans works best when paired with his minions, who are made to work in tandem with him. Each one he has increases his CR by 2.

### Von Löwenhacke Tactics
Von Löwenhacke is flexible in his tactics and how he approaches an opponent. Each of his minions is used in the following ways:

* **Archers** are used as damage dealers. They help keep opponents from removing stacks of Assessment, so they should prioritize those targets.

* **Swordsmen** are used to help keep Von Löwenhacke alive by remaining within 5 feet of him.

* **Pikemen** are used to lock down targets, especially in combination with Von Löwenhacke’s Push.

#### Versatile Hammer 
This attack can be used in a variety of ways, mostly attacking targets that have low saving throws against the chosen effect. Here are a few combos to consider when focusing on a single target:

* **Bash and Push** — Best when a Pikeman is lined up to lock down a highly mobile target (such as a rogue or monk).

* **Expose and Bash** — Good if the target has low Dexterity and you want to prevent them from escaping, especially if an Archer is Quickened.

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br>

* **Expose and Push** — Useful if the Hans is low on health but has a large number of minions ready to attack.

#### Tactical  Actions
##### Bolster 
Best used on targets that are close to death to keep them alive. Otherwise, use it on Archers so they can multiply pressure on targets that have high levels of Assessment.

##### Coordinated Strike
Can be used both to reposition allies and to grant them extra attacks. Common uses include:
* Moving a Swordsman back within 5 feet of Von Löwenhacke.
* Moving a Pikeman into position to impale a target, or to drag an already impaled target toward or away from allies.
* Having an Archer retreat from melee, or keeping as many targets as possible from removing stacks of Assessment.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Dazed 
> A Dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Quickened
> A Quickened creature gains an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack or unarmed strike only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action. The *Haste* spell applies the Quickened condition among its other effects, and a creature cannot benefit from the Quickened condition from multiple sources at once.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Hold the Line! (3/Day).*** While Von Löwenhacke is not Incapacitated, when he or an allied creature within 30 feet of him that can hear him makes a saving throw, that creature and each other allied creature also making the saving throw against the same effect have advantage. If the effect allows a creature to take only half damage on a success, it instead takes no damage on a success and only half damage on a failure. Additionally, if damage from the effect would reduce a creature to 0 hit points, it drops to 1 hit point instead.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** Von Löwenhacke makes two attacks.
>
> ***Versatile Hammer.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (4d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. Von Löwenhacke chooses one of the following effects that he has not chosen yet this turn. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;***Push.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 16. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Failure:* The target is pushed up to 20 feet. If it is pushed into an allied creature, that creature can use its reaction to make an attack against the target. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;***Bash.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Failure:* The target loose connection and is Dazed* until the start of Von Löwenhacke's next turn. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;***Expose.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Failure:* The target gains three levels of Assessment. Assessment lasts one minute or until Von Löwenhacke dies. Whenever Von Löwenhacke or one of his allies hits a creature with an attack, they deal an extra 2 (1d4) damage for each level of Assessment the target has. A creature loses all Assessment if it takes the Dodge or Disengage action.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Strategic Assessment.*** Von Löwenhacke chooses up to five creatures he can see within 120 feet of him. Each creature must make an *Intelligence or Charisma Saving Throw (its choice):* DC 16. <br>
> *Failure:* The target gains one level of Assessment.
>
> ### Tactical Actions
>
> Von Löwenhacke gains two additional actions in combat: Bolster and Coordinated Strike. Each action can be performed on its own initiative count, separate from Von Löwenhacke's. On the action's initiative count (losing ties), Von Löwenhacke can take the action if he is not Incapacitated. He can replace either action with Tenacious Will, even if Incapacitated.
>
> ***Bolster (Initiative Count 21).*** Von Löwenhacke chooses another creature he can see within 60 feet to gain 20 temporary hit points until Initiative Count 20 of the next round. While it has these temporary hit points, it is Quickened*.
>
> ***Coordinated Strike (Initiative Count 5).*** Von Löwenhacke commands another creature that is not Incapacitated to move up to half it's movment feet without provoking opportunity attacks. It can then make a single attack.
>
> ***Tenacious Will.*** If Von Löwenhacke is subject to an ongoing effect that allows a saving throw as an action or at the start or end of his turn, he can immediately make that saving throw with advantage, ending the effect on a success.

## Greatswordsmen
*Elite Soldiers Wielding*<br>
*MightyTwo-Handed Swords*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

Greatswords are mighty weapons, typically <br> wielded only by elite infantry. These soldiers <br> engage the deadliest and toughest foes, using <br> their heavy weapons to cleave through enemies' <br> defenses. They eschew shields, relying instead on <br> the overwhelming power of their greatswords to cut <br> down their opponents before they can counterattack.

### Doppelsöldner
The mightiest of soldiers grip swords nearly the length of a full-grown man, with massive blades of steel. They station themselves at the head of the army to barrel into the front of enemy formations, clearing a path so their allies can plunge into the foes and strike. Those who have refined this technique are known as Doppelsöldner, or “Double-Paid,” as the skill required and the hazardous nature of their role mean commanders must pay a hefty sum to acquire such fighters.


#### Tactics – Doppelsöldner
Doppelsöldner can be used to protect against high-reach attacks such as polearms and *Thorn Whip*. They can also lock down mobile targets like rogues and monks.


### Crimson Greatswordsmen
The Crimson Greatswordsmen are a rare breed that possess a particularly potent lust for violence. They have gained their grisly title from the fact that once their blades cut deep, crimson coats both the swordsman and his adversaries. As fever seizes the gore-caked soldier, they sweep their massive blade with ever-growing power, and a vehemence courses through every fiber of their being, allowing body and mind to withstand the most brutal punishments.

#### Tactics – Crimson Greatswordsmen

Crimson Greatswordsmen should prioritize the lowest-AC target to trigger Bathed in Blood.

### Greatsword Cleaver
Late at night in boisterous taverns, countless tales are told that grow more grandiose with every retelling from one loquacious bard to the next. A common thread in these vainglorious stories is the heroes and dogs of war who wield colossal blades against ogres, manticores, and other remorseless brutes. The valiant figure charges forward and brings down their steel, slicing the monster cleanly in two as its halves topple. Such fables are mocked or enjoyed as pure entertainment, since no ordinary mortal could ever achieve such a feat.

#### Tactics – Greatsword Cleaver
Greatsword Cleavers prefer low-AC targets. They can also use their charge to reach the back line or targets with low Strength.

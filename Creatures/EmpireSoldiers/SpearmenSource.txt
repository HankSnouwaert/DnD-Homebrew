## Spearmen
*Loyal Guards & Foot Soldiers*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Spearmen are among the most common soldiers found standing watch over villages and making up the bulk of an imperial army. They grip a polearm in one hand and a shield in the other.

### Spearmen Sentinels
Those spearmen most skilled at keeping watch and intercepting foes who try to bypass them and strike at other vulnerable targets are known as Sentinels. Many serve as security for nobles and other figures of high standing. In war they are charged with ensuring that artillery crews or spellcasters are not disrupted by enemy forces.

### Spearmen Valorguard
There are few regiments of foot troops that pose a greater threat to cavalry than the Valorguard. These specialized warriors have been trained to brace against the most powerful charging opponents and break their momentum. Greater still is the iron will of such masters: those behind their barricade of steel and determination are filled with courage and will not falter even as the most hideous of foes barrel toward them.

It is said that when a great invasion of beastmen emerged from the deep woods to consume the city of Soithune, it was a group of these warriors who formed a living wall between the rest of Soithune’s army and the herd of minotaurs at the head of the savage force.

### Spearmen Tactics
Spearmen Sentinels and Spearmen Valorguards perform best when they place themselves between opponents and more vulnerable ranged targets, using their halting strikes to keep enemies from advancing.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Charge Defense.*** If a creature moved 10 or more feet straight toward the Spearman immediately before making an attack against it while the Spearman is not Incapacitated, Prone, Grappled, or Restrained, the attack has disadvantage. The Spearman has Resistance to any damage dealt by the attack and advantage on any saving throws it is forced to make by the attack.
>
> ***Inspiring Presence.*** The Spearman emits an aura in a 10-foot emanation from itself while not Incapacitated. The Spearman and each allied creature in the aura have advantage on Wisdom saving throws.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Halting Spear.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (3d6 + 4) Piercing damage. If the attack was an opportunity attack, the creature's speed becomes 0 until the end of the turn.
>
> ### Reactions
>
> ***Guardian.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 5 feet of the Spearman takes the Disengage action or hits a target other than the Spearman with an attack. <br>
> *Response:* The Spearman can make an opportunity attack against that creature.
>
> ***Charge Reflection.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 5 feet of the Spearman moved 10 or more feet straight toward it immediately before making an attack against it. <br>
> *Response:* The Spearman can make an opportunity attack against that creature with advantage.

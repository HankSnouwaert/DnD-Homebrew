## Irregular Militia
*Ragtag Soldiers Operating Outside Usual Military Structures*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

In empires ravaged by all manner of marauding armies that perpetually attempt to tear down and consume all that they have built, the forces that defend such provinces must be laden with as many able bodies as possible to drive off these rapacious hordes. In dire times, many of these troops are not part of any formal military but drafted or willing conscripts gathered from the region, local residents finding what weapons they can to protect their homes and loved ones against the oncoming evil. Others are bandits, mercenaries, or other outlaws pressed into service through threat or the promise of coin, whether from government coffers or by picking clean the corpses of their enemies for payment. Most informal footsoldiers are commonly known as Irregulars. 

Many nobles and commanders are eager to take such lawless men into their ranks, as it allows them to keep these unruly lots under some semblance of control. Callous commanders also see it as a convenient opportunity to remove troublemakers from their lands, for it is not uncommon to assign such willful fighters to the most dangerous tasks so that military funds are not wasted on these uncouth soldiers.

### Vengeful Militia

Some warriors do not join a cause out of potent avarice for silver or gold, nor from a higher calling to protect the weak or maintain law in the land. Instead, they are driven by a flame kindled by vengeance. Many cities and hamlets have been scoured from the land by the clawed limbs of furious beasts or the axes and swords of merciless scum. Yet from the rubble of such ruins, those few who have persisted through tragedy rise transformed; all that they once were left broken as wreckage beneath their feet, with only their hate left to sustain what remains of them. 

These vengeful militiamen travel far and wide in small bands, seeking to eradicate any person or creature even remotely connected to those responsible for destroying their homes. Once they have caught wind of its target, fury begins to boil in their blood as they rush forward, unleashing a hail of bullets before hacking their foes apart with decisively brutal strikes.

### Veteran Militia
Many freelancers set out across the land to gather a fortune that no commoner could hope to acquire, aiming to amass such a stockpile through the application of steel and black powder. Such undertakings rarely result in the mountains of wealth one would hope for; these endeavors are usually halted by a well-placed swipe of a behemoth’s clawed appendage, being surrounded by a gaggle of spiteful goblins with jagged implements, or some other deadly encounter.

> ##### New Condition: Fury  
> The creature gains levels of Fury that cause its attacks to deal increasing amounts of damage with each level. 
> 
> **Gaining Fury.** Unless an ability specifies otherwise, a creature can gain up to 6 levels of Fury and only during combat. <Br>
> &emsp;**Extra Damage.** The first time each turn that the creature hits a target with an attack, it deals an additional 1d4 damage for each level of Fury it has.<br>
> &emsp;**Losing Fury.** All levels of Fury are lost if a creature goes 1 minute without gaining any levels of Fury. Additionally, a creature loses all levels of Fury if it becomes Charmed or Frightened, or is affected by the *Calm Emotions* spell. It also can't gain levels Fury while under these conditions.

### Irregular Militia Tactics

Militiamen continue advancing to engage a target in melee after firing their pistol, unless hazards or obstacles block their path. In which case, they seek cover and continue firing from afar. 

#### Veteran Tactics
When veterans become bloodied, they switch tactics: they use Adrenaline Rush to disengage to a safe distance, then resume using their pistol.

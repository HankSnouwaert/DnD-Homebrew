## Archers
*Classic Ranged Soldiers & Marksmen*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Archers are among the most common type of soldier, with regiments observing the heavens as volleys of arrows streak toward the earth, raining down upon enemy formations.

### Deathjacks
Though woodland paths and city alleyways may conceal shadowed figures that stride noiselessly through the landscape, few ever notice their presence until arrows spring from the darkness and lodge themselves into the unsuspecting victims.

Such masterful feats are performed by archers known as Deathjacks. These cunning marksmen serve as scouts and assassins for armies, able to approach enemy encampments and regiments without a soul catching wind of their presence. They can even slink into the heart of hostile territory to strike vulnerable targets, then vanish without consequence as they silently retreat, leaving chaos in their wake. As fallen soldiers' comrades search bewilderedly for their murderers, those responsible are already long gone.

#### DeathJacks Tactics
**Target the least perceptive creature.** The Deathjacks should attack the enemy they believe is least likely to notice them, reducing the chance of being discovered.

**Avoid prolonged engagements.** If they are discovered, the Deathjacks should disengage.

### Order of the Bow
Some archers view their craft as more than merely the mastery of a deadly weapon. To them, archery is a meditative experience, a pursuit of tranquility in which there is no greater calm than drawing one's bowstring. As the string is pulled taut, the mind becomes free of all burdens, leaving only the archer and their mark. When the arrow is finally loosed, it flies straight and true.

Those who embrace this meditative philosophy often form guilds and monasteries where they hone their techniques alongside like-minded warriors. These organizations are commonly known as the Order of the Bow.

Such sages are forces to be reckoned with. Their mastery allows them to launch projectiles with such precision that even the protections of their enemies offer little refuge. They can pin foes from leagues away, and even those shrouded by foliage, rubble, or debris find little safety from their watchful eyes.

More impressive still are the archers' abilities when their enemies close the distance. Their aim is so precise that they can place an arrow into the most lethal locations, slipping shafts through the narrowest gaps in armor or striking even the most agile and reflexive of assailants.

#### Order of the Bow Tactics
**Force enemies out of cover.** When positioned safely behind cover, the archer can pressure enemies into advancing toward them. Enemies may struggle to find a safe position from which to retaliate.

**Pair with melee units.** This archer works particularly well alongside melee units. As enemies advance to close the distance, melee allies can intercept them.

**Maintain a 30-foot distance.** If enemies manage to close in, the archer should prioritize maintaining roughly 30 feet of distance.

**Let melee units protect them.** If an enemy continues closing the gap, melee allies should engage them before they can reach the archers, allowing the archer to continue firing from a safer position.

## Pistoleers
*Gunslinging Sharpshooters Armed With a Brace of Pistols*<br>
**Habitat:** Urban; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
Armed with a bandolier of pistols across their chest or waist, pistoleers must be exceptionally swift to make the most of such armaments. These warriors fire, discard, and draw another firearm in rapid succession, repeating the process without error. Because pistols lack the range of many other firearms, they are forced to fight closer to the heat of combat rather than safer distances that other marksmen enjoy. Only the boldest and most reckless embrace this strategy, and most are hot-headed fighters eager to rush into danger at any opportunity to display their deadly skill.

### Noble Riders
Many young nobles are too inexperienced or lack the pedigree required for acceptance into the knightly orders scattered throughout the kingdoms. When these highborn warriors cannot join such prestigious ranks, they use their family fortunes to purchase pistols, armor, and the finest stallions, then band together with like-minded rabble-rousers to form regiments known as Noble Riders. These fighters are well trained in both marksmanship and horsemanship. As fast cavalry, they race past enemy flanks, pouring fire into the foe as they pass and striking from every angle.

### Quick Draws
The most grizzled and battle-worn pistoleers are known as Quick Draws. These grim figures travel alone or in small groups, usually as mercenaries or guns-for-hire. They earn their title from the blistering speed with which they can empty their sidearms, as these veterans can unleash a volley of pistol fire as fast as a bolt of lightning. In the blink of an opponent’s eye, multiple rounds have already been fired with such accuracy that targets are disarmed, brought to their knees, or left dead. Many have become local folk heroes, with bards singing of wandering gunmen who ride into towns plagued by bandits or corrupt officials and deliver justice with iron and powder.

### Pistoleer Tactics
As mid-range fighters, they move in, take a shot, then fall back, aiming to end their turn roughly 40 feet from the enemy.

#### Noble Riders
Most effective on fast mounts that are not built for melee (e.g., riding horses). They weave in and out of combat, strike vulnerable targets, then have their mounts dash to a safe distance.

#### Quick Draws
Specialize in eliminating other fragile long-range attackers.
When using Precision Shot, they prioritize their target based on the following circumstances: 
* Arm – Used against martial targets. Most effective when an ally is close enough to seize the dropped item and keep it away from the enemy permanently. Also useful for freeing allies from enemy grapplers.
* Leg – When the target is trying to close the gap and enter melee reach.
* Chest / Head – When neither of the above situations applies.

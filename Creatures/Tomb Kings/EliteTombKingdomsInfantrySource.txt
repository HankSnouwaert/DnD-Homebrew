## The Eternal Legion

Legends speak that the most devoted and formidable of regiments among the armies of ancient dynasties devoted everything to their rulers, many using ceremonial daggers to split their palms and let blood flow to fuel mystic oaths made in front of their sovereign and the idols of numerous gods of the underworld - that body and soul belong to their kingdom, and that not even the hand of death may drag them away from their duties. Such promises have outlasted the empires for which they served. Rising from the grave to fulfill their vows, the gods grant them potent wards that repel the most powerful of spells that would attempt to quell such devotion. Though most dangerous is the resilience of such faithful servants, as each soldier is bound to one another: when one is smashed, his brother in arms will beckon to the soul, commanding it to return to the discarded remains, for such obligations are not so easily abandoned.

### Eternal Legion Tactics

Eternal Legion Spearmen are far less vulnerable to area-of-effect damage thanks to their resistance to spell damage. When paired with creatures such as a High Tomb Priest, they become nearly impossible to remove from the battle.

## Asp Legion Archers

The greatest archers in the bygone kingdoms pledged themselves to the goddess of the asp in hopes that they might gain her notice and be granted her blessing. Those who were seen as worthy in the deity’s slitted eyes were imbued with the lightning reflexes of a striking serpent, allowing them to weave and twist out of the path of streaking projectiles. Though such instincts are little when compared to the greatest of boons such a deity can impart, as the goddess’s formula to craft divine venoms is among the most coveted secrets her acolytes strive for. Those entrusted with such baneful knowledge coat their arrows with the deific toxins, each a lethal kiss from their god set forth to deliver her wrath. In death, such followers have not lost any of their lethality, able to sap the vitality of the hardiest heroes with a single shot.

### Asp Legion Archer Tactics

Asp Legion Archers focus on targets that lack resistance to Poison and have low Constitution.

## Skeletal Horseman of the Sun

It was said the greatest regiment commanded by Setara the Eternal was the Legion of the Sun, armed with shields of glimmering brass and spears that blazed with solar heat. As such scions were carried with haste toward the enemy front line on golden war carts, glaring rays of the sun would reflect off the surface of the aegises, engulfing their foes in gleaming white light, holy and deadly, as the torrid points of the horsemen’s weapons plunged deep and let loose effulgent flames sweep over their forms. When Setara rose once more to continue his campaign, so too did his most elite regiment, their weapons untarnished by the passing of the ages, still suffused with the brilliant power of the heavens.

### Skeletal Horseman of the Sun Tactics

The Skeletal Horseman prioritizes moving between targets to blind and burn them. It uses its Gleaming Shield to blind a chosen target, then charges that target. Once a target is set on fire, the Horseman exploits the light from the flames to blind a new target and charge it.

\columnbreak

##### New Condition: Burning
The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid.
 * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
 * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
 * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

##### New Condition: Dazed 
 A dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

## Tomb Warrior Awakeners

Numerous are the curses woven and draped onto the blades of undead warriors within the murk of temples beneath the sands, but few match the guileful efficiency of those weapons that carry the awakener’s curse. For not only can a swipe of the wicked artifact blacken flesh and turn bone to dust, but such an amplified implement is able to spend its corruption on other weapons, imbuing them with unnatural life as they lift into the air, shimmering with dark power and seeking to strike with their newfound deathly enhancement, usually assaulting the former owner of the freshly tainted weapon. Such potent enchantments come at great cost to the wielder, for the virulent and corrupting nature that drives such an incantation latches onto the soul of those who use it, so that in death such weapons will not be denied, using what is left of the host’s essence to rise so the bloodshed they inflict may persist.

### Tomb Warrior Awakener Tactics

Tomb Warrior Awakeners prioritize targets wielding bludgeoning weapons and any who attempt to remove the curse from those weapons.

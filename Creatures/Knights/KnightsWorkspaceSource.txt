## Knightly Actions

If you wish to make your knight a true solo threat, you may grant it an extra Knightly Action it can use each round of combat, increasing its CR by 1.

This grants the knight an additional action in combat, taken on Initiative Count 21 (losing ties), separate from the knight’s normal initiative. On that count, the knight can take one of the following actions, provided it is not Incapacitated; it may still use Tenacious Will even while Incapacitated.

You may choose to grant the knight additional Knightly Actions on Initiative Count 15 and Initiative Count 0 (losing ties). For each additional action granted this way, the knight’s challenge rating increases by 1.

**Charge.**  The knight, or controlled mount it is riding, moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The knight can make a melee attack with its lance at any point during this movement with the following properties: *Melee Weapon Attack:* Lance attack bonus to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Piercing damage.

**Tenacious Will.**  If the knight is subject to an ongoing effect that allows a saving throw at the start or end of its turn (or as an action), it can immediately make that saving throw with advantage. On a success, the effect ends.

## Knightly Virtues

Any knight can be given any of the following Knightly Virtues. Each one granted increases its CR by 1 unless otherwise stated. If a Knightly Virtue involves a saving throw, the DC is determined by the kight's rank, as described below:

| Knight Rank  | Virtue Save DC |
|:---:|:-----------:|
|  Knight Errant  | 12 |
|  Knight of the Realm  | 13 |
|  Questing Knight  | 15 |
|  Blessed Knight  | 16 |
|  Knight of Legend  | 17 |

### Virtue of Authority

*The word of the exalted king carried a supremacy none could ignore. From feral beasts to towering giants, all would kneel in terror and reverence.*

The knight gains the following Bonus Action: 

*Wisdom Saving Throw:* One creature the knight can see within 90 feet.

*Failure:* The creature falls Prone and is Frightened of the knight until the end of the knight’s next turn.  
*Failure by 5+:* The creature is Paralyzed until the end of the knight’s next turn.

### Virtue of Belligerence

*Many tales have been told of the rowdy warriors belonging to the Order of the Wild Boar, a boastful and truculent lot who cause a ruckus at any inn or keep in which they lodge. Such rabble-rousers barrel into the thick of violence, fueled by an arrogant confidence that drives them ever forward.*

When the knight rolls Initiative, it and one controlled mount it is riding become Belligerent for 1 minute.

While Belligerent, the first time each turn the knight or its mount hits with an attack, it deals an extra 13 (3d8) damage of the attack’s damage type.

The Belligerence ends early if the creature misses all attacks on its turn, becomes Charmed or Frightened, or is affected by *Calm Emotions*.

### Virtue of The Blazing Sun

*Holy was the saint of sacred flame and champion of the heavenly gods, master of both warfare and faith. Each blessed swing he landed against the heinous worshippers of false gods ignited them in seraphic fire.*

The first time each turn the knight hits a creature with a weapon attack, the target starts Burning. While burning, the creature takes 11 (2d10) Radiant damage at the end of each of its turns, sheds Bright Light in a 30-foot radius, attack rolls against it have advantage, and it can’t benefit from being Invisible.

### Virtue of Brutality

*The knights of the Pale Ox rode with warhammers in hand, barreling straight toward the dark orcs clad in black armor. The marauders stood ready to greet the oncoming cavalry with wicked axes. Upon collision, a great clamor rang across the field as each dark orc stood stunned, their helms dented and black blood dripping from their visors.*

The knight’s weapon attacks deal Bludgeoning damage instead of Slashing damage.

If the knight moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target immediately before hitting it with a weapon attack, the target must make a *Constitution Saving Throw*.

*Failure:* The creature is Dazed* until the end of its next turn. While Dazed, its Speed is halved, it can’t concentrate, and it can make only one attack if it takes the Attack action.  
*Failure by 5+:* The creature is Stunned until the end of its next turn.

> ##### New Condition: Dazed 
> A dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

### Virtue of The Commander

*Baldun had mastered many weapons through his long years of service, yet none did he wield with such precision and deadly effect as the loyal men who served under him.*

The knight gains the following Bonus Action: 

***Call to Battle.*** One ally within 30 feet of the knight that can see or hear it can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed without provoking opportunity attacks and then make one weapon attack.

*When the land has fallen to the wants and desires of iniquitous men, whose blasphemous acts have invited the tainted spawn of hell to walk the world, it is the righteous knight who leads their brothers to cleanse the realm of such perversion.*

The knight can cast *Aura of The Crusader* at 5th level using it's Charisma. Once cast in this way, the knight must finish a Short or Long Rest before doing so again.

### Virtue of Disdain

*It was said that the knight Hervé Courroux held a deep-seated loathing for those who refused to stand face to face with their opponent and instead chose to fell them from afar. So great was his hatred for such cowardly warfare that any foe who employed such tactics would soon feel the full measure of his wrath.*

The knight gains the following reaction:

***Disdainful Pursuit.***
*Trigger:* A creature 15 feet or more away damages the knight or its controlled mount.  
*Response:* The knight and its mount can each move up to their Speed toward the triggering creature without provoking opportunity attacks. The knight can then make one weapon attack with advantage against that creature. On a hit, the creature’s Speed becomes 0 until the end of the current turn.

### Virtue of Endurance

*Though the reptilian warrior unleashed a flurry of mace blows, each with enough force to kill a mortal, Iseult of the Rive did not falter. Ready for each incoming strike, she endured the brutal impacts with unyielding resolve.*

The knight has resistance to all damage from attacks unless the attacker has advantage on the attack roll.

An attacker doesn’t need advantage on the attack roll if at least one of the attacker’s allies is within 5 feet of the knight, the ally isn’t Incapacitated, and the attack roll doesn’t have disadvantage.

<bR>

> ##### New Condition: Fury  
> The creature gains levels of Fury that cause its attacks to deal increasing amounts of damage with each level. 
> 
> **Gaining Fury.** Unless an ability specifies otherwise, a creature can gain up to 6 levels of Fury and only during combat. <Br>
> &emsp;**Extra Damage.** The first time each turn that the creature hits a target with an attack, it deals an additional 1d4 damage for each level of Fury it has.<br>
> &emsp;**Losing Fury.** All levels of Fury are lost if a creature goes 1 minute without gaining any levels of Fury. Additionally, a creature loses all levels of Fury if it becomes Charmed or Frightened, or is affected by the *Calm Emotions* spell. It also can't gain levels Fury while under these conditions.

### Virtue of Fury

*Many who threatened the Tranquil Hills of the halflings would laugh when the knights of Great Goose marshaled to defend their farmland atop large armored fowl, only to feel the rancor behind each blow from the warriors’ blades and beaks.*

When the knight makes its first weapon attack on a turn, it and its mount gain 1d3 Fury.

If the knight moved at least 20 feet straight toward the target immediately before that attack, it and its mount instead gain 2d3 Fury.



### Virtue of Glory

*Great knights of the fabled kingdoms of the north dressed in gilded armor, with decorative wings of raptor feathers strapped to their backs and sashes fashioned from beast pelts wrapped across their chests. When such marvels charged, doubtless in their victory, angelic appendages fluttering in the wind, enemy lines abandoned their stations before such oncoming bravado.*

If the knight moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature immediately before hitting it with a lance attack, the target must make a *Wisdom Saving Throw*.

*Failure:* The creature is Frightened of the knight until the end of its next turn. While Frightened in this way, the creature can’t cast spells other than cantrips, can’t concentrate, and can make only one attack if it takes the Attack action.  
*Failure by 5+:* While Frightened, the creature can’t take Reactions or Bonus Actions and must use the Dash action on its turn to move away from the knight by the safest available route.

> ##### New Condition: Quickened
> A Quickened creature gains an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack or unarmed strike only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action. The *haste* spell applies the Quickened condition among its other effects, and a creature cannot benefit from the Quickened condition from multiple sources at once.

*To uphold a code of chivalry, a knight must accept another warrior’s invitation to single combat. Those who devote their entire being to such conduct can invoke divine law, compelling even the most iniquitous creature or sinner to stand before them and face the judgment of their blade.*

The knight can cast *Compelled Duel* as bouns action using its Charisma.

The knight can cast *Aura of Hope* using its Charisma. Once cast in this way, the knight must finish a Short or Long Rest before doing so again.

### Virtue of The Hunter

*The Templars of the Silver Scythe ride through the darkest woods and moors, venturing into remote villages where witches practice necromancy away from prying eyes and hide among the rural peasantry. Such acolytes of darkness cannot hide from warriors of zealous conviction; once accused, few escape the argent swords that deliver their final verdict.*

At the start of its turn, the knight marks one creature it can see within 90 feet for 1 hour.

The knight deals an extra 16 (3d10) damage when it hits the marked creature with a weapon attack for the frist time each turn.

The knight has advantage on Wisdom (Perception), Wisdom (Survival), and Wisdom (Insight) checks made to find or track the marked creature or tell if it lying.

Once the knight marks a creature in this way, it must finish a Short or Long Rest before doing so again.

### Virtue of The Jaculator

*Niall loosed his javelin, a furious messenger that streaked through the air, piercing cleanly through the wings of the vicious manticore. As the creature plummeted earthward, Niall drew his blade, the message made clear to the falling beast.*

The knight is equipped with javalins and gains the following Action: 

***Javalin.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 10 (3d6) Piercing damage. If the target is flying, it is knocked Prone.


### Virtue of The Joust

*Étienne Briselance’s greatest achievement was the slaying of a devil of the Black Grove, an elusive fiend that wove in and out of battle, felling its victims as they vainly attempted to conquer it. With a single strike, Étienne pinned the creature and made short work of the trapped monster.*

If the knight moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature immediately before hitting it with a lance attack, the target takes an extra 11 (2d10) Piercing damage.

If the target is Large or smaller, it is also impaled by the lance and Grappled (escape DC = the knight’s save DC). While Grappled in this way, the target is also Restrained and takes 11 (2d10) Piercing damage at the start of each of the knight’s turns.

The knight can’t Grapple another creature with its lance while this Grapple persists. The Grapple ends if the knight drops the lance.

*The ogre twins held an iron grip upon their bridge, demanding heavy taxes from any who wished to cross and throwing those who refused into the raging waters below. It was Duc Thibaut who rallied the militia of the nearby village to end the gouging. The heroes awakened a might within the ragged townsfolk they had never known; united, they overpowered the greedy brutes and sent them tumbling into the rapids to be dashed to pieces.*

The knight can cast *Aura of Might* using its Charisma. Once cast in this way, the knight must finish a Short or Long Rest before doing so again.

### Virtue of Opportunism

*The abyss-born demon had already torn through many soldiers with barbed chitin blades before reaching Henriot Stormleaf. Though the monster was swift, it was reckless, and Henriot was just as quick to seize such an opening, his halberd striking true.*

**(1/Turn).** The knight deals an extra 17 (5d6) damage when it hits a creature with a weapon attack made with advantage.

### Virtue of Power

*The most ferocious among the Circle of Frost Wolves were said to be shrouded in electrified air, a veil that allowed the leader and his subjects to withstand the full brunt of the fiercest magics and yet live.*

The knight can cast *Aura of Power* using its Charisma. Once cast in this way, the knight must finish a Short or Long Rest before doing so again.

#### Aura of Power 
*5th-level Evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:**  Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Classes:**  Paladin

When an affected creature makes a saving throw against a spell or magical effect that allows a save to take only half damage, it takes no damage if it succeeds on the save.

The knight can cast *Aura of Purity* using its Charisma. Once cast in this way, the knight must finish a Short or Long Rest before doing so again.

### Virtue of Radiance

*Warnings spread among roaming warbands not to raid the dukedom of Sascha Hass. The duke would ride out to meet any who sought to despoil his lands, diving from the heavens upon a crimson griffin with silver spear pointed earthward. The weapon shone with a brilliance heated by the ruler’s conviction, blinding those who dared gaze upon its gleaming tip.*

If the knight moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and makes an attack against it (hit or miss), the target must make a *Constitution Saving Throw*.

*Failure:* The creature is Blinded until the end of its next turn.  
*Failure by 5+:* While Blinded, at the start of its turn the creature moves up to half its Speed in a random direction (roll 1d8 to determine the direction).

### Virtue of The Sentinal

*On the night known as the Ghoul Tide that befell Ratzinger Manor, the noble Lady Rena was the only survivor of her high house, for her personal guardian allowed none of the countless, nauseating ghasts to slink past him and harm the damsel.*

The knight can take one Reaction on every turn in combat.

It can use its Reactions only for opportunity attacks or the following:

*Trigger:* A creature within 5 feet of the knight takes the Disengage action or makes an attack against a target other than the knight.  
*Response:* The knight makes an opportunity attack against that creature.

### Virtue of The Slayer

*Da Boyz always said there ain’t nuffin’ in the whole warband louder or meaner than Crusader Jack’s own personality, 'cept maybe that choppin’ arm of his. That whirrin’, teef-spittin’ slab o’ metal could cleave even the biggest humie clean in two like it was nuffin’ at all.*

The first time each turn the knight hits a creature with a weapon attack, the target must make a *Strength Saving Throw*.

*Failure:* The creature takes 13 (3d8) Slashing damage.

### Virtue of Swordsmanship

*The beastman of chaos lunged predictably at Therion Willowvoice, Warden of the Wood, with a crude axe encrusted with filth. With a simple yet precise flick of the warden’s blade, the clumsy assault was deflected. The guardian repaid the abomination for his effort with a stroke beneath his jaw, fitting recompense for such a blunder.*

The knight gains the following Reaction.

***Defensive Flourish.*** 
*Trigger:* The knight is hit by an attack.  
*Response:* The knight gains a +4 bonus to its AC against that attack. If the attack then misses, the knight can make one melee weapon attack against the attacker.

### Virtue of Tenacity

*When the halfling Idous Stonestep and his trusted battle dog were swallowed by a massive minotaur, his company mourned their brave companion’s passing, only for the behemoth to clutch at its gut as a blade erupted forth, slicing through layers of flesh and fate. The small warrior and his loyal mount emerged through the newly-made opening, scarred by acid and marked by savage teeth, yet wearing boisterous smiles upon their faces.*

When the knight is reduced to half its hit points or fewer, it gains advantage on saving throws for 1 minute.

At the start of each of its turns during this time, it gains 20 temporary hit points.

The knight must finish a Short or Long Rest before using this ability again.


### Virtue of Vigilance

*The Legion of the Owl has patrolled the walls of their kingdom since an age long forgotten; none have slipped past the Baleful Guardians’ watch.*

The knight gains a +5 bonus to Initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.

The knight can’t be Surprised or Blinded.

The knight has Blindsight out to 15 feet.

The knight can take the Search action as a Bonus Action.

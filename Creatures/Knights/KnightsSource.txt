## Knightly Rankings
The following are representations of knights across a range of rankings and power levels.

### Knight Errant
Young nobles who wish to walk the path of knighthood and become figures of legend often begin as Knight Errants. These eager youths have typically spent their childhood mastering all manner of weapons and memorizing countless codes of conduct. Some may be taken into service by a lord or seasoned knight to begin their journey as Errants.

Despite their rigorous study, such recruits are inexperienced and lack the refined discipline forged through years of hardship and conflict that veteran knights possess. Many are brazen and overconfident in their martial and diplomatic abilities, often disregarding the advice and warnings of their elders. They expect reverence and respect from all they encounter.

For all their pompous behavior, even novices to knighthood remain formidable adversaries. These hot-blooded warriors fight with ardent ferocity, eager to prove their worth and earn a place among the heroes of old. Knights in higher standing often admire such youthful enthusiasm, seeing it as a necessary stage on the path to full knighthood, one that is inevitably tempered by the hardships of sacred duty.

### Knights of the Realm

An Errant who has proven themselves through years of faithful service or through a great feat of heroism may claim the title of Knight of the Realm, becoming a fully recognized knight in the eyes of their superiors. Such exalted individuals have not only honed their combat skills to great proficiency but have also refined their conduct, allowing them to maneuver skillfully through the political landscapes of noble courts while presenting an air of majesty to all who behold them.

Many Knights of the Realm belong to great orders pledged to the service of a god or kingdom, acting as elite forces when dire threats arise. When the security of the state or the sanctity of divine law is challenged, these knights ride forth gallantly to put an end to such villainy.

Others are granted stewardship over small estates, ruling from modest castles or manors and charged with protecting nearby villages and farmers from roaming bandits and beasts. In return, the common folk pay taxes and offer loyalty to their lord. Most knights are capable of crushing entire bands of robbers single-handedly, and even savage beasts such as ogres or minotaurs have fallen before these valiant defenders, a lance piercing through blubbery fat and corded muscle alike.

### Questing Knights
Many knights grow restless in their roles as ministers of the state or high-ranking soldiers and yearn for a greater purpose. Whether driven by the desire to have their names sung by bards for generations or compelled by visions and whispers from transcendent powers, these knights abandon their worldly possessions save for armor, weapons, and mount, and set forth upon a sacred quest.

Some wander aimlessly across the realms, seeking signs to guide them toward their higher calling. These brave nomads often become local heroes, slaying entire warbands of orcs or beastmen single-handedly, driving their swords through nightmarish foes and casting them back into the hells from which they came. Others delve into ancient tombs and forsaken ruins to retrieve holy relics from ages long past.

On occasion, groups of Questing Knights travel together in pursuit of a shared goal. Such bands are capable of extraordinary feats, liberating villages from the tyranny of dark lords and their minions, or standing against dozens of well-armed raiders who ravage the countryside.

### Blessed Knights
When a Questing Knight proves their valor through trials and hardships that would break lesser warriors, they may draw the attention of higher beings. Angels, great fey, or other divine entities may visit such champions and grant them mystic boons in recognition of their selfless deeds.

These blessings can grant strength surpassing that of a troll, longevity far beyond that of mortal men, and a will resilient enough to withstand even the darkest temptations. Blessed Knights become figures of renown; wherever they appear, crowds of commoners gather to offer praise in hopes of receiving a blessing themselves. Royal courts grant them full attention, hanging on every word they speak.

Many Blessed Knights become stewards of hallowed lands such as enchanted meadows, sacred groves, or forgotten chapels, purging any impurity that dares to set even a taint claw upon their domain. Others devote themselves to protecting the weak, seeking out the greatest evils that plague the land, and eradicating them utterly.



> ##### Knight Champion

### Knight of Legend

Only the greatest of knights are able to rise to the heights of legend. Such figures, through their deeds, inspire young warriors long after they have passed on into the next life. These paragons of courage, during their long lives, were able to change the course of history through their feats of valor. 

\columnbreak

&emsp;Many founded knightly orders that revere the hero as an ideal they aspire to reach. Some were even able to find entire empires, whether through liberating kingdoms from their wicked rulers or taming unforgiving wildernesses infested with all manner of savage monstrosities so that civilization might take root. Giants, demons, and even dragons have been slayed by such heroes, armed with sword and astride a loyal steed.

## Knightly Actions

If you wish to make your knight a true solo threat, you may grant it an extra Knightly Action it can use each round of combat, increasing its CR by 1.

This grants the knight an additional action in combat, taken on Initiative Count 21 (losing ties), separate from the knight’s normal initiative. On that count, the knight can take one of the following actions, provided it is not Incapacitated; it may still use Tenacious Will even while Incapacitated.

You may choose to grant the knight additional Knightly Actions on Initiative Count 15 and Initiative Count 0 (losing ties). For each additional action granted this way, the knight’s challenge rating increases by 1.

**Charge.**  The knight, or controlled mount it is riding, moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The knight can make a melee attack with its lance at any point during this movement with the following properties: *Melee Weapon Attack:* Lance attack bonus to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Piercing damage.

**Tenacious Will.**  If the knight is subject to an ongoing effect that allows a saving throw at the start or end of its turn (or as an action), it can immediately make that saving throw with advantage. On a success, the effect ends.

*The word of the exalted king carried a supremacy none could ignore. From feral beasts to towering giants, all would kneel in terror and reverence.*

*Wisdom Saving Throw:* One creature the knight can see within 90 feet.

*Many tales have been told of the rowdy warriors belonging to the Order of the Wild Boar, a boastful and truculent lot who cause a ruckus at any inn or keep in which they lodge. Such rabble-rousers barrel into the thick of violence, fueled by an arrogant confidence that drives them ever forward.*

When the knight rolls Initiative, it and one controlled mount it is riding become Belligerent for 1 minute.

While Belligerent, the first time each turn the knight or its mount hits with an attack, it deals an extra 13 (3d8) damage of the attack’s damage type.

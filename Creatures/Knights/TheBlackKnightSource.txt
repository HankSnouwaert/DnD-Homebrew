## The Black Knight
*An Undying Warrior Who Fights On Even As His Body Is Destroyed*<br>
**Habitat:** Any; **Treasure:** Armaments  <br>
Through folktales and many legends, there are stories of a similar nature, of valiant kings and heroes who, in their journeys, have their paths blocked by grim figures clad in ebon armor and clutching great blades. Such foreboding sentinels allow no passage through their domain and offer only death to those who ignore their command.

These foes are no mere men armed with steel, for it has been recounted time and again that even when a sword strikes true against such villains, hewing limbs and carving deep wounds, such injuries do not halt the fanatical guardians. Through dark magic, their skin, flesh, and bone may be chopped away, and yet the dread champion continues to persist through some unholy determination.

Some hold that even when the body is hacked into red mist, the hateful soul of such men fights on.

\columnbreak

> ##### New Condition: Dazed 
> A dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

### Black Knight Tactics
The Black Knight’s primary goal is to apply Death’s Curse as much as possible. Whatever a target reaches the maximum stacks of Death’s Curse, it becomes the Black Knight’s focus.

**Living State.** The Black Knight targets the creature with the lowest Charisma or a creature with 10 or fewer hit points with its Mark of Death.

**Corpse and Husk.** Using Death’s Respite, the knight pulls in ranged attackers, then has the husk attack and prevents them from escaping.

**Skeleton and Fragments.** The knight lays Desecration on areas with immobile targets, such as a creature restrained by its husk or struck by its flesh. Any fragments created attack the same target as the knight. Otherwise, it places its Desecration in the largest group of foes.

**Black Knight’s Soul.** The Black Knight’s Soul teleports into the largest group of enemies and uses Death’s Bite

> ### Monstrous Actions
>
> The black knight gains two additional actions in combat: Mark of Death and Strike. It can replace either action with Tenacious Will, and it can use Tenacious Will even while Incapacitated.
>
> ***Mark of Death (Initiative Count 20).*** The black knight moves up to 15 feet and unleashes a curse at one creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must make a *Charisma Saving Throw:* DC 16 (made with disadvantage if the target has 10 or fewer hit points).  
> *Failure:* The target gains one stack of Death’s Curse. Whenever the target takes damage from the black knight or one of its minions, it takes an additional 3 (1d6) Necrotic damage for each stack it has. All stacks fade after 1 minute if no new stack is gained, if holy water is splashed on the creature, or if *Remove Curse* is cast on it.  
> *Failure by 5+:* The target gains two stacks of Death’s Curse.
>
> ***Strike (Initiative Count 1).*** The black knight moves up to 15 feet and makes one Greatsword attack.
>
> ***Tenacious Will.*** If the black knight is subject to an ongoing effect that allows a saving throw as an action or at the start or end of its turn, it can immediately make that saving throw with advantage, ending the effect on a success.

> ### Monstrous Actions
>
> The black knight’s soul gains two additional actions in combat: Death’s Bite and Strike. It can replace either action with Tenacious Will, and it can use Tenacious Will even while Incapacitated.
>
> ***Death’s Bite (Initiative Count 20).*** The black knight’s soul teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet. Each Non-Undead creature within 30 feet of it must make a Charisma saving throw (DC 16). (Made with disadvantage if the target has 10 or fewer hit points.)  
> *Failure:* The creature takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage and gains one stack of Death’s Curse.  
> *Failure by 5+:* The creature instead takes 17 (5d6) Necrotic damage and gains two stacks of Death’s Curse.
>
> ***Strike (Initiative Count 1).*** The black knight’s soul moves up to 15 feet and makes an Extinguish attack.
>
> ***Tenacious Will.*** If the black knight’s soul is subject to an ongoing effect that allows a saving throw as an action or at the start or end of its turn, it can immediately make that save with advantage, ending the effect on a success.

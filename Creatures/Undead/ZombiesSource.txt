## Zombies
*Relentless Reanimated Corpses*<br>
**Habitat:** Planar (Shadowfell), Underdark, Urban; <br> **Treasure:** None <br>

Of all the various undead brought back to unlife <br> through the magics of necromancy, none are more <br> feeble than shambling zombies, also known as <br> coffin walkers or flesh puppets. These heinous <br> mockeries of life are forged from rotting cadavers <br> by dark power. Such power may arise from <br> macabre energy seeping through places of great <br> death, such as battlefields or towns ravaged by <br> sickness, and flowing into any remains. At other <br> times, twisted spellcasters deliberately raise them <br> to serve as unquestioning soldiers against any  <br>who would oppose them.

Whatever anathema event triggers their creation, <br> the outcome is the same: black magic replaces the <br> breath and heartbeat that once flowed through the <br> body of the deceased. These vile forces now <br> command the remains to shuffle forward and feast <br> on the flesh of the living.

A single zombie poses little threat. Such <br> creatures are slow in both body and mind. All that <br> they were in life is gone, leaving only a mindless <br> husk that lurches forward on tattered, decaying <br> muscle. With no regard for its surroundings <br> beyond the presence of warm flesh, the putrid <br> halfwit sluggishly grasps and holds down its prey, <br> sinking filth-encrusted teeth into living tissue. <br> Even those mystically bound to the will of a <br> necromancer prove little more than stiff <br> marionettes. The caster can only point the <br> creatures toward those they wish the dark thralls <br> to ravage, or halt their awkward movement so <br> they stand motionless.

Zombies do possess one singular trait that gives them an edge and allows them to overcome those who might otherwise destroy them: their bodies can suffer vast amounts of punishment that would fell any living creature. A lumbering carcass may be stabbed through the chest or have limbs hacked off, yet it presses on with its clumsy assault, unperturbed and hungry.

### Relentless Zombies

Some zombies are infused with far greater quantities of baleful magic. Dark tendrils course through their foetid forms, rendering them nearly indestructible and granting them superior strength as well. Only the complete obliteration of the creature’s head or heart, where the magic has pooled, can end these necromantic troopers for good.

### Zombie Hordes
Zombies become a far greater threat when gathered in large groups driven by a common purpose: to consume the living. Necromancers can easily raise vast numbers of these unholy minions and maintain control over them with far less effort than is required for other undead, then send the revolting flock to enact their will. A shambling throng can withstand all manner of attacks and still march onward, enraged, toward its foes.

\columnbreak

<br><Br>

When opponents become overwhelmed and exhausted, masses of rotten arms reach out to snatch them up, dragging them into the tide of hungering dead to be torn apart. Such resilient regiments are often used to tire out opponents through sheer weight of numbers, leaving them vulnerable to even more insidious creations that finish off the enervated combatants.

### Zombie Apocalypse
When storms of wicked magic, or the most dreadful liches and vampires, descend upon a locale of great tragedy, brimming with countless fresh corpses, the outcome is apocalyptic. Cold bodies twitch and jerk, rising in unison as an army ready to wash over the land and eradicate any creature that draws breath. A sea of countless undead moves without need of rest or warmth. Entire towns can be easily consumed by the gnashing of rotten teeth; even entire cities may not be able to withstand the army of the damned when their numbers are vast enough and they are guided by a devious will. 


\columnbreak

### Zombie Tactics
Zombies swarm over the first target they are able to reach, then surround it and use their actions to attempt to grapple and knock it prone, at which point they bite it. Zombies are slow, so allies should use zombies as meat shields and distractions to keep the enemy busy.

## Armadillos
*Adaptable Mammalian Tanks of The Animal Kingdom*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Forest, Grasslands, Hills, Swamps; <br> **Treasure:** None <br>
Armadillos are among the strangest of mammals. These small burrowing creatures possess a hardened shell across their backs, segmented with leathery bands that grant them remarkable flexibility compared to other armored animals. The three-banded armadillos are even capable of short bursts of speed to escape predators. If such sprints fail, they can roll into a tight ball, fully enclosing themselves so that their bony armor protects their entire body.

Many subterranean races keep armadillos as pets. Some powerful celestials who command warlocks even grant these humble creatures as familiars to their devoted servants, for armadillos embody both protection and humility.


### Armadillo Tactics
Armadillos are timid and avoid combat whenever possible, fleeing or burrowing to escape danger.

As familiars, they use the Help action, then roll into a defensive ball to protect themselves.

### Hunter Armadillos

Hunter armadillos, often called Killer Dillos, are far more fearsome than their tiny kin. Roughly the size of a small bear, they possess massive claws capable of tearing through earth and powerful jaws lined with sharp teeth that can seize and hold prey in place.

These predators hunt small subterranean creatures such as gophers and snakes, as well as small humanoids like goblins and kobolds. They can even be found stalking prey in the Underdark, where they are capable of short, explosive sprints to close the distance.

If gravely wounded, a hunter armadillo may curl into a defensive ball like its smaller kin, though its sheer size makes this a last resort rather than a reliable defense.

### Hunter Armadillo Tactics
Hunter armadillos Dash to engage, attempting to grapple and drag targets away from allies.

If overwhelmed, they roll into a defensive ball until they can reengage or escape.

When directed by intelligent allies, they rush the back line to disrupt weaker targets. Enemies must choose between wasting time taking out the armadillos, who will roll into a ball, or ignoring them and suffering continued attacks.

### Glyptodons

Glyptodons are enormous mammals, roughly the size of a cart, boasting a massive fused bony shell that covers most of their body. Only the head and tail remain partially exposed, though both are reinforced with heavy armor plating. Their spike-studded tails can sweep in devastating arcs, capable of crushing would-be predators.

Such formidable defenses come at a cost. Glyptodons are slow and cannot keep pace even with a walking human at full speed. Clever humanoids have also learned that their underside lacks protective plating, making it their greatest vulnerability.

Many tribal societies have hunted glyptodon to extinction in their regions, as these creatures provide not only an abundance of meat but also enormous shells that can be fashioned into shelters. Others, however, have captured and trained these behemoths for war, using them as living barricades and battering rams. Archers march behind the lumbering creatures, loosing volleys of arrows from behind their armored bulk, while any who attempt to close the distance must contend with the sweeping, mace-like strikes of the glyptodon’s tail.


### Glyptodon Tactics
Alone, glyptodons are slow and easily avoided.

They are most effective alongside ranged allies using them as cover, or creatures that can drag foes into range of their tail attacks.

The most dangerous encounters involve creatures capable of teleporting a glyptodon into the center of a group, negating its slow speed.

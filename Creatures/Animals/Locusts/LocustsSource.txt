## Locusts
*Devouring Swarms Leaving Famine & Ruin In Their Wake*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Grasslands; **Treasure:** None <br>
Few mundane creatures rival the destructive force of a natural disaster, but locust swarms force to be reckoned with. When gathered in vast numbers, they scour the land as surely as any tornado or earthquake, leaving only barren earth in their wake. Fields are stripped bare, forests reduced to husks, and entire regions rendered lifeless.

Despite their apocalyptic impact, locusts arise through natural means. In times of abundant rainfall and rapid plant growth, certain grasshopper populations explode in number. Normally solitary, these insects are driven into constant contact by overcrowding. This triggers a rapid metamorphosis: their bodies enlarge, their coloration shifts to stark yellow and black, and their behavior becomes aggressive and insatiable.

The transformed swarm devours everything in its path—plants, crops, and even one another. As they migrate, their numbers swell into the billions, darkening the sky and consuming entire ecosystems. These migrations can persist for years, crossing continents and even oceans. In their wake lie famine, collapse, and the quiet ruins of once-thriving civilizations.

Ancient texts speak of such swarms as warnings and punishments sent to humble the proud and destroy the faithless. Entire empires are said to have fallen beneath their shadow.

### Locust Plague Tactics 
When guided by a higher intelligence, the plague of locusts moves onto the largest group of spell casters to disrupt concentration and make as many attacks as possible. Beyond that, it acts without any regard for self-preservation.

## Divine Locusts
*Ruinous Heralds of Divine Punishment*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Grasslands; **Treasure:** Relics <br>
At times, the gods themselves weaponize the endless voracity of the locust.

When kingdoms fall into corruption or abandon their sacred rites, divine will may bend the natural order, calling forth locusts as instruments of judgment. Within these vast swarms emerge towering figures known as divine locusts, harbingers of famine and living avatars of divine wrath.

These beings stand over seven feet tall, shaped like humanoids but bearing the grotesque features of immense locusts. Their chitinous bodies are armored like plate, their compound eyes gleam with alien purpose, and their massive mandibles click with restless hunger. Long, segmented limbs end in blade-like tarsi, capable of cutting through armor and flesh alike.

Their wings beat with a deafening, chittering roar, filling the air with a maddening cadence that erodes the will of those who hear it. Few can stand firm as the swarm descends.

Divine Locusts are never solitary. They move in small cadres within the greater swarm, surrounded by countless lesser locusts. Though possessed of keen intelligence, they are utterly devoted to their divine purpose, showing no hesitation, mercy, or remorse.

A handful of these beings can consume entire harvests in hours. Armies that rise against them are cut down amid chaos and terror, their ranks broken by both blade and madness. Should one of these creatures fall, its kin descend upon it without hesitation, devouring the wounded angel alive to reclaim its strength and press the assault.

Even in death, they remain a threat. The corpse of a slain divine locust ruptures into a frenzy of smaller insects, lashing out at anything nearby before dispersing into the swarm.

Druids, in particular, regard divine locusts with deep hatred. Many sagas recount bitter wars between druidic circles and zealous clerical orders. When diplomacy fails, clerics may call down these celestial horrors to strip sacred groves and sanctuaries bare.

### Divine Locust Tactics
  
Divine locusts swarm vulnerable targets to gain advantage on their attacks. When one divine locust falls below 40 hit points, it moves into the largest group of foes to bring them in range of its death burst, and its allies will attack and consume it.

> ##### New Condition: Quickened
> A Quickened creature gains an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack or unarmed strike only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action. The *Haste* spell applies the Quickened condition among its other effects, and a creature cannot benefit from the Quickened condition from multiple sources at once.

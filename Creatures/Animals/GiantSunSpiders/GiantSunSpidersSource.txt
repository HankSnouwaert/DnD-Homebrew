## Giant Sun Spiders
*Fast & Deadly Arachnid Hunters of The Desert*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert; **Treasure:** None <br>
Sand is flung into the air by hair-covered legs as they scuttle in unison across the dry dunes at speeds that make them appear as little more than a blur to any observer. These limbs are attached to a large, oval body crowned by a massive head, its chelicerae chittering ominously. Such a sight can mean only one thing: the approach of the beast known as a Wind Scorpion, or Sun Spider.

Though they belong to neither the spider nor scorpion families, they are their own distinct type of arachnid. Stalking deserts and barren, flat landscapes, they hunt in the dead of night, sprinting across the earth at speeds rivaling a galloping horse, guided by their pedipalps and the faintest vibrations as they navigate the darkness. With such agility, they bear down on prey with chitinous maws as sharp as greatswords, slicing hunks from their victims and leaving behind gaping wounds.

Such a creature is nearly untamable. Its speed and rapacious appetite drive it to tear apart anything within reach. To slay such a nightmare is considered a great feat among most cultures, for only the most skilled warriors dare face these skittering terrors, and even the finest armor offers little protection against the remorseless weapons of these predators.

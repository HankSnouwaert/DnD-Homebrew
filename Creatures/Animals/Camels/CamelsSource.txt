## Camels
*Paragons of Endurance & Survival In The Harsh Desert*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert; **Treasure:** None <br>
Camels are one of the few large animals able to thrive in the harshest deserts of the world. Whether in biting cold or sweltering heat, these creatures can be found in herds striding for miles across the sand in both extremes of temperature, able to go without water or food for days. Though camels are protected by the mere fact that most predators cannot survive in such brutal wastelands, they are not defenseless. Despite their lack of tusks, horns, or even hard hooves, a camel’s bite is particularly brutal for a herbivore and is capable of breaking bones.

Such an ability to withstand hardship has been harnessed by many peoples, allowing them to traverse vast dunes with all manner of goods secured to the backs of these animals. Even in warfare, many armies have used the hump-backed beast as a mount, replacing horses in such regions. So necessary are camels that they have become symbols of civilization and survival.

### Dire Camel
Such camels tower like colossi, casting long shadows over the arid land. Their heads stand twelve feet high, with bites that can crush a man. Though they require more food and water than their smaller kin, they are still utilized by nations as beasts of burden in the construction of massive monuments and gleaming cities, hauling stones that no other creature of the region could manage. They are also beasts of war, favored by nomadic raiders who strap small howdahs to their humped backs, allowing marauders to pelt foes from on high with arrows or thrust downward with polearms while their towering mounts crush enemies underfoot.

#### Camel Tactics
Camels used as mounts, or fighting in large groups, may replace their Bite attack with a Shove attempt to knock a creature Prone. This tactic grants advantage to their allies’ melee attacks and allows the camel to deal additional damage with its Stomp against a fallen target.

> ##### New Condition: Fatigue
> The creature experiences debilitating weariness that can accumulate in levels. Each time a creature gains the Fatigue condition, its Fatigue Level increases by 1. If the level reaches 6, the creature dies. Fatigue Levels have the following effects on a creature:
> * **d20 Rolls:** When a creature makes a d20 Test, subtract its Fatigue Level from the d20 roll.
> * **Spell Save DCs:** Subtract a creature's Fatigue Level from the Spell Save DC of any spell it casts.
> * **Speed** A creature's speed is reduced by a number of feet equal to 5 x its Fatigue level. This can't lower its speed below 5 feet.
> 
> **Ending the Condition:** Completing a Long Rest reduces a creature's Fatigue Level by 1.
> 
> **Fatigue vs. Exhaustion:** Fatigue is an alternative mechanic to the Exhaustion condition, and any ability that removes levels of Exhaustion or grants immunity to it does the same for Fatigue.

### Desiccated Camels
Even with all the camel’s adaptations to <br> withstand its uncaring homeland, the sands <br> still claim many of these stubborn beasts, <br> which expire from thirst, their dried <br> carcasses buried beneath shifting dunes. <br> Some camels, seeking to quench an <br> unrelenting thirst, wander into realms whose <br> arid state was born of dreadful curses, lands <br> stripped of any verdancy they once held. There, <br> baleful magics suffuse the relentless creature <br> so that even death does not end its  search.

What rises is nothing but shriveled skin and <br> withered muscle stretched over dried bone,<br> shambling onward, thirst driving it eternally <br> forward. Such horrors are never able to sate <br> their craving. Any oasis they find is drained of <br> every last drop, the water drawn into the walking <br> cadaver only to evaporate in seconds, leaving the  <br> creature as parched as before.

This endless need for moisture inevitably drives the undead to attack any living creature they encounter, seeking to consume the liquid within. Such monsters are lethal to any traveler who dares traverse the desert, for the Desiccated Camel can overwhelm its victims with a terror that stiffens the limbs. The bite of these nightmares causes flesh to shrivel and weaken. Worst of all, the camel can, with a single inhale, draw all moisture from the surrounding air, only for it to vanish down its weathered gullet.

#### Desiccated Camel Tactics

If unable to reach a creature, a Desiccated Camel uses its Dreadful Glare to hinder distant prey. When two or more creatures are within range, it employs Desiccating Inhale to weaken multiple targets at once before closing in to attack.

## Gordak Knifetwister
*A Fel Goblin Boss Who Uses Luck, Underhanded Tactics, & Disposable Minions to Come Out On Top*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Mountain, Underdark, Urban, Volcanic; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

Fel goblins rarely live long lives. The constant struggle beneath their infernal dwarf masters, combined with their own treacherous instincts, ensures that most of these vile grunts meet their end by a crude shiv driven deep into the spine or a well-placed arrow to the back of the head.

Gordak Knifetwister, however, has survived through a continuous chain of unfortunate miracles, persisting long past the expiration date of his disposable kin. Filled with vitriol, cunning, and uncanny good fortune, Gordak is able to sidestep even the most expertly placed attacks and retaliate with his Blade of Malice, a cruel weapon capable of bypassing even the strongest defenses to strike where a target is most vulnerable.

He carries a pair of cursed dice said to aid him in escaping death’s grasp, whether by granting him the strength to pry himself free from a ferocious beast’s maw or sharpening his wits enough to see through some dreadful illusion.

Gordak has risen far above any fel goblin before him, amassing a large band of wolf riders and becoming one of the most formidable and sought-after groups of hirelings within the fel dwarf empire. Leading from atop his ragged and irritable mount, Thrall Trap’a, Gordak and his riders are able to cheat death itself and secure entire workforces of laborers for the highest bidder.

### Gordak's Lair

Gordak never stays in one place for long. Constantly on the move, he roams in search of his next bounty, or his next victim. When he lingers in an area for any length of time, he and his flunkies throw together some ramshackle camp for shelter. Other times, the cruel band of marauders can be found lurking within the dark towers and forsaken facilities of the fel dwarves.

Wherever Gordak travels, he always seems to have a fresh supply of minions willing to take upon themselves the grisly fate he truly deserves.

When fighting within their lair, Gordak's Challenge Rating (CR) increases by 2.

#### Lair Action
When either Gordak or Thrall Trap’a rolls initiative, their lair awakens to aid them. Until combat ends or both die, the lair has an Intelligence of 16 and can act of its own accord, always acting in the best interest of both. While active in this way, the lair is aware of the location of all creatures within it. On initiative count 1 (losing initiative ties), the lair chooses an unoccupied space at the edge of its boundaries. A Gordak Boyz (stat block on the page after next) appears in the space and remains until destroyed. It acts on initiative count 0 (losing initiative ties).

### Gordak's Tactics
Gordak focuses on a single low-AC target to build Luck Points and apply weakness against his attacks. He constantly repositions himself so that no two enemies are within 5 feet of him, allowing his Slippery ability to remain active.

Gordak rolls his Crooked Dice whenever:
* He is affected by a condition or effect that allows repeated saving throws, and he does not currently have advantage on those saves
* An enemy is repeatedly forcing him to make a saving throw without advantage.

#### Gordak & Thrall Trap’a Tactics
When fighting alongside Thrall Trap’a, Trap’a uses his bite attack first against the nearest target within range, preferably one that Gordak is not currently attacking.

* If the bite misses, Trap’a uses Menacing Growl on Gordak’s target to frighten it and provoke attacks of opportunity from Gordak.
* If the bite hits, Trap’a flings the target away unless it is Gordak’s chosen target. If it is Gordak’s target, Trap’a keeps the creature Grappled instead.

#### Gordak's Layer
When they appear, Gordak's boys will first hit a target with their burning arrows, then dash into melee combat to absorb damage for Gordak.  

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Burning
> The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid.
> * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
> * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
> * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

### Epic Battle: Fang and Blade
You can pair Gordak with is steed Thrall Trap'a to make him an even greater threat. Together, they should be treated as having a combined Challenge Rating (CR) of 13.

> ##### Trained Beasts
>
> Trained beasts acquire skills over the years, similar to how players gain experience in a class. This means spells and abilities, such as *polymorph* or a druid's Wild Shape, cannot transform a creature into a trained beast.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Back Stabber.*** Gordak has advantage on opportunity attacks. Creatures provoke opportunity attacks from Gordak even if they take the Disengage action before leaving his reach, though the attack made against a creature this way has disadvantage instead of advantage.
>
> ***Lucky Git.*** Every time Gordak succeeds on a saving throw or hits with an attack roll during combat, he gains a luck point up to a maximum of 10. He loses all luck points at the end of combat or when he fails a saving throw or misses with an attack roll.
>
> ***Reactive.*** Gordak can take one reaction on every turn in combat.
>
> ***Slippery.*** Attacks against Gordak have disadvantage and deal half damage to him unless Gordak has a speed of 0 or there are two or more hostile creatures within 5 feet of him.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Blade of Malice.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage for each luck point he has. Gordak deals an additional 7 (2d6) Piercing damage if the attack was made using his reaction. If the target is a creature, it gains one level of Vulnerability to Gordak, up to a maximum of 10.
> 
>For each level of Vulnerability to Gordak a creature has, the critical hit threshold for Gordak's attacks made against that target is lowered by 1. At the end of the creature's turn, if it hasn't received a level of Vulnerability to Gordak since its last turn, it loses all levels. 
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Crooked Dice.*** Gordak rolls 1d6 and gains the corresponding perk tied to an ability score for the next minute, or until he gains another perk. While he has the perk, he has advantage on checks and saving throws made with the chosen ability. Additionally, when Gordak makes a saving throw using that ability against a spell or effect that allows a save to take only half damage, he takes no damage on a success and only half damage on a failure. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**1. Cunning.** Intelligence. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**2. Quick.** Dexterity. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**3. Tough.** Constitution. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**4. Brawny.** Strength. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**5. Smooth.** Charisma. <br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**6. Intuitive.** Wisdom. <br>
>
> ### Monstrous Actions
>
> Gordak gains one additional action in combat on Initiative Count 10. He can take any of the following actions if he is not Incapacitated. He can also take Tenacious Will even if Incapacitated.
>
> ***Stick 'Em (Initiative Count 10).*** Gordak or a creature he is mounted on moves up to 15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks and then Gordak makes a Blade of Malice attack.
>
> ***Leg It (Initiative Count 10).*** Gordak or a creature he is mounted on moves up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
>
> ***Tenacious Will.*** If Gordak is subject to an ongoing effect that allows him to make a saving throw as an action or at the start or end of his turn, he can immediately make that save with advantage, ending the effect on a success.
>
> ### Reactions
>
> ***Better Them Than Me.*** *Trigger:* A creature attacks Gordak. <br>
> *Response:* Gordak chooses an ally within 5 feet of him and within range of the attack to become the target of the attack. If no other creatures are within range, the reaction is wasted. If the target is reduced to 0 hit points this way, Gordak gains a luck point.

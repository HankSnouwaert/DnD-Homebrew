## Fel Goblins
*Devious Sadists Who Cowardly Serve Their Brutal Masters*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Mountain, Underdark, Urban, Volcanic; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>

A wide variety of unpleasant goblinoids pillage and spread mayhem across the civilized world, earning the ire and disgust of folk everywhere. Yet any who have had the misfortune of contending with fel goblins confidently claim these vile marauders are the most despicable of their kind. The sheer duplicity, sadism, and cowardice displayed by the average fel goblin is so extreme that even other goblinoids spurn them, attacking such perfidious spawn on sight.

These creatures are slightly larger than common goblins, though still somewhat shorter than the average man. Their builds are wiry and sinuous, and their gaunt faces twist into gaping grins whenever an opportunity to inflict misery presents itself, revealing rows of yellow, needle-like teeth.

Because of their craven and spiteful nature, many fel goblins have been subjected and conscripted into the service of the Fel Dwarf Empire. Their cowardice keeps any individual from standing against their iron masters for fear of incurring their wrath, while their duplicitous nature prevents them from ever forming any meaningful rebellion with their wretched brethren to depose imperial rule.

The fel dwarfs employ these lowly minions for tasks considered unfit even for the lowest ranks of dwarven society: taskmasters, messengers, expendable warriors, and other dirty work.

Fel goblins pour onto the battlefield, wielding crude weapons and shoddy armor. When given a choice of arms, they favor short swords and daggers for close combat, along with bows for striking from afar before rushing in for the kill.

Though not particularly deadly foes, these goblinoids are equally prone to charging their enemies in malicious fervor as they are to breaking ranks and fleeing in terror, unless a dwarven overseer is nearby to keep them in line. Even so, none should ever turn their back on such cretins, for fel goblins are all too eager to seize any opening, driving jagged blades and rusted spikes into the unsuspecting.

### Fel Goblin Sneaks

Fel goblin sneaks serve as assassins for the fel dwarfs and are among the most deplorable of their kind. These creatures can move with alarming stealth despite carrying ample supplies of poison-laced daggers hidden throughout their garb. Striking with foul precision, they excel at eliminating high-value targets before vanishing back into the shadows.

### Fel Goblin Burning Archers

Armed with crude shortbows and oil-coated arrows, these malign marksmen delight in setting anything they can ablaze, whether buildings, siege works, or enemy soldiers. Greater still is the mirth they find in driving flaming arrows into the backs of unsuspecting victims and watching panic spread through the ranks.

### Fel Goblin Wolf Riders
Highest among the lowly fel goblins are the wolf-riding bands that dwell along the fringes of the fel dwarfs’ domain. These outriders are employed as mercenaries by the empire, though they remain nearly as treacherous as their footbound counterparts.

Mounted atop vicious fanged steeds, these goblins serve as scouts and skirmishing cavalry, using their speed to outmaneuver enemies and strike vulnerable flanks, sowing chaos wherever they ride. Their swiftness, however, makes them even more precarious than their infantry brethren. Should the tide of battle turn against them, they are quick to rout and flee beyond the reach of their overseers’ punishment.

Despite this risk, the value of such swift raiders cannot be denied. Few soldiers in the fel dwarfs’ arsenal are more effective during slaving expeditions and border raids. Able to run down fleeing victims with terrifying efficiency, these relentless bandits and their savage mounts are feared wherever they roam.

### Fel Goblin Vile Gits
The self-proclaimed Vile Gits are a notorious mercenary band that has managed to secure a rare degree of autonomy within the tyrannical dominion of the fel dwarfs. Their unmatched talent for despoiling settlements and hunting down fleeing victims has made them invaluable to dwarven factions and warlords alike.

So prized are their cruel services that competing fel dwarf states will surrender vast sums of wealth simply to keep these hirelings out of enemy hands. In return, the Vile Gits excel at gathering fresh slaves for the covetous industries of their masters.

These warriors strike with brutal force, slicing any foolish enough to flee,  as tarnished blades and the snapping jaws of their monstrous canids make short work of those who turn their backs, leaving the duo out of their sight. They also wield arrows coated in viscous tar that ignite upon impact. Worse still, the substance burns hotter the longer it is exposed to flame, rendering victims increasingly susceptible to further pyromantic attacks.

### Fel Goblin Tactics
Fel goblins specialize in forcing enemies to flee, creating openings for reaction attacks that deal additional damage. They Dash as a bonus action to rapidly close the distance to vulnerable targets, overwhelming isolated enemies through coordinated attacks.

Fel goblins work especially well alongside fel dwarfs with ranged weapons. While the goblins tie enemies down in melee and prevent them from advancing, the dwarfs can safely attack from a distance.

#### Fel Goblin Sneak Tactics
Fel goblin sneaks follow the same general tactics as standard fel goblins, but fight with greater mobility. They prefer to target creatures with low Constitution. If threatened, sneaks will Hide or Disengage

#### Fel Goblin Burning Archer Tactics
Fel goblin burning archers focus on igniting as many enemies as possible from a distance while constantly repositioning to maintain range. They Dash between firing positions and avoid prolonged melee whenever possible.

If forced into close combat:
* They will Dash away from opponents who have the advantage over them.
* If escape is not possible or they have many allies nearby, they will Dodge and wait for their allies to overwhelm the enemy together.


#### Fel Goblin Wolfrider & Vile Git Tactics
Wolf riders attack from a range first, using their bows to ignite targets before charging into melee. Once engaged, they attempt to overwhelm isolated enemies through coordinated attacks, then break away and continue hit-and-run assaults in the same style as other fel goblins.

> ##### Alternative Fel Goblin Masters
> The Malign Authority trait leads fel goblins to work well with fel dwarves. However, if this doesn't make sense in your setting, feel free to modify this trait to utilize a different creature type that can serve as the fel goblins' dark masters. Other creatures could include hobgoblins, salamaders, red dragons, or fire giants.


<br>

> ##### New Condition: Burning
> The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid.
> * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
> * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
> * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

## Urvhak Skaar
*First of The Wicked Fel Dwarf Demonsmiths*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Mountain, Underdark, Urban, Volcanic; **Treasure:** Arcane <br>

Legend speaks of Urvhak Skaar, the first dwarf to institute the dark practices of Demonic Artifice upon which the fallen Dwarf Empire built its power.

It is said that, in the distant past, the ancestors of those who would become the corrupt conquerors were once a noble-hearted people. They had established a great colony in lands far beyond those known to the dwarven race, which was still young in the world during that age.

Then calamity struck.

The fledgling civilization, along with the expanding dwarven kingdom, was battered into chaos. Earthquakes shattered the land, monstrous storms ravaged the skies, and unnatural beasts tore through the realm. As the great dwarven holds sealed their underground cities to weather the devastation, the distant colonies, lacking mighty fortifications and cut off from aid, were left to fend for themselves within the ravaged wilderness.

It was then that Urvhak Skaar, a great leader among the desperate pioneers, was approached by a demigod of shadow and flame. The unholy being perceived within Skaar a will and determination greater than that of any of his kin. Together, the demigod and the dwarf descended into the Underworld and the Realms of Chaos, where they absconded with all manner of forbidden lore.

With this newfound knowledge, the demigod ascended to the mantle of the Father of Flame, a terrible and fearsome god. Urvhak Skaar, in turn, used his understanding of the dark arts to become the first Demon Smith and the first priest of the dark divinity. 

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br>

Through his forbidden craft, he saved his people from oblivion, teaching them many of the stolen secrets and laying the foundations of both the Fel Dwarf Empire and the Demon Smith Brotherhood.

Yet such power demanded a dreadful price.

One by one, those whom Urvhak taught began to suffer a terrible curse. Their flesh slowly petrified until they became entombed in stone for all eternity. Though Urvhak Skaar managed to stave off this fate for generations, he did so by passing the curse onto others, condemning countless victims to bear the burden of his own sins.

Even this grim sacrifice could not halt the curse entirely. With every passing year, more of the affliction remained within him. His skin darkened until it resembled polished obsidian, hardening as the curse slowly claimed him.

Then, one day, Urvhak Skaar vanished without a trace.

In his absence, the other Demon Smiths hastily sought to secure their own domains and claim what remained of his legacy. Some say Skaar disappeared so that none would witness him finally succumb to the foul price of his magic, his body becoming a monument to his own hubris.

Others tell a different tale.

They claim that Urvhak Skaar rode forth upon his baleful taurus, Ghorramokh, a monstrous beast black iron and brass dark as onyx, and journeyed back into the hellish realities from which his forbidden knowledge had come. There, they say, he still scours the endless realms in search of a cure for the curse that consumes him.

Those Fel Dwarfs who believe this legend speculate that only by ascending to true godhood could Urvhak Skaar cast off the limitations of mortality and tear down the Father of Flame himself.

### Urvhak Skaar's Lair

Urvhak Skaar is said to have been a master of metal, who wrought a plethora of twisted monuments across the ashen landscape. These nightmarish forms stood as grim testaments to the artificer's unmatched craft and absolute authority over the wasteland. His fortresses were reputed to be nearly indestructible, their grim ramparts resisting not only siege engines but also the vain efforts of wizards and other spellcasters who sought to bypass them through magic. Some even claimed that Urvhak could reshape his architecture as effortlessly as a sculptor molds clay, causing his citadels to shift and transform at his command.

#### Lair Actions

When fighting within his lair, Urvhak Skaar's Challenge Rating increases by 2.

When Urvhak Skaar rolls initiative, his lair awakens to aid him. Until combat ends or Urvhak Skaar dies, the lair has an Intelligence of 22 and can act of its own accord, always acting in Urvhak Skaar's best interest. While active in this way, the lair is aware of the location of all creatures within it. On Initiative Count 20 (losing ties), either an axe made of fire or a hammer made of thunder appears at a point within the lair of the lair's choice. It cannot use the same weapon two rounds in a row.

The weapon flies in a straight line up to 100 feet in a direction the lair chooses before disappearing, disappearing early if it impacts a solid surface.

\columnbreak

 If the weapon would strike a creature, that creature must make a *Strength or Dexterity Saving Throw (its choice):* DC 20. <br>
*Failure:* The creature takes 14 (4d6) Thunder damage (if it is a hammer) or 14 (4d6) Fire damage (if it is an axe), and the weapon moves past. <br>
*Successful Dexterity Save:* The creature takes no damage and the weapon moves past. <br>
*Successful Strength Save:* The creature takes half damage and the weapon disappears.

#### Regional Effects

The area where Urvhak Skaar dwells is warped by his presence, creating one or more of the following effects everywhere within 5 miles of his lair:

- Stone, earth, and metal in the area can't be affected by spells of 4th level or lower cast by creatures other than Urvhak Skaar or creatures he designates.
- Creatures can't teleport through walls of iron unless they are Urvhak Skaar or creatures he designates. The iron also extends into the Ethereal Plane, blocking ethereal travel through the wall.
- Urvhak Skaar can perform a 10-minute ritual while in the area to become aware of all metal structures within it. He may then use *Passwall* with no range limit, choosing any point on a metal structure he is aware of as the target.

### Urvhak Skaar Tactics
#### General Strategy
* Urvhak Skaar uses Gift of Stone on a durable front-line target with high hit points and low Charisma, such as a barbarian or fighter.

* Once a target has been weakened, Urvhak Skaar uses Ruinous Torrents against:
    * Low-hit-point targets to finish them off, or
    * Targets with high Intelligence (such as wizards or artificers) to eliminate characters most capable of removing petrification or other debilitating effects.

#### Spell Priorities
##### Shard Storm
* Cast if it can affect three or more targets.
* Do not cast if already concentrating on Shard Storm.

##### Ash Storm
* Prioritize targets without any levels of petrification 

##### Divine Hammer
* Cast when it can hit multiple targets with petrification levels.
* These targets typically have reduced Dexterity from petrification, making them more likely to fail the saving throw and suffer the thunder damage.

#### Ghorramokh Tactics
* Ghorramokh aggressively charges the target that Urvhak Skaar is focusing with Ruinous Torrents.
* Ghorramokh shifts to enemies with levels of petrification, using Wave of Destruction to capitalize on their reduced Dexterity and vulnerability to thunder damage.

#### Lair Actions
The lair alternates between its two weapon effects each round:
* Round 1: Fire weapon
* Round 2: Thunder weapon

##### Fire Weapon
Aim it so it passes through as many targets as possible that do not have petrification levels

##### Thunder Weapon
Aim to hit as many targets as possible that already have petrification levels, exploiting their reduced Dexterity and increased vulnerability to thunder.

> ##### New Condition: Burning
> The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid. 
> * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
> * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
> * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Resolute
> The creature stubbornly digs in to weather any assault. It experiences the following effects:
>  * Attack rolls made against the creature have disadvantage.
>  * The creature can't take its move action on its turn.
> * The creature makes Strength checks and all saving throws with advantage, except Dexterity saving throws, which it makes with disadvantage.
> 
> The condition ends if an effect moves the creature against its will, knocks it prone, or the creature becomes charmed, frightened, or incapacitated. A creature that is prone, charmed, frightened, or incapacitated can't become Resolute.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Dazed 
> A dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

## New Spells

#### Ash Storm
*5th-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of soot)
- **Duration:**  Instantaneous
- **Classes:**  Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard

You unleash a wave of scalding ash in a 60-foot-long, 15-foot-wide line originating from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d10 fire damage, is pushed 15 feet away form you, and is blinded until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no other effect.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the fire damage increases by 1d10 and for each slot level above 4th.

#### Divine Hammer
*6th-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 150 ft
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Classes:**  Cleric

Choose a point you can see on the ground within range and a direction. A giant hammer manifests and slams the ground at the chosen point, impacting everything in a 10-foot cube centered on the point. It then sends a 30-foot-long, 10-foot-wide shockwave in the chosen direction before disappearing.

Each creature in the area of the hammer’s impact or shockwave must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d6 fire or lighting damage (your choice) and 5d6 thunder damage and is knocked prone; it is also dazed* until the end of its next turn if it is in the area of the impact. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked prone or dazed.

#### Shard Storm
*4th-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 50 feet (50-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
-  **Classes:**  Artificer

You send a wave of burning rock shards from your fingertips flying in a 50-foot Cone. Choose up to five creatures you can see in the cone. Make a ranged spell attack against each target. On hit, target takes 3d8 Piercing damage and a shard embeds itself in the creature. At the end of each of the creature's turns until the spell ends, it takes 2d8 Fire damage if the shard is still embedded.

#### Subjugation
*2nd-level enchantment*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 ft
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Classes:**  Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

You attempt to crush the will of one creature you can see within range. Constructs and undead are immune to this effect. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you until the spell ends. While frightened in this way, the target is also charmed by you.

The frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.

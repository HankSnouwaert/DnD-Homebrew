## Magma Dragons
*Cataclysmic Harbingers of World-Shattering Destruction*<br>
**Habitat:** Volcanic; **Treasure:** Any <br>
Magma dragons are among the most powerful creatures to stalk the mortal planes. They are among the mightiest and most destructive of all draconic species, possessing power surpassed only by the gods themselves. These colossal behemoths make their abodes within volcanoes and vast magma-filled caverns that lie deep beneath the earth's surface, where they slumber for centuries atop their treasure hoards.

The treasure hoards of magma dragons are the envy of all other dragons, for the immense wealth of precious metals and rare minerals they gather from the world's depths is so vast that even the greatest human vaults of riches are mere trinkets by comparison.

When magma dragons finally awaken from their long slumber and soar forth from their torrid domains, the world shudders at their passing. The cataclysmic power they unleash is capable of disfiguring entire landscapes and reducing forests to ash within moments. Nor do magma dragons take any sorrow in the breaking of the world; rather, they revel in their ability to shatter all around them with absolute impunity. Viewing all other living things as little more than insects to be swept aside, the only things that give such tyrants pause are creatures or objects capable of withstanding the brutal force of their might. Such defiance awakens a malicious wrath, and a magma dragon will stop at nothing to assert its dominance and grind its opposition into rubble.

Nearly nothing can stand against such living forces of destruction. Those who have heard the tales of dragons, their torrents of flame and steel-rending claws, and still choose to bear arms against such legendary beasts are hopelessly naïve to the danger they face. To stand before a magma dragon is to stand before a living catastrophe.

Sweltering heat and toxic fumes pour endlessly from the dragon's body, sapping the life from all who venture too close. Its mighty wings beat with such force that they unleash blinding streams of ash that sting the eyes and crackle with lightning. Its immense bulk causes the earth to split and tremble, while thunderous shockwaves crush anything caught within their devastating wake. Claws sharper than obsidian cleave through the strongest metals and solid stone alike, tearing apart even the most heavily armored and resilient foes.

Greatest of all, however, is its breath. A magma dragon can exhale a torrent of annihilation: a cataclysmic flood of lava, lightning, fire, and toxic ash that surges across the land, sealing the doom of all in its path. The oldest of these dragons are said to be capable of engulfing an entire village with a single bellow.

### Magma Dragon Tactics
#### Opening Turn
The Magma Dragon begins combat by using its Breath Weapon, positioning it to hit as many targets as possible. It only uses Draconic Resistance if the effect causes it to be unable to attack effectively.

It prioritizes creatures with low mobility or limited ability to reposition, allowing it to stack Molten Wounds on the same targets over multiple rounds.

##### Initiative Count 21
The Magma Dragon uses the ability that can affect the greatest number of targets.
If multiple abilities would hit the same number of targets, it chooses in the following order:
* Ash and Lightning if it can hit at least one target with 2 or more Molten Wounds.
* Molten Snare if there is at least one additional target with low Dexterity that can be caught in the effect.
* Otherwise, use whichever available ability deals the most damage or provides the greatest battlefield control.

##### Initiative Count 1
The Magma Dragon uses Shattering Quake unless:
* It cannot hit two or more targets, or
* There is a target with 3 or more Molten Wounds that has not gained a Molten Wound this turn. In that case, the dragon prioritizes applying another Molten Wound to that target instead.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Aura of Sweltering Heat.*** The dragon emits an aura of heat in a 15-foot emanation from itself. Each creature not immune to Fire that is within the emanation at the end of the dragon's turn must make a *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 25. <br>
> *Failure:* The creature takes 14 (4d6) Fire damage and is Poisoned until the end of its next turn. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* The creature also gains one level of Fatigue*. <br>
> If the dragon takes 15 or more Cold damage, this trait doesn't function until the end of the dragon's next turn.
>
> ***Draconic Power.*** The dragon has 3 charges. It gains a charge at the start of each of its turns, up to a maximum of 3 charges.
>
> ***Draconic Resistance.*** If the dragon fails a saving throw, it can spend a charge and choose to succeed instead.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes two Rend attacks.
>
> ***Rend.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 26 (3d10 + 10) Slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it receives a Molten Wound. For each Molten Wound a creature has, its AC is lowered by 1 and it suffers a -1 penalty to all Constitution saving throws. This effect can't reduce a creature's AC below 1. At the end of the creature's turn, if it hasn't received a Molten Wound since its last turn, it loses all Molten Wounds.
>
> ***Volcanic Breath (3 Charges).*** The dragon exhales volcanic gas in a 120-foot cone. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 26, each creature in the cone. <br>
> *Failure:* The target takes 36 (8d8) Poison damage, 36 (8d8) Fire damage, and 36 (8d8) Lightning damage, and gains one level of Fatigue. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* The target gains two levels of Fatigue instead. <br>
> *Success:* The target takes half damage only.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Dread Presence.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature of the dragon's choice within 120 feet of it that is aware of it. <br>
> *Failure:* The target is Frightened for 1 minute. While Frightened, the creature can't cast spells other than cantrips. It can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* While Frightened, the creature can't take reactions or Bonus Actions and must use the Dash action on its turn to move away from the dragon by the safest route. <br>
> *Success:* The target is immune to the dragon's Dread Presence for the next hour.
>
> ### Monstrous Actions
>
> The dragon gains two additional actions in combat, each performed on its own initiative count, separate from the dragon's: one performed on initiative count 21 and the other on initiative count 1. On each initiative count (losing ties), the dragon chooses one of the available actions for that count if it is not Incapacitated. It can replace any action with Tenacious Will, even if Incapacitated.
>
> ***Ash and Lightning (Initiative Count 21).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 150-foot-long, 20-foot-wide line originating from the dragon. <br>
> *Failure:* The target takes 21 (6d6) Lightning damage, is pushed back 30 feet, loses concentration, and is Blinded until Initiative Count 21 of the next round. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* The target is Paralyzed until Initiative Count 21 of the next round. <br>
> *Success:* The target takes half damage only.
>
> ***Molten Snare (Initiative Count 21).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within 120 feet of the dragon. <br>
> *Failure:* The target takes 21 (6d6) Fire damage and is Restrained by hardening rock. A creature can use an action to make a DC 25 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing itself or a creature within reach from the rock on a success. The rock has AC 18 and 30 hit points, and is immune to Fire, Poison, and Psychic damage. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* The target also receives a Molten Wound. <br>
> *Success:* The target takes half damage only.
>
> ***Pounce (Initiative Count 1).*** The dragon moves up to half its speed and makes one Rend attack.
>
> ***Shattering Quake (Initiative Count 1).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 25, each creature in a 30-foot Cube originating from the dragon. <br>
> *Failure:* The target takes 21 (6d6) Thunder damage and is knocked Prone. <br>
> *Failure by 10+:* The target can't stand up until the end of its next turn. <br>
> *Success:* The target takes half damage only.
>
> ***Tenacious Will.*** If the dragon is subject to an effect that allows a saving throw at the start or end of its turn, it can immediately make that saving throw with advantage, ending the effect on a success. This action can be taken even while Incapacitated.

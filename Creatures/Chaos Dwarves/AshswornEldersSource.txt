## Ashsworn Elders
*Dwarven Clerics Serving Fiery Gods of Tyranny*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Mountain, Underdark, Urban, Volcanic; **Treasure:** Relics <br>

Within the foreboding depths of the dark iron cathedrals, all manner of atrocious sacrifices, iniquitous spells, and blasphemous prayers are evoked. These rites are performed so that the dread gods of the world’s deepest recesses may be communed with, allowing the faithful to bargain for even the smallest portion of their unholy magics.

The wretched practitioners who dwell within these accursed halls are the Fel Dwarf Ashsworn. Within the Fel Dwarf empire, they are the ones who hoard the most foul rites and incantations, keeping the altars of their avaricious deities placated with meat, bone, and souls. The Ashsworn rank second in the Fel Dwarf hierarchy, standing just below the demon smiths. Though both orders clash constantly as they vie for power and status within Fel Dwarf society’s ruthless ranks.

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br>

Unlike their demon smith brethren, the Ashsworn do not rely on blasphemous ingenuity to invent new weapons and spells. Instead, they beseech their hellish masters directly through dark hymns and blood rituals, pleading for raw might. That power becomes terrifyingly evident when these clerics leave their temples and march onto the fields of war. With a guttural cry and the raising of their onyx stone hammers, they draw forth the burning blood of the earth and hurl it at their foes, searing away skin and flesh in an agonizing death.

So dreadful are these conduits of destruction and suffering that even fellow Fel Dwarfs fear to fall before them. In their presence, warriors have been known to withstand wounds that would have slain them otherwise. Yet, just like the demon smiths, such malign practices come at a terrible cost. The magics of their heartless masters slowly turn the Ashsworn’s bodies to stone, trapping them eventually in a living prison of rock.

### Fel Dwarf Ash Sworn Tactics

The primary goal of an Ashsworn is to secure a position where they can become Resolute, increasing the damage of their spell attacks and activating their Aura of Fanaticism, which bolsters nearby Fel Dwarfs.

Once established, the Ashsworn employs the following spells according to battlefield conditions:

**Magmatic Stone.**
Used when enemies are beyond the range of the Ashsworn's other offensive spells. The Ashsworn targets the front of an enemy formation whenever possible, increasing the likelihood that the storm created by the spell will pass through additional targets and hinder the enemy's advance.

**Bellows Breath.**
Bellows Breath is used as soon as two or more enemy creatures are within range. Friendly Fel Dwarfs will often position themselves to be affected by the breath as well, allowing them to benefit from its effects. This spell is particularly effective when combined with Shard Storm, as it enhances the damage dealt by subsequent attacks.

**Shard Storm.**
Shard Storm is typically cast after Bellows Breath whenever it can strike two or more enemies within range. The spell takes advantage of the bonuses granted by Bellows Breath, allowing the Ash Sworn to maximize its damage against clustered foes.

\columnbreak

> ##### New Condition: Resolute
> The creature stubbornly digs in to weather any assault. It experiences the following effects:
>  * Attack rolls made against the creature have disadvantage.
>  * The creature can't take its move action on its turn.
> * The creature makes Strength checks and all saving throws with advantage, except Dexterity saving throws, which it makes with disadvantage.
> 
> The condition ends if an effect moves the creature against its will, knocks it prone, or the creature becomes charmed, frightened, or incapacitated. A creature that is prone, charmed, frightened, or incapacitated can't become Resolute.

### New Spells

#### Bellows Breath
*5th-level Evocation (Artificer, Cleric, Paladin)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self (50-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You breathe out the fires of creation, reforging your allies and searing your foes. Each creature in a 40-foot Cone must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 3d10 Fire damage and is Tempered until the end of your next turn. While Tempered, the creature takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage whenever it is hit by an attack.

You may choose up to three creatures within the area to automatically succeed on this saving throw, taking no damage. You and those creatures are instead Heated until the end of your next turn. While Heated, whenever a creature hits another creature with an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 Fire damage on that hit.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The initial damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 5th.

#### Magmatic Stone
*3rd-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 100 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of igneous rock)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You conjure a molten rock and hurl it at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 4d6 Bludgeoning damage. Whether the attack hits or misses, the rock explodes on impact, creating a 15-foot-radius sphere of whirling magma centered on the point of impact. Its area is Lightly Obscured and Difficult Terrain. The sphere spreads around corners and seeps through openings at least 5 feet in diameter.

At the start of your next turn, the sphere moves 30 feet in the direction you hurled it and dissipates. Any creature in the sphere when it first appears, and any creature whose space the sphere enters or passes through, must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 4d6 Fire damage. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Bludgeoning and Fire damage both increase by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

\columnbreak

#### Shard Storm
*4th-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 50 feet (50-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
-  **Classes:**  Artificer

You send a wave of burning rock shards from your fingertips flying in a 50-foot Cone. Choose up to five creatures you can see in the cone. Make a ranged spell attack against each target. On hit, target takes 3d8 Piercing damage and a shard embeds itself in the creature. At the end of each of the creature's turns until the spell ends, it takes 2d8 Fire damage if the shard is still embedded.

**Removing the Shard.** A creature within reach can use an action to make a Wisdom (Medicine) check against your spell save DC to remove the shard. On a success, the shard is removed. On a failure, the shard is still removed, but the creature takes 1d8 Slashing damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You may target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.

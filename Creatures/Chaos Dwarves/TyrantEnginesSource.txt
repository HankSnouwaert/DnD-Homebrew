## Tyrant Engines
*Demonic Constructs Wielding Cruel Maces & Fiery Cannons*<br>
**Habitat:** Desert, Mountain, Underdark, <br> Urban, Volcanic; **Treasure:** Armaments <br>
 
Tyrant engines are insidious automatons brought to life by fel dwarf artificers through binding the very contemptuous thoughts of their dark patrons within the brass and iron frames of these inventions. Born from such hatred, these machines possess an innate urge to inflict pain and crush hope wherever their heavy metal frames tread. Even their makers have little control over their actions when they encounter a foe filled with courage, for such resolve is something they long to snuff out.

Their hulking metallic bodies are adorned with sculpted bull heads and possess the strength to send foes flying through the air, broken and limp. Even if such an impact does not outright kill an adversary, the tyrant engine carries a variety of murderous implements with which to finish its quarry.

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br>

The greatest chance one has when facing such a manufactured avatar lies in the machine's unstable design. The animus that drives it also slowly consumes its internal workings. Should the artificial demon sustain significant damage, its frame can catastrophically fail as the malignant essence within breaks free and tears through the rest of the construct's body.

### Tyrant Engine - Bane Mace
Tyrant engines animated by the dark will to dominate and control are armed with immense maces. However, the construct's eyes are the feature one must be most wary of. These robotic warriors bear two blazing gems that burn with baleful power. When those hexstones become fixed upon a living soul, they sap away all determination, leaving the victim unable to raise their weapon against the foul spawn, or even freezing them completely in place. Only for the blackened mace of the tyrant engine come crashing through their rigid body.

### Tyrant Engine - Immolation Cannons
Tyrant engines driven by an overwhelming urge to destroy are fitted with massive cannons in place of their arms. Acting as living artillery, they unleash torrents of hellish fire that bathe the battlefield in flame. Such constructs become relentlessly fixated upon specific targets once battle is joined, stopping at nothing until their chosen victim has suffered an agonizing death and been reduced to nothing more than cinders.

\columnbreak

#### Tyrant Engine - Immolation Cannons Tactics
The tyrant engine marks a target that is surrounded by allies, then begins bombarding that target with its Immolation Cannons as it advances. It fights especially well alongside allies that are resistant or immune to fire damage, or that absorb fire damage. These allies should help by knocking the marked target prone.

> ##### New Condition: Burning
> The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid.
> * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
> * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
> * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Obsessed 
> The creature becomes fixated on a creature or object, which becomes a source of its obsession. Being obsessed has the following effects on a creature:
> * While the creature can see a source of its obsession, it can't target any creature or object with an attack, spell, or ability except for a source of its obsession.
> * While the creature can see a source of its obsession, it can't cast spells or use abilities that target an area (such as the *fireball* spell) unless a source of its obsession is in that area. 
> *  The creature can't willingly move away from a source of its obsession.

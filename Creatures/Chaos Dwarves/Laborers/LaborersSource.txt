## Laborers
*Enslaved Wretches Press Ganged Into Service*<br>
**Habitat:** Any; **Treasure:** None <br>
The vast and dark industries of the fel dwarfs are fueled by the sweat and blood of laborers, accrued through long and pitiless campaigns by the cruel, bearded raiders as they sack countless kingdoms for the resources needed to sustain their hellish society.

Numbers untold, drawn from many different cultures and races, suffer and toil in volcanic mines, slowly poisoned by the heated air until life is drained from them, while others work in titanic factories on hazardous assembly lines, building iron war machines as they wither away. Many are simply sacrificed to blasphemous gods to earn their masters’ favor and devotion, their souls becoming playthings for infernal beings for the rest of time.

Whenever war comes to the fel dwarfs, the brutal fel dwarves rally their vast hordes of slaves and harshly press them into service. These unwilling conscripts are driven headlong into the oncoming forces, armed with nothing more than rusted pickaxes and battered wrenches, forced to act as living walls to blunt the enemy’s assault, and those foes of the fel dwarfs who possess any morality may pause when faced with such broken men and women.

Such wretched souls face a grim choice: death at the hands of the advancing warriors, or unspeakable torment at the hands of their dwarf overseers.

> ##### Alternative Masters
> The Malign Authority trait leads laborers to work well with fel dwarves. However, if this doesn't make sense in your setting, feel free to modify this trait to utilize a different creature type that can serve as the laborers' dark masters. Other creatures could include hobgoblins, drow, devils, evil dragons, or fire giants. Any creature willing to press slaves into their service will work.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Tormentor's Authority.*** The laborer has advantage on Wisdom saving throws as long as there is an allied dwarf with a higher CR within 30 feet that is not Incapacitated, Charmed, or Frightened. Otherwise, the laborer has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Tool.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) Piercing damage, or 5 (2d4) Piercing damage if there is an allied dwarf with a higher CR within 30 feet that is not Incapacitated, Charmed, or Frightened.

## Racial Traits for NPCs

The following are racial traits for monsters with no specific race listed in their stat block. They differ from player traits, as player traits may not be helpful for a monster in a single combat encounter. This does make creatures more dangerous, though not enough to increase their CR.

##### Dwarf
- ***Dwarven Resilience.*** The dwarf has Resistance to Poison damage.
- ***Squat.*** The dwarf's speed is reduced by 5 feet.
- ***Resolute Stance.*** At the start of the creature's turn, the dwarf can use its Bonus Action to become Resolute until the start of its next turn.
- Darkvision 60 ft.

##### Human
- ***Determined.*** The human gets +2 to all saving throws.

##### Human, Northern
- ***Survivor.*** When the human becomes Bloodied, for the next minute it gains advantage on all saving throws and gains 10 temporary hit points at the start of each of its turns.

##### Elf, Dark
- ***Fey Ancestry.*** The elf has advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic can't put it to sleep.
- ***Murderous Prowess.*** When the elf attacks a creature, if the target has three or more Wounds* or is Bloodied, it can reroll one of the d20 dice for the attack roll.
- Darkvision 60 ft.

> ##### New Condition: Resolute
> The creature stubbornly digs in to weather any assault. It experiences the following effects:
> * Attack rolls made against the creature have disadvantage.
> * The creature can't take its move action on its turn.
> * The creature makes Strength checks and all saving throws with advantage, except Dexterity saving throws, which it makes with disadvantage.
> 
> The condition ends if an effect moves the creature against its will, knocks it prone, or the creature becomes charmed, frightened, or incapacitated. A creature that is prone, charmed, frightened, or incapacitated can't become Resolute.

<br>

##### Elf, High
- ***Fey Ancestry.*** The elf has advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic can't put it to sleep.
- ***Martial Prowess.*** When the elf attacks a creature, as long as the elf is not Bloodied or the elf has ally within 5 feet of it that is not Incapacitated and not Bloodied, it can reroll one of the d20 dice for the attack roll.
- Darkvision 60 ft.

##### Elf, Forest
- ***Fey Ancestry.*** The elf has advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic can't put it to sleep.
- ***Wild Prowess.*** When the elf attacks a creature, if it has advantage on the attack roll or the target is standing on difficult terrain made of plants, it can reroll one of the d20 dice for the attack roll.
- Darkvision 60 ft.

##### Fel Goblin
- ***Back Stabber.*** The fel goblin has advantage on opportunity attacks. Creatures provoke opportunity attacks from the fel goblin even if they take the Disengage action before leaving its reach, though the attack made against a creature this way has disadvantage instead of advantage.

##### Goblin
- ***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a Bonus Action on each of its turns.

##### Orc
- ***Aggressive.*** The orc can use its Bonus Action to move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.
- ***Chopper.*** The first time the creature attacks on its turn, it deals an extra 4 (1d8) damage on a hit.
- Darkvision 60 ft.

##### Ratfolk
- ***Overwhelming Mass.*** When the ratfolk hits a creature with an attack, it deals an extra 2 (1d4) damage per ally it has within 5 feet of its target that isn't Incapacitated, up to three allies.
- ***Scurry Away.*** At the start of its turn, the ratfolk can choose to become Frightened of a creature it can see until the start of its next turn (ignoring immunity to the Frightened condition) and move up to half its speed away from that creature without provoking opportunity attacks. While Frightened this way, it can only take the Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Hide action.
- ***Crevice Crawler.*** The ratfolk can move through and stop in a space large enough for a creature one size smaller than it without squeezing.
- Darkvision 60 ft.




##### Skink
- ***Cold Blooded.*** The skink has advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened.
- ***Jungle Traversal.*** The skink has a Swim speed of 30 ft. and a Climb speed of 25 ft.
- ***Hold Breath.*** The skink can hold its breath for 1 hour.
- ***Primeval Mask.*** The skink has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide using foliage or a creature larger than it, and may do so even if it is only lightly obscured while hiding this way.
- Darkvision 30 ft.

Along the worn-out and forgotten roads of this dismal of some of this cursed province, large wagons lumber through muddy trails, their movements accompanied by the cries of rotten wood and rusted metal, mixed with a cacophony of guttural moans. The appearance of these caravans is far more loathsome still. Decaying timber and weathered iron are haphazardly cobbled together, forming a visage akin to a rib cage facing skyward, with heavy treads fixed to its sides. Rancid flesh is heaped upon the structure, wreathing it in a chorus of wails and reaching out attempt grab at those unfortunate enough to draw too close. At the front, rows of mangled corpses are impaled and bound to the vehicle, straining as they march forward, dragging the nightmarish display behind them.
Sitting atop the entire revolting caravan is a figure, sallow and gaunt, shrouded in heaps of decaying linen and cloth. Its face is distorted by nothing but insanity.
Such sights are commonly known as corpse carts, and they are magnets for necrotic energy, luring in weak-willed undead who are driven to follow these wagons as moths are drawn to a flame. The undead that join these profane processions are bolstered by the dark power that suffuses them, granting them unnatural brio.
Some corpse carts possess other horrid artifacts fused into their twisted frameworks. Fires that burn with evil magic shed an azure light that debilitates all spells around them. Others are hung with black bells that, when rung, command undead sinew and bone to stitch themselves back together, undoing all manner of wounds inflicted upon them. Perhaps most gruesome of all are the banners fashioned from scraps of brightly colored skin. These sway and hypnotize any mortal who gazes upon the sickening streamers, drawing them ever closer.
The origins of these carts remain unknown, though many towns have their own local legends about the grim nomads that lurk throughout the countryside, tales passed down through generations.
Some speak of a rampant plague that scoured the land many ages ago. Thousands were claimed by the pestilence, their fetid remains filling the streets and roads. It was said that hundreds of wagons were deployed by the state to gather the contaminated cadavers. However, such ghastly work was said to attract only the most desperate individuals. None of sound mind would dare to crew such carts and risk contracting the fatal sickness, even for the heftiest sums of money.
The unstable men carried out the unsavory work, gathering up the deceased in ramshackle  carts bringing their amassed piles to great charnel pits to burn the tainted bodies. However such crews were burned alongside their grisly deliveries due to their exposure to virulent pathogens and Scrupulous nobility to not have waste coin in such trying times.
Some, it was said, escaped being set ablaze. Stricken with fever and driven into a frenzy, they fled into the wilderness, clacking as they hauled their pox-covered troves behind them as though they were heaps of precious metal.
It is unlikely, however, that the corpse carts of today are the same mobs of lunatics that slipped into the wilds centuries ago. For that would mean these decrepit contraptions had lurched onward in a state of perpetual decay for hundreds of years.
Such a notion is preposterous.
The Corpse Cart: Tactics
The Corpse Cart excels at controlling the battlefield by grappling enemies, knocking them prone, and holding them in place. Meanwhile, the Corpse Masters can grant other undead the Quickened condition, allowing them to capitalize on grappled and prone targets.
Corpse Masters are extremely fragile and must be protected by the Corpse Cart and the other undead. Use Dance of Death on the Corpse Cart during the first turn of combat to help it close the distance quickly. Once in range, use the spell to target undead allies capable of dealing the most damage with their additional attacks.
The Corpse Cart: Upgrades
Spell Bane Fire
This upgrade reduces the threat posed by enemy spellcasters, making them less of a priority for the undead to target. This allows your forces to focus on more immediate threats while the upgrade helps mitigate the danger posed by enemy magic.
Unholy Lodestone
This upgrade allows Dance of Death to help keep your undead allies alive. It can also be used on the Corpse Cart itself if it becomes bloodied and loses access to its additional attack, helping it remain effective in combat.
Entrancing Banner of Skin
This upgrade helps the Corpse Cart close the distance against ranged enemies. The charm effect can create an opportunity for the Corpse Cart to approach and grapple its target, potentially preventing the enemy from escaping even after the charm ends.

The corpse cart can have any of the following upgrades. If it does, its CR is increased by 1.

***Spell Bane Fire.*** The corpse cart emits an aura in a 60-foot emanation from itself. Whenever a hostile creature attempts to cast a non-necromancy spell while within the aura, that creature must make a *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13. <br>
*Failure:* The spell is dampened and becomes weakened until the start of the creature's next turn. While weakened, attack rolls made by the spell have disadvantage, targets have advantage on saving throws made to resist the spell, and if the spell allows a creature to make a saving throw to take only half damage, a successful save causes no damage and a failed save causes half damage instead. <br>
*Failure by 10+:* The spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot or daily use, the slot or use is expended.

***Unholy Lodestone.*** The corpse cart emits an aura in a 60-foot emanation from itself. Whenever an allied undead creature is targeted by a necromancy spell while within the aura, it regains 10 hit points.

***Entrancing Banner of Skin.*** The corpse cart emits an aura in a 60-foot emanation from itself. Whenever a hostile creature starts its turn within the aura, it must make a *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 13. <br>
*Failure:* The creature is Charmed by the corpse cart until the start of its next turn. While Charmed, it can't willingly move away from the corpse cart. <br>
*Failure by 10+:* While Charmed, the target is Incapacitated and ignores the Entrancing Banner of Skin of other corpse carts. If the target is more than 5 feet from the corpse cart, it moves on its turn toward the corpse cart by the most direct route, trying to get within 5 feet of it. It doesn't avoid opportunity attacks; however, before moving into damaging terrain (such as lava or a pit) and whenever it takes damage from a source other than the corpse cart, the target repeats the saving throw. <br>
*Success:* The creature is immune to this aura for the next hour.


#### Dance of Death
*1st-level Necromancy (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

Threads of dark power leap from your fingers to an Undead creature you can see within range. Until the start of your next turn, the target's Speed increases by 20 feet and it becomes Quickened*.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional Undead creature for each slot level above 1st.

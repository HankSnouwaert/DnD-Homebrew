## Fel Bats
*Giant Bloodsucking Bats Warped By Unholy Energies*<br>
**Habitat:**  Forest, Grassland, Hills, Jungle, Mountain, Swamp, Underdark; **Treasure:** None <br>
Ferocious fel bats are the product of ordinary bats that hunt and consume prey in realms polluted by unholy power. Their bodies swell from the abundant blighted flesh and tainted blood they greedily devour. The name “fel bat” is misleading, for these beasts share as many similarities with an ordinary bat as a house cat does with a wild lion.


Their corded limbs and muscle are covered in reddish-grey skin, patched with mangy, coarse hair. Their blotched leather wings stretch longer than a man’s arm. Two small, glowing yellow orbs burn on their stub-snouted faces, and their maws can unhinge to an unnatural length, revealing rows of stained, nail-sized teeth.

These creatures possess superb hunting abilities. They soar through the night sky in complete silence, striking isolated targets with horrific precision. They latch on with jagged fangs, blood gushing and a horrid gurgling breaking the quiet.

Many vampire lords keep several of these distorted creatures as pets, a dark parody of the way mortal royalty keep falcons. They send the airborne nightmares out to run down potential prey for their masters. The masters are not without fondness for their companions, however, and ensure that the juiciest cuts from the newly apprehended meal are given to the gore-hungry fiends.

### Fel Bat Tactics
Fel bats are fragile and must be careful not to be overwhelmed by opponents. The bat attacks isolated targets; it will attach to the target if it thinks it can have at least a full round without being engaged in melee by another opponent, at which point it will feed.

> ##### New Condition: Wounds
>While most creatures are wounded in combat, in this context, the wound is a special form of injury that causes severe bleeding over a short period of time and only specialized attacks and effects can cause wounds. A creature can suffer from multiple wounds at once and at the end of its turn, a wounded creature takes 2 (1d4) necrotic damage for every wound it has sustained. Only creatures with blood (or something equivalent to blood) can suffer wounds. Wounds can be removed in the following ways:
>
> * When a creature is healed, one wound is removed for every 5 hit points of healing it receives. 
> * A creature can expend one use of a healer's kit to remove two wounds from itself or another creature. 
> * A wounded creature, or another creature within 5 feet of it, can make a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check as an action to remove one wound on a success. For every 5 points by which the check exceeds the DC, an additional wound is removed.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Echolocation.*** The bat can't use its Blindsight while Deafened.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit (advantage if the target has three or more Wounds* or is Bloodied), reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is Small or larger, the bat can Attach to it. Otherwise, it can Grapple the target (escape DC 12). While Attached or Grappled, the bat can't make Bite attacks against other targets, and the target has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. 
>
> The bat can detach itself by spending 5 feet of its movement. It also detaches if it is Incapacitated, if it is pushed or pulled, or if a creature grapples it.
>
> ***Feed.*** One target the bat is Attached to or Grappling takes 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage and receives three Wounds.

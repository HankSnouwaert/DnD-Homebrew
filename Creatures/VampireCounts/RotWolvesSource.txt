## Deathmaw Wolves
Few undead are as efficient hunters as the voracious Deathmaw Wolves—creatures fashioned from the fetid remains of slain dire wolves. Their fur is now matted and coated in all manner of decayed filth. Rotting flesh and worn bone can be seen through gashes and holes, while their skulls glow with a faint, unholy energy. Noisome, damp breath leaks from their ossified jaws.

These are far more than mere animated corpses of hunting beasts. The soul and will of the creature are drawn back into its withered shell and perverted by the iniquitous magic that raised it turning into a true unholy monster. Whereas most undead, such as zombies, lack the speed or wit they possessed in life, Deathmaw Wolves exceed their living counterparts in every way. Their sickening limbs drive them to gallop at a startling pace, delivering bites that drag a full grown house off its hooves. They stalk with a cunning far greater than they had in life and possess a cruelty that drives them to torment those they chase. They can even outwit hunting parties of the most seasoned warriors. When the opportunity arises, these diabolic creatures capitalize and deliver fatal attacks.

How such scions of death are constructed remains a mystery, though it is said they were raised in the service of vampire lords, who kept entire packs ready to harry their foes. Much as a mortal duke or lord might keep domesticated hunting hounds, the vampires command these beasts. At their masters’ word, tainted claws scar the earth as the unholy throng of killers races through the night, howls ringing from savage carcasses to proclaim the bloodshed yet to come.

### Deathmaw Wolves
Some rot wolves grow to become even more dangerous and horrific than the rest of their kin. Sometimes this is the result of absorbing ungodly amounts of dark magic, which warps and mutates their rotting forms. Other times, these are rot wolves that became a necromancer's or vampire's favored pet, groomed by their master and regularly fed the choicest victims. Regardless of origin, these gruesome hounds swell in size, strength, and intelligence, their jaws twisting into new deadly forms, helping them to bring down ever greater prey and earning them the name: Deathmaw Wolves. Their newfound cunning also lends them to assuming the role of pack leader among other rot wolves, though particularly powerful lords of undeath may command entire packs of deathmaw wolves who move with enough speed to catch the fastest prey and rip and tear with enough unholy strength to shred even the hardiest victims.

### Rot Wolf Tactics
Deathmaws move fast to take down low-strength targets. The wolves’ bonus damage against prone targets, combined with advantage from Pack Tactics, lets them destroy most targets very quick

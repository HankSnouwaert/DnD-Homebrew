## Reapers
*Spectral Scythe-Wielding  Avatars of Death*<br>
**Habitat:**  Planar (Shadowfell), Underdark; <br> **Treasure:** Arcane <br>

### Flame Reapers

Tearing their way into the mortal realm from the bowels of the underworld on bewitched nights, Flame Reapers seek out the souls of the wicked to ferry them away to their umbral abodes. Few know the true nature of these Flame Reapers, though they share much in common with Cairn Reapers; both possess immaterial bodies and wield scythes to cut down their quarry. Yet Flame Reapers are easily distinguished by the emerald flames that wreathe them, ceaselessly smoldering along with their seething weapons, which leave nothing but ashes and souls in their wake. These displaced essences are added to the Reaper’s own macabre pyre that surrounds it.

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br>

It is said that Flame Reapers ride out from the nether at the command of the gods of death, sent to deliver the spirits of those who have displeased such lords. At other times, they are beckoned into the living world by great necromancers or vampires, serving as ethereal assassins. No matter the nature of their master, once an otherworldly rider is given its task, doom is certain for the chosen victim. The Flame Reaper knows precisely where its quarry is, no matter where they flee, and no fortress rampart or barred gate will halt its phantom stride.


Eventually, a sweep of its infernal blade brings a fate worse than death. Spectral figures crackle with sick delight as they ride away, green fire ablaze in the dead of night, leaving only a path of blackened soot as a sign of its passing.

### Frost Reapers

There are rare cases where a Flame Reaper is unable to carry out its designated execution, and the assigned target escapes its intended fate of eternal torment through magical wards or potent weapons imbued with holy boons. The hideous mirth of the lonesome shade ends as the flames that waft from it are snuffed out, and the creature dissolves into black shadows and sparks. Such victories are short-lived, for it is said that upon the dispatching of a Flame Reaper, an even more lethal agent is sent in its place.

The Frost Reaper does not emit a shimmer of fire, but is instead swathed in a cloak of frigid mist. The skeletal rider chills the air around it, and its scythe is coated in black frost that transmutes flesh to ice with every strike of the cursed blade. 

\columnbreak

<br><br>

It is unknown how these gelid undead are spawned. Some claim they are created through rituals performed by the most powerful practitioners of dark magic, and that the most essential component is the lingering, vengeful essence of a Flame Reaper that has been destroyed and failed to carry out its murderous objective. The unholy knight’s bitter state allows it to reform with the aid of the unholy power supplied by its masters, ready to deliver torment to its would-be slayer.

For vengeance is a blade best swung cold.


### Flame & Frost Reaper Tactics

These Reapers prioritize Precision Attack whenever they can, unless they can hit three or more targets with another option. They achieve this by focusing on lone targets or by staying just outside melee range. Using a mount that takes the Disengage action is an effective way to maintain this positioning.

### Cairn Reapers

Cairn Reapers are avatars of woe, ethereal figures that hover in robes of shadow, hanging suspended in the air. Their ebon forms move with startling speed and pass through solid barriers as if they were little more than a veil of fog. Withered, yellowed skeletal hands grip scythes as long as a man. These macabre implements phase through flesh without leaving gashes, instead consuming all vitality and cleaving into the victim’s very soul.

Beneath the cowl of its hood, an ossified face of death peers forth: a skull that was once human, its features exaggerated and twisted into a nightmare visage. The presence of a Cairn Reaper is so ghastly that all within its vicinity are filled with dread. Even the most stalwart champions find their minds bound in a trance of terror, unable to move or feel as the murderous harbinger closes in.

\columnbreak

<br><br>

It is said that Cairn Reapers were once primitive shamans and bards, among the earliest magic-users. They used their arts to peer into the world beyond and performed rituals that inspired their fellow tribesmen, who regarded these practitioners with a mixture of awe and fear. In those days, the arcane arts were young, and few understood the true nature or dangers of the esoteric forces they sought to master.

Some pushed the boundaries of that power. A handful discovered they could extend their lives through the emotions cultivated in their bardic disciplines, living for decades or even centuries. Yet such habits preserved only the spirit of the storytellers; their bodies withered steadily as the years passed. In time, the decayed sages became as insubstantial as the stories and emotions they preached, trapped between the world of the living and the dead.

Their present state has degraded their minds until all they can think of is sustaining their shadowed existence. To that end, a Cairn Reaper seeks to extract the only feelings it can still cultivate from mortals: sadness and fear. They bind themselves to places where grief hangs heavy, scavenging the lingering emotions that saturate the air.



\columnbreak

Many ghost tales are told around the fire of a Cairn Reaper appearing on moonless nights. Soon the sounds of slaughter ring through the darkness as men and women, frozen in fright, are cut down, their souls claimed by the shroud-knave. Most accounts end with only a single soul escaping the Reaper’s sickle. Some speculate this is by design; the foul wraith wishes the grim accounts of its deeds to be retold so that it may glut itself on the alarm such stories evoke.

### Cairn Reaper Tactics

The Cairn Reaper uses Dread Presence to keep targets separated from one another. By positioning itself between enemies, it creates opportunities to use Precision Attack. Targets standing next to an ally (allowing Arcing Swing) are especially high-priority.  

It uses Precision Attack whenever possible, switching to Whirlwind Attack only if Precision Attack is unavailable or if three or more targets can be hit by Whirlwind Attack.

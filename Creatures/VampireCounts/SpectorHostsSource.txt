## Specter Hosts

*Swarms of Malevolent Spirits*<br>
**Habitat:**  Planar (Shadowfell), Underdark, Urban; <br> **Treasure:** None <br>


When the souls of the dead attempt to depart their mortal forms and pass through the veil into the next life, those in regions blighted by dark magic may find their passage barred. Corrupting mystical energies trap them between worlds, transforming them into insubstantial entities capable of passing through solid matter. Should enough spirits be denied entry to the afterlife, they begin to coalesce into a spectral host: a mortifying storm of discarnate undead.


\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br>


Figures of swirling, marble-grey mist twist and writhe through the night sky in great clusters, drifting aimlessly until they sense the warmth of the living. At once, they descend upon their prey, plunging through the air in chilling streaks. Their ghostly hands reach into living flesh, sapping the victim's vitality in mere seconds.
columnbreak Physical weapons prove futile against the life-draining assault of these poltergeists, and only powerful holy prayers and divine rites offer any hope of quelling such onslaughts.

>

There are even tales of gatherings so vast that they blot out the moon, as the otherworldly masses march across the heavens like rivers of pale vapor. Descending upon the earth, they sweep over villages and hamlets, overwhelming those who have huddled within their homes, trusting in wards fashioned from sacred trinkets and blessings bestowed through divine incantations. From behind their windows, the frightened townsfolk watch as a sea of the damned surges past, distorted faces forming in the swirling vapor, their ghostly wails echoing through the night.

\columnbreak

 Only when the procession has passed into the darkness beyond do the beleaguered villagers dare to hope that the worst has passed.

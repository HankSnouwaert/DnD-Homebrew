## Banshees
*Wailing Harbingers of Death*<br>
**Habitat:**  Any; **Treasure:** Relics <br>

Among the many unnatural sounds of the undead (the endless groaning of zombies, the clatter of bones as skeletal soldiers march, the snarl of a ghoul catching the scent of rotting meat), none is more feared than the wail of the banshee.

In life, these spirits were often bards, performers, or other women of great charisma and magical ability. Many possessed the power to take on the emotional burdens of others, siphoning away grief, melancholy, and sorrow so that the afflicted might find relief. Such women became essential to funeral rites. They would appear before grieving assemblies and draw the community’s pain into themselves, and the sudden flood of sorrow would pour out of them as a melody of untold anguish. Few of these wemon could withstand the onslaught woe. Many were driven mad, becoming rambling, disheveled outcasts who lived on the fringes of their communities. The most severely afflicted could not pass into the next life; the grim pain still flowing through them bound their spirits between the worlds of the living and the dead, and they became banshees.

\columnbreak

<br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br><br>

These tortured ghosts can unleash a scream that carries every ounce of the heartache they absorbed in life, an accursed cry capable of killing those who hear it. Their appearance is equally dreadful. Their visages, twisted by the overwhelming emotions that still plague them, freeze onlookers in terror, locking their limbs rigid as they stand transfixed by the gruesome sight.

Despite their lethal power and hideous presence, most banshees are not malevolent. Many remain tied to a bloodline or royal house descended from those they served in life. They appear to members of these lineages to foretell impending tragedy or doom. In some cases, the living may even call upon their spectral guardian to turn its baneful presence against those who would harm the family.

Other banshees wander the countryside, drawn to communities smothered by loss. In the dead of night, they gather on the outskirts of afflicted settlements and begin their sorrowful keening, drawing the grief of the living into themselves like rivers flowing into the sea.

The most dangerous are those wholly overcome by the forlorn emotions within them. These spirits cry out in desperation, clawing at the living with withering, vaporous hands in a frantic search for relief from their unending torment.

Though the first of their kind are said to have arisen in ancient tribal lands, it is said that others have become banshees upon their deaths over the ages. Bardic women who unknowingly continue the old rites, turning personal suffering into bittersweet songs that entrance and comfort others, may find that when they are finally laid to rest, their song does not end. Instead, they are pulled back onto the stage once more.

### Banshee Tactics
Banshees use Wail as soon as they can affect three or more creatures with it. Afterward, they prioritize attacking targets that have either averted their eyes or been paralyzed.

### Tomb Banshees

There are women skilled in the arcane arts who use their talents to indulge their own iniquitous desires, weaving melodies laced with charm and wicked magic to manipulate those around them without remorse. When such vile enchantresses finally die, their stained souls are barred from the heavenly realms, and only worlds of untold torment will have them. Rather than face such punishment, their spirits cling to the mortal world and become tomb banshees.

These creatures exceed ordinary banshees in power. Their very touch robs the vigor of any living being their ghastly hands pass through, leaving the victim defiled. Their wails are even more potent, for their sorrow is born not of mourning but of selfish spite: rage at the power they can no longer wield and the pleasures they can no longer glut themselves on. Even seasoned witch hunters have fallen to the unholy tantrums of these grave hags.

##### Banshees Art Credit
* Morkrut Banshee - Jodie Muir, Wizards of the Coast
* Morkrut Banshee - Fajareka Setiawan, Wizards of the Coast

\columnbreak

Drawn by their malevolence, many necromancers bind these spirits of devastation to their will with powerful magic, offering them the chance to loose their animosity upon the living. Some tomb banshees even serve as seers, foretelling tragedies from which their dark masters may profit. Yet they make bitter and disloyal advisors. It is said that a tomb banshee’s first prediction is often its own master’s death, and that the treacherous spirit may work in secret to bring that prophecy to fruition, for misery is the only comfort left to such a heartless beast.

#### Tomb Banshee Tactics
Tomb Banshees focus on targets with low hit points, applying Despair until those targets have disadvantage on all rolls (including against the next Wail).

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Dazed 
> A dazed creature can’t take reactions and on its turn, it must choose whether it gets a move, an action, or a bonus action; it gets only one of the three.

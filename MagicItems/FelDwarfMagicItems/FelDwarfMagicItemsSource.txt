## Fel Dwarf Magic Items
Just as their uncorrupted kin, fel dwarves are master craftsmen. However, their works are twisted and cruel creations that reflect the darkness in their hearts. Most are created through profane means, incorporating dark fiendish rituals and blood sacrifice of enslaved wretches or even other unfortunate fel dwarves. The demon smiths of the fel dwarves sell many of their creations for hefty sums, and so they are often wielded by other unscrupulous warlords and mages to wicked ends. However, the demon smiths guard their grandest creations jealously, only bringing them to bear in the direst of situations. 

#### Ashen Hammer
*Weapon (Warhammer), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 12,000 gp*

*This weapon is inscribed with countless hateful hymns, its onyx surface covered in verses proclaiming desolation and the downfall of any who oppose its bearer. Infernal runes grant the hammer the power to shatter and sear the bones of any creature it strikes. Even those with great resilience to flame and heat struggle to withstand the weapon's torrid wrath.*

Whenever you hit a target with this weapon, you can apply one of the following effects:

* The target loses any resistance to Fire damage until the end of your next turn, and immunity to Fire damage is treated as resistance instead.
* The target takes an additional 11 (2d10) Fire damage.

#### Blade of Malice
*Weapon (Scimitar), Rare (Requires Attunement), 2,000 gp*

*Said to have been excavated from a barrow buried beneath the ashen soil of a volcano by a band of fel goblin mercenaries, this blade has passed from one grasping owner to another for centuries. Every bearer has met an untimely end at the hands of a covetous rival. The sword fills its wielder with boundless violence, granting uncanny insight into where its strikes will inflict the greatest harm.*

Whenever you make an attack with advantage against a creature with this weapon, the creature gains one level of Vulnerability to you, up to a maximum of 10.

For each level of Vulnerability a creature has to you, the critical hit threshold for your attacks made against that target with this weapon is lowered by 1. A creature loses all levels of Vulnerability if you end your turn without attacking that creature or you score a critical hit on the creature.

#### Blasting Charge
*Bomb, Rare (Requires Attunement), 150 gp*

*Issued to the elite warriors of the fel dwarf empire, these explosive devices possess enough force to grievously wound or outright kill even foes protected by heavy plate armor.*

You can use your action to throw the bomb at a point you can see within 30 feet. Each creature within a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw.

*Failure:* The creature takes 10 (3d6) Fire damage and 10 (3d6) Piercing damage.
*Success:* The creature takes half as much damage.

When you attempt to throw the bomb, creatures within reach can use their reaction to make an opportunity attack against you, as though you had moved out of their reach. If a creature hits you, you drop the bomb, and it detonates centered on you.

#### Chalice of Chaos
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Druid, Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard), 3,000 gp*

*This brass chalice is filled with corrupted ichor, ash, demonsmith blood, and living shadow. When the ruby-inlaid goblet is raised, it can disrupt the winds of magic, causing spells to unravel before they are completed. Yet even its bearer can never be certain what consequences will follow the use of such a cursed relic.*

While you are holding this item, you can use it as a spellcasting focus.

Whenever a creature within 60 feet of you casts a spell using a Verbal, Somatic, or Material component, While holding the chalice, you can use your reaction to attempt to interrupt it. The creature makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, one of the following effects happens at random.

\columnbreak

##### Chalice Effect
| d6 | Ability | Effect |
|:---:|:-----------|:-----------|
| 1 | **Absorb Energy** | The spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or Reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot or daily use, it is wasted. You roll a number of d6s equal to the spell's level and gain temporary hit points equal to the total rolled. |
| 2 | **Disruption** | The spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or Reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot or daily use, the slot isn't expended. |
| 3 | **Destructive Feedback** | The spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or Reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot or daily use, it is wasted. You roll a number of d6s equal to the spell's level and take Force damage equal to the total rolled. This damage can't be reduced in any way. |
| 4 | **Reflective Strike** | The spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or Reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot or daily use, the slot isn't expended. You can then immediately make a ranged spell attack against the target, range 60 feet, using your spellcasting modifier to hit. On a hit, you roll a number of d6s equal to the spell's level, and the target takes Force damage equal to the total rolled. |

The Chalice of Chaos can't be used this way again until the next dawn. You can use a Magic Action to expend a 3rd-level spell slot to regain the use early.



#### Dark Mace
*Weapon (Mace), Rare (Requires Attunement), 3,300 gp*

*This ebon stone weapon's origins have been lost to history, though it has been wielded by a multitude of fel dwarf champions throughout the ages to devastating effect. The artifact chills both body and soul to behold. When swung, it moves with such speed that it becomes little more than a blurred shadow, draining the warmth from the air around it and leaving only cold corpses in its wake.*

Whenever you hit a creature with this weapon that has 50 or fewer current hit points, it takes additional 2d8 Cold damage. Additionally, you can use your action to make a melee attack with this weapon against any number of creatures within 5 feet of you, with a separate attack roll for each target.

#### Graven Star
*Weapon (Morningstar), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 10,000 gp*

*This iron rod once served as the emblem of the lords of the Black Spire, an infernal outpost on the edge of the fel dwarfs' domain. Each cruel lord who claimed the Star engraved three names upon its shaft. Even the mightiest giant crumples beneath its relentless blows, for no man nor beast, regardless of their resilience, can withstand the judgment of the Black Spire's lords and live to speak of it.*

When you hit a creature with this weapon, its damage die changes to match the target creature's largest hit die for that attack, and it deals additional damage equal to the creature's Constitution modifier.
 

 
#### Hellshard Amulet
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 6,000 gp*

*Crafted by Drazhul the Ruinous himself, this crimson gemstone was formed by transmuting his own overwhelming hatred into solid form. It emits a faint glow from its corrupted core. The wearer may draw upon the amulet's depths, allowing themselves to be consumed by its stubborn malevolence and unleashing terrible vengeance upon any who dare oppose them.*

You can use your bonus action to become Resolute* until the start of your next turn.

While Resolute, you can cast *Hellish Rebuke*; the spell save DC is 19.

**New condition described below*

#### Infernal Round
*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Uncommon, 60 gp*

*Designed to devastate tightly packed formations, these munitions contain a potent explosive core. Upon impact, they erupt in a violent burst of infernal flame, engulfing nearby foes in the ensuing blast.*

When you hit or miss a target with this magic ammunition, the target and each creature within 10 feet of it must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 Fire damage. A creature hit by the attack has disadvantage on the saving throw. Once this ammunition hits a target, it is no longer magical.

<br>
 
> ##### New Condition: Resolute
> The creature stubbornly digs in to weather any assault. It experiences the following effects:
>  * Attack rolls made against the creature have disadvantage.
>  * The creature can't take its move action on its turn.
> * The creature makes Strength checks and all saving throws with advantage, except Dexterity saving throws, which it makes with disadvantage.
> 
> The condition ends if an effect moves the creature against its will, knocks it prone, or the creature becomes charmed, frightened, or incapacitated. A creature that is prone, charmed, frightened, or incapacitated can't become Resolute.

#### Intractable Armor
*Armor (Plate), Very Rare, 25,000 gp*

*In ages past, the great overseer Zhirgir ruled a domain so vast that even the clergy of the demonsmiths viewed him as a threat. To shield himself from their wrath, he forged a suit of armor capable of withstanding any foe the dark hierarchy could send against him. And so it did. It was said that no weapon could pierce Zhirgir's enchanted shell, nor could any warrior force him to falter. Yet even such protection could not defy fate. During a campaign against a roaming band of orcs, his army was ambushed upon a tidal flat. Forced backward by the relentless assault, Zhirgir was driven into a powerful riptide. The armor that had preserved his life for decades became his doom, dragging him beneath the waves. Decades later, the suit was dredged from the ocean floor, entirely unscathed.*

This armor has a Strength requirement of 18.

While wearing this armor, you gain a +3 bonus to your AC. Additionally, while you aren't Prone, you gain a +3 bonus to saving throws and checks made against being Dazed*, Stunned, Paralyzed, Incapacitated, or moved against your will. If you fail a saving throw made this way, you are knocked Prone, and until the end of your next turn, you must spend all your movement to stand up.

**New condition described above*

#### Mask of the Hellforge
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement), 4,000 gp*

*Forged to resemble the visage of a greater demon, this brass visor has witnessed thousands of sacrifices performed within the demonsmiths' most sacred Hellforges. The suffering of those consumed by infernal fire has forever tainted the mask. Those who practice pyromancy may draw upon the cursed memories trapped within it, imbuing their flames with hellish corruption.*

Whenever you cast a spell that deals only Fire damage and allows a creature to make a saving throw to take half damage, you can change the type of saving throw the spell forces to a Wisdom saving throw. Additionally, if a creature without Fire resistance or immunity fails the saving throw against the spell, it is Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

#### Molten Round
*Weapon (Any Ammunition), Uncommon, 60 gp*

*Developed to bring down massive and heavily armored warbeasts, these rounds release molten lead after embedding themselves within their target, slowly cooking the victim from the inside unless the projectile is removed swiftly and with precision.*

When you hit a creature with this magic ammunition, the ammunition becomes embedded in the target for 1 minute. While embedded, the creature can't regain hit points, and at the end of each of its turns, it takes 2d6 Fire damage. The target or a creature within 5 feet of it can use an action to make a DC 14 Wisdom (Medicine) check to remove the ammunition. The target has disadvantage on this check if attempting to remove the ammunition from itself. On a success, the ammunition is removed; on a failure, the ammunition is still removed, but the creature takes an additional 3d6 Fire damage. Once this ammunition hits a target, it is no longer magical.

#### Naphtha Bomb
*Bomb, Uncommon (Requires Attunement), 60 gp*

*A volatile mixture of vile chemicals and boiling demon blood is sealed within these devilish bombs. When hurled into enemy ranks, the contents erupt upon impact, showering the battlefield in blazing liquid and engulfing the unfortunate in searing flames.*

You can use your action to throw the bomb at a point you can see within 30 feet. Each creature within a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw.

*Failure:* The creature takes 3d6 Fire damage and starts Burning*.
*Success:* The creature takes half as much damage.

When you attempt to throw the bomb, creatures within reach can use their reaction to make an opportunity attack against you, as though you had moved out of their reach. If a creature hits you, you drop the bomb, and it detonates centered on you.

**New condition described below*

#### Stone Mantle
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement), 5,000 gp*

*Fashioned from the flayed, ashen skin of Ashragoth Ironfist's many apprentices who failed to earn his approval, this grisly cloak carries the power of the demonsmiths along with their terrible curse.*

While wearing this mantle, whenever your concentration would be broken as a result of taking damage, you gain one level of Petrification instead. For each level of Petrification, your speed is reduced by 5 feet, and you gain a +1 bonus to Constitution saving throws and a -1 penalty to Dexterity saving throws. If you accumulate four or more levels of Petrification, you become Petrified, ignoring immunity to the condition. Any effect that removes the Petrified condition removes one level of Petrification instead.

<br>

> ##### New Condition: Burning
> The creature has been set ablaze by something that continues to damage it each round until it is extinguished. The default source described here is fire, but other sources could deal different types of damage, such as acid. 
> * **Default Damage:** A burning creature takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of its turns unless the source that inflicted the condition states otherwise.
> * **Ending the Condition:** As an action, the burning creature can extinguish the fire by dropping prone and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. For example, another creature could use an action to put out the flames with their cloak or by pouring water on them.
> * **Multiple Sources of Burning:** If a burning creature would gain this condition again from another source, the new burning effect only replaces the old if it deals more damage each round. Otherwise, it is ignored.

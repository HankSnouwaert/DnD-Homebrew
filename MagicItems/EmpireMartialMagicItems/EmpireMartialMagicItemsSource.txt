## Magic Armaments of The Empire

The armories of mighty human empires often hold magical implements of war reserved for lords and heroes of great standing. Some are ancient artifacts from the empire's founding; others are newly forged armaments imbued with eldritch enchantments or divine blessings. Adventurers who perform great service may occasionally earn such items as rewards, or pry them from enemies who looted them from the Empire's armories and fallen heroes.

#### Amber Bow
*Weapon (Longbow), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 7,000 gp*

*Carved from the oak of the most ancient forests, this bow was crafted by order of the Amber Druids to slay the unnatural behemoths plaguing their land. It conjures a luminous arrow that glows a faint honey-gold, and when loosed, that golden arrow can fell even the mightiest monstrosities.*

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls made with this weapon.

When you take the Attack action on your turn while holding this weapon, you can forgo all your attacks to cast *Amber Spear** (save DC  10 + your Dexterity modifier + the number of attacks forgone). Increase the spell's effective level by 1 for each attack forgone. If only one creature makes a saving throw against the spell and it is a Beast, Monstrosity, Giant, Dragon, or Fey that is Huge or larger, the damage is doubled.

The bow can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

#### Halberd of Power
*Weapon (Halberd), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 8,000 gp*

*This polearm was forged from the iron of a meteorite that fell during the celebration of the Empire's founding. Its wielders are said to draw might from the heavenly metal, striking with otherworldly strength.*

While holding this weapon, you know the Overwhelming Attack* and Pushing Attack maneuvers from the Battle Master subclass of the Fighter class. The saving throw DC equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength modifier.


The weapon has five Superiority Dice, each a d10. These dice are added to any Superiority Dice you have from another source while you are holding the weapon, and they are used to fuel your maneuvers. A Superiority Die is expended when you use it.

The weapon regains 1d4 + 1 expended Superiority Dice daily at dawn.

While holding the Halberd, you gain a +1 bonus to the saving throw DCs of your maneuvers. 

**New maneuver described at end of document*

#### Hammer of War
*Weapon (Warhammer), Legendary (Requires Attunement), 50,000 gp*

*Said to be one of the most powerful weapons ever forged, gifted to the mightiest hero of the age. In the hands of a true champion, there are no challenges that cannot be overcome.*

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, which deals Force damage instead of Bludgeoning damage. It deals an extra 2d8 Force damage to any target it hits, or 2d10 if you use both hands to wield it. When you miss with this weapon, you hit instead, except you deal only half damage and the creature suffers no effects other than damage from the attack.

When you score a critical hit with this weapon, you can roll three additional weapon damage dice when determining the extra damage for the critical hit.

#### Helm of the Indomitable
*Helmet, Rare (Requires Attunement), 5,000 gp*

*Decorated with an ornate golden griffon that sits atop the cap with its left talon outstretched. Commanders who don the chapeau have their tenacity magnified to the point where they shrug off death-inducing blows.*

You gain one use of the Indomitable feature from the Fighter class, even if you don't have the feature. You regain this expended use when you finish a Long Rest.

When you use your Indomitable feature or the Shared Resilience feature of the Banneret subclass of the Fighter class, if the effect the creature is making the saving throw against allows a creature to take only half damage on a successful save, the creature instead takes no damage on a successful save and only half damage on a failed save. Additionally, if damage from the effect would reduce a creature to 0 hit points, it drops to 1 hit point instead.

#### Rune Fang
*Weapon (Longsword), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 24,000 gp*

*It is said that only twelve of these blades were ever made by one of the greatest dwarf artificers to ever live, and given as gifts to a band of chiefs who united their tribes in solidarity with the mighty dwarven strongholds to push back the advancing hordes that sought to bring down the bastions of these brave folk. Since that time, a number of those tribes have grown into dukedoms and provinces, their lords wielding the etched weapons as a sign of office. Others have been lost as the leaders and their lands were annihilated by invading armies. Such blades are priceless, for no other soul has been able to replicate such masterpieces. The blades are as light as air and able to cut through the thickest steel plate and hardest dragon hide as if they were little more than sheets of paper.*

When you miss with this weapon, you hit instead, except you deal only half damage and the creature suffers no effects other than damage from the attack.


**New maneuver described at end of document*

#### Shield of Protection
*Armor (Shield), Rare (Requires Attunement), 1,500 gp*

*Commissioned by a timorous count for his personal guardian, the shield is able to provide even greater protection to those next to it than to the wielder.*

Whenever you use the Protection Fighting Style to protect a creature while holding this shield, you can add 3 to the target's AC until the end of the current turn.

#### Silver Horn
*Wondrous Item (Instrument), Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Fighter), 1,000 gp*

*Used by commanders to turn the tide of countless battles. When blown, the conviction of the warrior playing the instrument becomes manifest as a roar unlike any other. Allies who hear it, even those who are maimed and beaten, rise to fight with renewed vigor.*

When you use your Second Wind feature, you can blow this horn. If you do, you roll 2d10 instead of 1d10, and you can choose any number of Bloodied creatures within 60 feet of you (which can include you). You can divide the Hit Points you would gain from this feature among them. This feature can't restore a creature to more than half its Hit Point maximum.

#### Spear of the Fates
*Weapon (Spear), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), 1,000 gp*

*Said to have been fashioned by a mad seer of the fanatical Inquisition whose mind was eroded by thousands of visions of crimes and wrongdoings yet to take place, stretching even to the end of time, which were imprinted onto the weapon. The spear holds that manic foresight within its tip. When pointed at a soul, the spear will hum and shake, recognizing the sins yet to take place and seeking to prevent such events from unfolding.*

At the start of your turn, you can choose to predict the fate of up to three creatures you are aware of within 60 feet of you. When you predict a creature's fate, roll a d6. On a roll of 5 or 6, the creature is marked by the weapon until you next use this property to mark another creature. While marked, whenever you hit the creature with the weapon, it takes an additional 1d8 Piercing damage. If the creature's hit points are 20 or fewer after taking this damage, it is reduced to 0 hit points.

Once you mark a creature, you can't mark that creature again for the next 24 hours. A creature immune to Divination spells can't have its fate predicted.

#### Laurels of Victory
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 12,000 gp*

*These enchanted medals of honor are woven with magic by Imperial Mages and are bestowed by the Emperor upon heroes who have proven their devotion to their lord through their valor. Such a symbol amplifies the presence of the warrior who wears it. Adversaries who look upon such a figure are filled with doubt and are likely to cower and flee should the odds of their victory even be slightly against them.*

You learn the Commanding Presence and Menacing Attack maneuvers from the Battle Master subclass of the Fighter class. The saving throw DC equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice).

The weapon has five, each a d10. These dice are added to any Superiority Dice you have from another source, and they are used to fuel your maneuvers. A Superiority Die is expended when you use it.

The weapon regains 1d4 + 1 expended Superiority Dice daily at dawn.

Additionally, a creature has disadvantage on saving throws against your Battle Master maneuvers if you have hit it at least twice this turn with a weapon attack.

#### Marvellous Magnifier
*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 12,000 gp*

*Devised by a fastidious tinker, when one looks through this small monocle and focuses, they are able to see with clarity the most minuscule detail. It was said that no flaw was able to avoid the creator's inspection while wearing such a glass, nor could any target escape his rifle.*

You learn the Survey* and Precision Attack maneuvers from the Battle Master subclass of the Fighter class.

Once per turn, when you use a maneuver, you can roll 1d10 and use the number rolled instead of expending a Superiority Die. You can use this feature only if you haven't moved during this turn, and after you use it, your Speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.

**New maneuver described at end of document*

#### Pearl Dagger
*Weapon (Dagger), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 8,000 gp*

*Such gleaming and flawless blades are light as a feather. The wielder is able to invoke a fleetness that allows them to strike with deadly speed.*

While you are holding this weapon, you know the Feinting Attack and Lunging Attack maneuvers from the Battle Master subclass of the Fighter class. The saving throw DC equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Dexterity modifier.

The weapon has five Superiority Dice, each a d10. These dice are added to any Superiority Dice you have from another source while you are holding the weapon, and they are used to fuel your maneuvers. A Superiority Die is expended when you use it.

The weapon regains 1d4 + 1 expended Superiority Dice daily at dawn.

Finally, when you roll a Superiority Die while holding this weapon, you can reroll it, but you must use the new roll.

\columnbreak

#### Repelling Armor
*Armor (Half Plate), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 6,500 gp*

*This gilded suit of armor was fashioned as thanks to the empires of man after the great alliance against the forces of Chaos. A potent barrier of translucent force eddies around the wearer, halting all manner of weapons that would strike them.*

Whenever a creature attempts to make a melee attack against you for the first time on a turn, the attacker must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it can't attack you with melee attacks until the end of the turn, and it must choose a new target for its attack or the attack is wasted. On a successful save, it can attack you on this turn.

#### Twice-Blessed Armor
*Armor (Plate), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), 7,000 gp*

*Etched with sacred prayers by two great Grand Theogonists. Those in the burnished shell who are bestowed with its boons will share such mystic bounties with their comrades.*

When another allied creature you can see within 30 feet of you casts a spell of 4th level or lower, such as *Bless* or *Fly*, you can allow the creature to increase the spell's effective level by 1. If it does, you must be one of the spell's targets, or the spell dissipates with no effect and the action, Bonus Action, or Reaction used to cast it is wasted.

### New Battle Master Maneuvers

#### Overwhelming Attack
When you hit a creature with an attack roll using a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you can expend one Superiority Die to attempt to overwhelm the target. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take damage equal to three times the number rolled on the Superiority Die. The damage is the same type dealt by the original attack.

#### Survey
As a Bonus Action, you can expend one Superiority Die to take the Search action. Roll the die and add the number rolled to the ability check.

### New Spell
#### Amber Spear

*3rd-level evocation*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:**  V S M (A Piece of Amber)
- **Duration:**  Instantaneous
- **Classes:**   Druid, Ranger
___

You conjure a large amber hunting spear and launch it in a straight line at a point you can see within range. Upon reaching its destination or striking a solid surface, the spear shatters, producing an explosion within a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. The spear can pass through spaces occupied by creatures and objects of large size or smaller. Each creature the spear moves through, as well as those within the explosion's radius, must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d6 piercing damage; on a successful one, the damage is halved.

***At Higher Levels.***  When casting this spell using a 4th-level spell slot or higher, the range increases by 20 feet and the damage by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

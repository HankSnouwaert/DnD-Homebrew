## Desert Spells
Spellcasters living in arid environments often wield magic that reflects the harsh nature of their home. They may be wizards drawing inspiration from their surroundings, sorcerers who've been shaped by them, or druids drawing direct power from the desert. The following are new and revised versions of spells that thematically fit the desert biome. Most either summon and manipulate sand or rapidly subject their victims to the withering effects of the desert. 

You can provide these options to your players or equip your desert-themed NPC spellcasters with them.

#### Ash Storm
*5th-level Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of soot)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a wave of scalding ash in a 60-foot-long, 15-foot-wide line originating from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d10 Fire damage, is pushed 15 feet away from you, and is Blinded until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no other effects.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 5th.

#### Decrepify
*4th-level Necromancy (Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of shriveled weeds)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You attempt to drain the vitality of a creature within range, leaving it feeble and vulnerable. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have its Speed halved and its Strength and Dexterity scores reduced to 1. At the end of each of its turns, the affected creature can make another Constitution saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this spell using a 6th-level spell slot, the duration becomes 24 hours and the affected creature can only attempt a new saving throw after completing a Short Rest. If you cast it using an 8th-level spell slot, the spell lasts until dispelled and no longer requires Concentration; the affected creature can only attempt a new saving throw after completing a Long Rest.

#### Dust Devil (Revised)
*2nd-level Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dust)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A 5-foot-diameter sphere of whirling dust appears in an unoccupied space of your choice within range and lasts for the duration. The sphere's space is Heavily Obscured and Difficult Terrain, and creatures can move through it.

When the sphere first appears and as a Magic action on your following turns, you can move the sphere up to 30 feet in a straight line. If the sphere moves into a creature's space, that creature must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 1d10 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed 10 feet away. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

If the sphere moves over sand, dust, loose dirt, or small gravel, it sucks up the material and forms a 10-foot-radius cloud of debris around itself that lasts until the start of your next turn. The cloud Heavily Obscures its area.

A creature that ends its turn within 5 feet of the sphere must also make the saving throw described above.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.

#### Earthen Grip (Revised)
*1st-level Transmutation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of clay)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You choose an unoccupied 5-foot square on the ground that you can see within range. A hand made from compacted soil rises from that space. The hand is a Medium object with 20 hit points, AC equal to your spell save DC, and immunity to Poison and Psychic damage.

When the hand appears, it reaches for one creature you can see within 5 feet of it. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw or take 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and is Restrained by the hand for the duration.

As a Magic action, you can cause the hand to crush the Restrained target, who must make a Strength saving throw. The target takes 4d8 Bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

\columnbreak





To break free, the Restrained target can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC. On a success, the target is no longer Restrained by the hand.

As a Magic action, you can cause the hand to move up to 20 feet along the ground and reach for another creature as described above. The hand releases any Restrained target when it moves.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage of both effects increases by 1d8 and the hand gains an additional 10 hit points for each slot level above 1st.


#### Grasp of Dust
*4th-level Necromancy (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Your touch causes flesh to wither and crumble. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier Necrotic damage. If the target has hit points equal to 5 times the spell slot's level or fewer after taking this damage, it is reduced to 0 hit points and dies. A creature that dies this way is reduced to dust.

You can make this attack again on each of your turns as a Magic action.

#### Horrid Wilting (Revised)
*8th-level Necromancy (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of sponge)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You draw the moisture from every creature in a 30-foot cube centered on a point you choose within range. Each creature in that area must make a Constitution saving throw. Constructs and Undead aren't affected, and Plants and Water Elementals make this saving throw with Disadvantage.

On a failed save, a creature takes 12d8 Necrotic damage and gains one level of Fatigue*. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

Nonmagical plants in the area that aren't creatures, such as trees and shrubs, wither and die instantly.

#### Sand Blast
*1st-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dust)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You let loose a blast of abrading dust toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 3d4 Slashing damage and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the <Br> target is also Blinded until the end of your next turn.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage <br> increases by 2d4 for each slot level above 1st.

#### Sickening Radiance (Revised)
*4th-level Transmutation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 100 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A dim, sickening light spreads within a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. The light spreads around corners and lasts until the spell ends.

When a creature moves into the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 4d10 Radiant damage, gain one level of Fatigue*, and emit dim light in a 5-foot radius. This light prevents the creature from benefiting from the Invisible condition. The light and any Fatigue* levels caused by this spell end when the spell ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 4th.


#### Wall of Sand (Revised)
*3rd-level Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a handful of sand)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a wall of swirling sand on the ground at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 10 feet thick, and it vanishes when the spell ends. It blocks line of sight but not movement. A creature is Blinded while in the wall's space and must spend 3 feet of movement for every 1 foot it moves there.

When a creature starts its turn in the wall or moves through the wall's space for the first time on a turn, it must make a Constitution saving throw or be Blinded until the end of its next turn.

As a Magic action on your turn, you can cause the wall to move up to 30 feet. After it moves, it disperses and the spell ends. Each creature the wall moves through must make the saving throw described above.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The length of the wall increases by 10 feet for each slot level above 3rd.

> ##### Alternative Condition: Fatigue
> The creature experiences debilitating weariness that can accumulate in levels. Each time a creature gains the Fatigue condition, its Fatigue Level increases by 1. If the level reaches 6, the creature dies. Fatigue Levels have the following effects on a creature:
> * **d20 Rolls:** When a creature makes a d20 Test, subtract its Fatigue Level from the d20 roll.
> * **Spell Save DCs:** Subtract a creature's Fatigue Level from the Spell Save DC of any spell it casts.
> * **Speed** A creature's speed is reduced by a number of feet equal to 5 x its Fatigue level. This can't lower its speed below 5 feet.
> 
> **Ending the Condition:** Completing a Long Rest reduces a creature's Fatigue Level by 1.
> 
> **Fatigue vs. Exhaustion:** Fatigue is an alternative mechanic to the Exhaustion condition, and any ability that removes levels of Exhaustion or grants immunity to it does the same for Fatigue.

#### Withering Heat
*7th-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 ft
- **Components:** V, S, M (A bit of baked clay)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You cause a creature to be gripped by blistering heat. Choose one creature within range. Until the spell ends, at the end of each of your turns, if the target is still within 90 feet of you, it must make a Constitution saving throw.

On a failed save, the target takes 7d10 Fire damage and gains one level of Fatigue*. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and does not gain Fatigue.

A creature that is immune to Fire damage cannot gain Fatigue from this spell.

Any levels of Fatigue caused by this spell are removed when the spell ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***  The damage increases by 2d10 for every slot level above 7.

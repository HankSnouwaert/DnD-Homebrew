## Lore of Dark Magic
This form of magic bends all winds of magic to the will of the user through brute force to create tainted spells that defy natural order and corrupt the soul. Rather than harmonizing each wind of magic, dark magic smashes them together, creating vicious spells that brutalize their victims and blight reality with their very existence. These spells can come in many forms, but all inflict pain and misery upon their foes, whether it be in the form of blackened flame, chilling despair, or malevolent lightning. 

This form of magic is naturally shunned, if not forbidden, in most societies, and those wielding it are usually met with fear and suspicion, if not outright persecution. But, for most practitioners of dark magic, the true risk is to their very souls, for such malignant magic recognizes no master and will quickly consume any wielder too weak-willed to control it. All too often, those wielding dark magic with the best intentions will find those intentions corrupted by the power they wield as they descend down the path of madness and damnation.



#### Dark Power
*1st-level Evocation (Warlock)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bone from a humanoid)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You call upon power from other worlds at great personal cost. When you cast this spell, you roll up to two d6s, taking Force damage equal to the total rolled. This damage can’t be reduced or prevented in any way.

You then create a number of rays equal to the number of d6s rolled and hurl them at targets within range. You can direct the rays at one creature or multiple creatures. Make a ranged spell attack for each ray. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Force damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You roll one additional d6 and create one additional ray for each spell slot level above 1st.

#### Devouring Despair
*3rd-level Enchantment (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a shard of obsidian)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a surge of numbing darkness. Choose up to three creatures you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against each target. You have advantage on the attack roll if the target is in dim light or darkness.

On a hit, the target takes 4d6 Cold damage and must make a Wisdom saving throw. The target has disadvantage on this saving throw if it has no allies within 5 feet of it. On a failed save, the target takes an additional 4d6 Psychic damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Cold and Psychic damage each increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3rd.

#### Discordant Strike
*6th-level Enchantment (Sorcerer, Warlock)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 15 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You strike with a spear of crimson, disruptive electricity. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 5d6 Psychic damage and 5d6 Lightning damage. Until the end of your next turn, the creature can’t concentrate, regain hit points, or cast spells.

A creature immune to either Lightning or Psychic damage ignores these additional effects. On a miss, the electricity grazes the target, dealing half of the initial damage and no other effects.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Psychic and Thunder damage each increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 6th.

#### Doom Bolt
*5th-level Evocation (Warlock)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You hurl a bolt of black fire toward a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 5d8 Fire damage and 5d8 Necrotic damage.

\columnbreak

#### Enervation
*5th-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

When you cast this spell, and as an Action on each of your turns for the duration, you can make a ranged spell attack against one creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 4d10 Necrotic damage, and a crackling arc of dark energy forms between you and the target.

On each of your turns for the duration, you can use an Action to automatically deal 4d10 Necrotic damage to a creature currently linked to you by the arc. The arc ends for a target if it is ever outside the spell’s range or gains total cover from you. The spell also ends if you use your Action to do anything other than deal damage with this spell.

Whenever this spell deals damage to a creature, you regain hit points equal to half the Necrotic damage dealt.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage dealt by each instance of this spell increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 5th.

#### Razorgale
*4th-level Evocation (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a sliver of glass)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You conjure four spectral swords that orbit you for the spell’s duration. When you cast the spell, and as an Action on each of your turns thereafter, you can expend one sword to make either a melee spell attack against a creature within 5 feet of you or a ranged spell attack against a creature within 90 feet. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 Slashing damage.

Any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 1d8 Slashing damage for each sword currently orbiting you. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.***  the number of swords created increases by 1 for each slot level above 4th.


#### Reverberating Chill
*2nd-level Evocation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Make a melee spell attack against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Cold damage and 2d8 Thunder damage. Hit or miss, the target becomes sheathed in chilling energy until the start of your next turn. While sheathed in this energy, the target has disadvantage on ranged attack rolls.

If the target willingly moves 5 feet or more before then, it immediately takes 1d8 Cold damage and 1d8 Thunder damage, and the spell ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Cold and Thunder damage from each effect increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2nd.

\columnbreak

#### Void Horror
*9th-level Evocation (Warlock)*
___
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of igneous rock)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You open a gateway to the dark between worlds. A 20-foot-radius sphere of absolute blackness and bitter cold appears at a point you can see within range and lasts for the duration. No light, magical or otherwise, can illuminate the area, and creatures fully within the sphere are Blinded. The sphere emits faint whispers and slurping sounds audible up to 30 feet away.

As an Action on your turn, you can move the sphere up to 30 feet.

**Entering the Sphere.** The area within the sphere is difficult terrain. Any creature that starts its turn in the area takes 5d8 Cold damage, and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of the turn. Any creature that ends its turn in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be banished to a realm of utter darkness for the duration. While banished, the creature is Incapacitated.

**Banished Creatures.** At the end of each of its turns, A banished creature takes 5d8 Cold damage and 5d8 Psychic damage, and then must make a Charisma saving throw. On a success, the spell ends for that creature. When the spell ends for a creature, it reappears at the center of the sphere or the nearest unoccupied space. If a creature dies while banished, its body remains behind, but any magic items it carried reappear at the sphere’s center.

**Tentacle Attacks.** Until the spell ends, you can use a Bonus Action on each of your turns to cause three shadowy tentacles to lash out from the sphere’s center at targets within 100 feet of it. You can target one creature or multiple creatures. Make a ranged spell attack for each tentacle. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 Acid damage and is pulled 20 feet toward the sphere’s center.
